# Sentences: Episode 7 | Short lines from the seventh episode
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Nakama ni natte kure, Nami. | 仲間になってくれ、ナミ。 | Join my crew, Nami.
nakama | 仲間 | crewmate, comrade
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
natte kure | なってくれ | become (for me) — a blunt request
~Nami | ナミ | Nami (name)

> Yakusoku doori omae wo buttobashi ni kita zo. | 約束どおりお前をぶっ飛ばしに来たぞ。 | As promised, I've come to send you flying.
yakusoku doori | 約束どおり | as promised
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~wo | を | (marks the object)
buttobashi ni | ぶっ飛ばしに | (in order) to send flying
kita | 来た | came
~zo | ぞ | (emphatic)

> Kaizu to takara ga te ni haireba sore de ii no. | 海図と宝が手に入ればそれでいいの。 | As long as I get the chart and the treasure, that's all I need.
kaizu | 海図 | sea chart
~to | と | and
takara | 宝 | treasure
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
te ni haireba | 手に入れば | if (I) get hold of
sore de ii | それでいい | that is enough
~no | の | (explaining, feminine)

> Nani shi ni kitan ja? | 何しに来たんじゃ。 | What did you come here for?
nani shi ni | 何しに | to do what
kita | 来た | came
~n ja | んじゃ | (an old man's "n da")

> Yosomono wa hikkonde ore. | よそ者は引っ込んでおれ。 | Outsiders stay out of this.
yosomono | よそ者 | outsider
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
hikkonde ore | 引っ込んでおれ | stay back (old-style command)

> Kore wa washi no tatakai ja zo. | これはわしの戦いじゃぞ。 | This is my fight.
kore | これ | this
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
washi | わし | I (old man's speech)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
tatakai | 戦い | fight, battle
~ja zo | じゃぞ | it is! (old man's "da zo")

> Washi no machi wa washi ga mamoru! | わしの町はわしが守る！ | I will protect my own town!
washi no | わしの | my (old man's speech)
machi | 町 | town
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
washi | わし | I (old man's speech)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
mamoru | 守る | to protect

> Hottoitara kono ossan machigainaku shinu ki da. | 放っといたらこのおっさん間違いなく死ぬ気だ。 | Leave him be, and this old man definitely means to die.
hottoitara | 放っといたら | if (we) leave him be
kono | この | this
ossan | おっさん | old man
machigainaku | 間違いなく | without a doubt
shinu ki | 死ぬ気 | intending to die
~da | だ | is

> Hoka ni mo houhou wa ikura demo aru deshou ga! | 他にも方法はいくらでもあるでしょうが！ | There are plenty of other ways, aren't there?!
hoka ni mo | 他にも | other, besides
houhou | 方法 | way, method
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
ikura demo | いくらでも | any number of
aru | ある | there are
~deshou ga | でしょうが | surely! (exasperated)

> Sore dake wa iccha dame na no yo. | それだけは言っちゃダメなのよ。 | That's the one thing you mustn't say.
sore dake wa | それだけは | that alone
iccha dame | 言っちゃダメ | mustn't say (casual "itte wa dame")
~na no yo | なのよ | (explaining, feminine)

> Saki ni ie yo na. | 先に言えよな。 | Tell me beforehand, will you.
saki ni | 先に | beforehand
ie | 言え | say it! (command form of "iu")
~yo na | よな | will you

> Teki ga hetta. | 敵が減った。 | The enemy's numbers have dropped.
teki | 敵 | enemy
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
hetta | 減った | decreased (past of "heru")

> Anta ittai nan na no yo! | あんた一体何なのよ！ | What on earth are you?!
anta | あんた | you (casual)
ittai | 一体 | (what) on earth
nan | 何 | what
~na no yo | なのよ | are (you)?! (feminine)

> Chanto setsumei shite yo! | ちゃんと説明してよ！ | Explain it properly!
chanto | ちゃんと | properly
setsumei shite | 説明して | explain (te-form request)
~yo | よ | (insisting)

> Sore ga nani ka tte kiiten no yo! | それが何かって聞いてんのよ！ | I'm asking you what that is!
sore ga | それが | that
nani ka | 何か | what (it is)
~tte | って | (quoting: "that…")
kiiten | 聞いてん | am asking (casual "kiite iru")
~no yo | のよ | (insisting, feminine)

> Teshita wo tate ni shita no ne. | 手下を盾にしたのね。 | You used your own men as a shield.
teshita | 手下 | henchmen
~wo | を | (marks the object)
tate | 盾 | shield
ni shita | にした | made into, used as
~no ne | のね | didn't you (feminine)
