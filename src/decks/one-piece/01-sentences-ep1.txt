# Sentences: Episode 1 | Short lines from the first episode — learn the words, then read the line
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Ore no zaihou ka? Hoshikerya kurete yaru. | 俺の財宝か？欲しけりゃくれてやる。 | My treasure? If you want it, you can have it.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
zaihou | 財宝 | treasure
~ka | か | (question: "…?")
hoshikerya | 欲しけりゃ | if you want it (casual "hoshikereba")
kurete yaru | くれてやる | I'll let you have it (talking down)

> Kono yo no subete wo soko ni oite kita. | この世の全てをそこに置いてきた。 | I left everything in this world in that place.
kono yo | この世 | this world
~no | の | 's, of
subete | 全て | everything
~wo | を | (marks the object)
soko | そこ | there, that place
~ni | に | in, at
oite kita | 置いてきた | left (it) behind ("oku" + "kuru", past)

> Yo wa masa ni dai-kaizoku jidai! | 世はまさに大海賊時代！ | The world is truly in the Great Pirate Era!
yo | 世 | the world, the age
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
masa ni | まさに | truly, exactly
dai-kaizoku jidai | 大海賊時代 | the Great Pirate Era

> Kaizokusen da! Tekishuu! | 海賊船だ！敵襲！ | A pirate ship! We're under attack!
kaizokusen | 海賊船 | pirate ship
~da | だ | is
tekishuu | 敵襲 | enemy attack

> Minasan, ochitsuite kudasai! | 皆さん、落ち着いてください！ | Everyone, please stay calm!
minasan | 皆さん | everyone (polite)
ochitsuite | 落ち着いて | calm down (te-form of "ochitsuku")
kudasai | ください | please (do)

> Kono umi de ichiban utsukushii mono wa dare dai? | この海で一番美しいものは誰だい？ | Who is the most beautiful of all on these seas?
kono | この | this
umi | 海 | sea
~de | で | in, on
ichiban | 一番 | the most
utsukushii | 美しい | beautiful
mono | もの | one, person
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
dare | 誰 | who
~dai | だい | (question, bossy tone)

> Boku, kouiu no nigate de… | 僕、こういうの苦手で… | I'm... not good at this kind of thing…
boku | 僕 | I (soft, boyish)
kouiu no | こういうの | this kind of thing
nigate | 苦手 | bad at, not fond of
~de | で | (trailing off: "and so…")

> Inochi made torou to wa iwanee yo. | 命まで取ろうとは言わねえよ。 | I'm not saying I'll take your lives.
inochi | 命 | life
made | まで | even, as far as
torou | 取ろう | (I'll) take ("toru", volitional)
~to wa | とは | (quoting: "that…")
iwanee | 言わねえ | don't say (rough "iwanai")
~yo | よ | (asserting)

> Koko wa daremo imasen ne. | ここは誰もいませんね。 | There's no one here, is there.
koko | ここ | here
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
daremo | 誰も | no one (with a negative)
imasen | いません | there isn't (polite negative of "iru")
~ne | ね | is there

> Zuibun ookina taru desu ne. | ずいぶん大きなたるですね。 | That's quite a big barrel.
zuibun | ずいぶん | quite, awfully
ookina | 大きな | big
taru | たる | barrel
~desu ne | ですね | is, isn't it (polite)

> Yoku neta naa! | よく寝たなあ！ | Ahh, I slept well!
yoku | よく | well
neta | 寝た | slept (past of "neru")
~naa | なあ | (satisfied sigh)

> Dare da, omaera? | 誰だ、お前ら？ | Who are you guys?
dare | 誰 | who
~da | だ | is
omaera | お前ら | you guys (rough)

> Hito no hanashi wo kike! | 人の話を聞け！ | Listen when people are talking!
hito | 人 | person, other people
~no | の | 's (possessive)
hanashi | 話 | talk, what someone says
~wo | を | (marks the object)
kike | 聞け | listen! (command form of "kiku")

> Ikinari nani sunda yo. | いきなり何すんだよ。 | What are you doing all of a sudden?
ikinari | いきなり | suddenly, out of nowhere
nani | 何 | what
sunda yo | すんだよ | are (you) doing?! (rough "suru n da yo")

> Nakama wo tsurete kaette kitara korosarechaimasu yo. | 仲間を連れて帰ってきたら殺されちゃいますよ。 | If they come back with their friends, you'll be killed!
nakama | 仲間 | friends, crewmates
~wo | を | (marks the object)
tsurete | 連れて | bringing along (te-form of "tsureru")
kaette kitara | 帰ってきたら | if (they) come back
korosarechaimasu | 殺されちゃいます | will end up being killed (passive + "-chau", polite)
~yo | よ | (warning)

> Sore ni shitemo hara hetta naa. | それにしても腹減ったなあ。 | Even so, I'm starving.
sore ni shitemo | それにしても | even so, all the same
hara | 腹 | belly
hetta | 減った | got empty (past of "heru")
~naa | なあ | (musing)

> Koko nara kaette mitsukaranai kamoshiremasen ne. | ここなら かえって見つからないかもしれませんね。 | In here, we might actually not be found.
koko nara | ここなら | if it's here
kaette | かえって | on the contrary, actually
mitsukaranai | 見つからない | won't be found
kamoshiremasen | かもしれません | might (polite)
~ne | ね | don't you think
