# Sentences: Episode 9 | Short lines from the ninth episode
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Kaizoku ga semete kita zo! | 海賊が攻めてきたぞ！ | Pirates are attacking!
kaizoku | 海賊 | pirates
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
semete kita | 攻めてきた | have come attacking
~zo | ぞ | (emphatic)

> Minna nigero! | みんな逃げろ！ | Everybody run!
minna | みんな | everyone
nigero | 逃げろ | run! (command form of "nigeru")

> Mubou da wa. Kono mama Gurando Rain ni hairu nante. | 無謀だわ。このままグランドラインに入るなんて。 | It's reckless — entering the Grand Line as we are.
mubou | 無謀 | reckless
~da wa | だわ | it is (feminine)
kono mama | このまま | like this, as we are
~Gurando Rain | グランドライン | the Grand Line
~ni | に | into
hairu | 入る | to enter
nante | なんて | such a thing as

> Yappa niku ga hoshii yo na. | やっぱ肉が欲しいよな。 | We really do want meat, huh.
yappa | やっぱ | after all (casual "yappari")
niku | 肉 | meat
~ga | が | (marks what is wanted)
hoshii | 欲しい | to want
~yo na | よな | don't we

> Mainichi kudamono bakka tte no mo na. | 毎日果物ばっかってのもな。 | Nothing but fruit every day gets old.
mainichi | 毎日 | every day
kudamono | 果物 | fruit
bakka | ばっか | nothing but (casual "bakari")
~tte no mo na | ってのもな | "…is a bit much"

> Sonna n ja nakute… | そんなんじゃなくて… | That's not what I mean…
sonna n | そんなん | that sort of thing
ja nakute | じゃなくて | not… but

> Sekai de mottomo kiken na basho na no. | 世界で最も危険な場所なの。 | It's the most dangerous place in the world.
sekai de | 世界で | in the world
mottomo | 最も | the most
kiken na | 危険な | dangerous
basho | 場所 | place
~na no | なの | it is (explaining, feminine)

> Nakama mo iru yo na. | 仲間もいるよな。 | We need crewmates too.
nakama | 仲間 | crewmates
~mo | も | too
iru | 要る | to need
~yo na | よな | don't we

> Totemo buji de irareru to wa omoenai wa. | とても無事でいられるとは思えないわ。 | I really can't see us coming through safely.
totemo | とても | (not) possibly
buji de irareru | 無事でいられる | can stay safe
~to wa | とは | (quoting: "that…")
omoenai | 思えない | can't think, can't imagine
~wa | わ | (feminine)

> Sake mo wasurenna. | 酒も忘れんな。 | Don't forget the booze either.
sake | 酒 | alcohol
~mo | も | too
wasurenna | 忘れんな | don't forget (casual "wasureru na")

> Kyou koso tocchimete yaru zo! | 今日こそとっちめてやるぞ！ | Today's the day I teach you a lesson!
kyou koso | 今日こそ | today for sure
tocchimete yaru | とっちめてやる | I'll give (you) what for
~zo | ぞ | (threatening)

> Mata nigerarechimatta ka. | また逃げられちまったか。 | He got away from us again, huh.
mata | また | again
nigerarechimatta | 逃げられちまった | (he) got away on us — passive + rough "-chimau"
~ka | か | huh

> Ittai doko ikiyagattanda. | 一体どこ行きやがったんだ。 | Where the hell did he go?
ittai | 一体 | (where) on earth
doko | どこ | where
ikiyagatta | 行きやがった | damn well went ("iku" + rough "-yagaru")
~n da | んだ | (demanding)

> Omaera futari dake ka? | お前ら二人だけか？ | Just the two of you?
omaera | お前ら | you guys (rough)
futari | 二人 | two people
dake | だけ | only
~ka | か | (question: "…?")

> Nani sawaidenda? Aitsu. | 何騒いでんだ？あいつ。 | What's he making a fuss about?
nani | 何 | what
sawaiden | 騒いでん | making a fuss (casual "sawaide iru n")
~da | だ | is (he)
aitsu | あいつ | he, that guy

> Kaizoku ga kaizoku ni bibitte dou surun desu ka! | 海賊が海賊にビビってどうするんですか！ | What good is a pirate who's scared of pirates?!
kaizoku | 海賊 | pirate
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
~kaizoku | 海賊 | pirates
~ni | に | of, at
bibitte | ビビって | getting scared (te-form of "bibiru")
dou surun desu ka | どうするんですか | what's the use?! (polite)
