# Sentences: Episode 5 | Short lines from the fifth episode
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Hito ga futte kita zo. | 人が降ってきたぞ。 | A person just fell from the sky!
hito | 人 | person
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
futte kita | 降ってきた | came falling down
~zo | ぞ | (emphatic)

> Tasuke ni kite kureta no ne. | 助けに来てくれたのね。 | You came to save me, didn't you.
tasuke ni | 助けに | to help, to rescue
kite kureta | 来てくれた | came (for me)
~no ne | のね | didn't you (feminine)

> Warui kedo, ato wa makaseta wa. | 悪いけど、あとは任せたわ。 | Sorry, but I'll leave the rest to you.
warui kedo | 悪いけど | sorry, but
ato | あと | the rest
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
makaseta | 任せた | left it to you (past of "makaseru")
~wa | わ | (feminine)

> Dokka meshi kuu toko nee ka? | どっかメシ食うとこねえか？ | Is there anywhere to eat around here?
dokka | どっか | somewhere (casual "dokoka")
meshi | メシ | food, a meal (rough)
kuu | 食う | to eat (rough)
toko | とこ | place (casual "tokoro")
nee ka | ねえか | isn't there? (rough)

> Ore no takaramono ni sawaru na. | 俺の宝物に触るな。 | Don't touch my treasure.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
takaramono | 宝物 | treasure, prized possession
~ni | に | (marks what is touched)
sawaru | 触る | to touch
na | な | don't (after a verb)

> Tsuyoi no ne, anta. | 強いのね、あんた。 | You're strong, aren't you.
tsuyoi | 強い | strong
~no ne | のね | aren't you (feminine)
anta | あんた | you (casual)

> Nee, atashi to kumanai? | ねえ、あたしと組まない？ | Hey, why don't you team up with me?
nee | ねえ | hey
atashi | あたし | I (feminine, casual)
~to | と | with
kumanai | 組まない | won't you team up? (negative of "kumu" as an invitation)

> Atashi ga gochisou shite agemashou ka. | あたしがごちそうしてあげましょうか。 | Shall I treat you to a meal?
atashi | あたし | I (feminine, casual)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
gochisou shite | ごちそうして | treat (someone) to a meal
agemashou ka | あげましょうか | shall I (do it for you)?

> Mada tsukamaran no ka? | まだ捕まらんのか？ | They still haven't been caught?
mada | まだ | still, (not) yet
tsukamaran | 捕まらん | isn't caught (old-style negative of "tsukamaru")
~no ka | のか | (pressing question)

> Ore ni sakarau to dou naru? | 俺に逆らうとどうなる？ | What happens when you defy me?
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ni | に | against
sakarau | 逆らう | to defy
~to | と | if, when
dou naru | どうなる | what happens

> Nido to iimasen. | 二度と言いません。 | I'll never say it again.
nido to | 二度と | never again
iimasen | 言いません | won't say (polite)

> Kaizu wa kanarazu torikaese. | 海図は必ず取り返せ。 | Get that chart back, no matter what.
kaizu | 海図 | sea chart
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
kanarazu | 必ず | without fail
torikaese | 取り返せ | get it back! (command form of "torikaesu")

> Omae, kono ie ni hitori de sunden no ka? | お前、この家に一人で住んでんのか？ | Do you live in this house alone?
omae | お前 | you (rough)
kono | この | this
ie | 家 | house
~ni | に | in
hitori de | 一人で | alone
sunden no ka | 住んでんのか | are you living? (casual "sunde iru no ka")

> Atashi wa kono machi no ningen ja nai wa. | あたしはこの町の人間じゃないわ。 | I'm not from this town.
atashi | あたし | I (feminine, casual)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
kono | この | this
machi | 町 | town
~no | の | of
ningen | 人間 | person
ja nai | じゃない | am not
~wa | わ | (feminine)

> Demo dorobou wa dorobou daro. | でも泥棒は泥棒だろ。 | But a thief is a thief, right?
demo | でも | but
dorobou | 泥棒 | thief
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
~dorobou | 泥棒 | thief
daro | だろ | right?

> Sonna ni kaseide dou sunda? | そんなに稼いでどうすんだ？ | What will you do with all that money you earn?
sonna ni | そんなに | that much
kaseide | 稼いで | earning (te-form of "kasegu")
dou sunda | どうすんだ | what will you do (rough "dou suru n da")
