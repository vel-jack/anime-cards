# Sentences: Episode 8 | Short lines from the eighth episode
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Kosodoro ja nee. Kaizoku da! | コソ泥じゃねえ。海賊だ！ | I'm not a petty thief. I'm a pirate!
kosodoro | コソ泥 | petty thief
ja nee | じゃねえ | am not (rough)
kaizoku | 海賊 | pirate
~da | だ | is

> Ore wa aitsu dake wa yurusanee! | 俺はあいつだけは許さねえ！ | He's the one person I'll never forgive!
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
aitsu | あいつ | him, that guy
dake | だけ | only
~wa | は | (emphasis: "him at least")
yurusanee | 許さねえ | won't forgive (rough "yurusanai")

> Docchi mo makenna yo! | どっちも負けんなよ！ | Don't lose, either of you!
docchi mo | どっちも | both, either
makenna | 負けんな | don't lose (casual "makeru na")
~yo | よ | (cheering)

> Mada iu ka, kora! | まだ言うか、こら！ | You're still saying that?!
mada | まだ | still
iu | 言う | to say
~ka | か | (challenging)
kora | こら | hey!

> Aa, nando demo iu ze. | ああ、何度でも言うぜ。 | Yeah, I'll say it as many times as it takes.
aa | ああ | yeah
nando demo | 何度でも | any number of times
iu | 言う | to say
~ze | ぜ | (rough, masculine)

> Ore ga tadashiinda! | 俺が正しいんだ！ | I'm the one who's right!
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
tadashii | 正しい | right, correct
~n da | んだ | (insisting)

> Kyou to iu kyou wa kecchaku wo tsukete yaru. | 今日という今日は決着をつけてやる。 | Today of all days, I'm settling this.
kyou to iu kyou | 今日という今日 | this very day, today of all days
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
kecchaku wo tsukete | 決着をつけて | settle it
yaru | やる | I'll (do it to you)

> Ii kagen ni shiro! | いいかげんにしろ！ | Knock it off!
ii kagen ni | いいかげんに | in moderation, within reason
shiro | しろ | do! (command form of "suru")

> Dou demo ii daro! | どうでもいいだろ！ | Who cares!
dou demo ii | どうでもいい | it doesn't matter
~daro | だろ | does it!

> Atama hiyase! | 頭冷やせ！ | Cool your heads!
atama | 頭 | head
hiyase | 冷やせ | cool! (command form of "hiyasu")

> Ureshisou da na, Bagii. | うれしそうだな、バギー。 | You look happy, Buggy.
ureshisou | うれしそう | looking happy ("ureshii" + "sou")
~da na | だな | aren't you
~Bagii | バギー | Buggy (name)

> Kaizoku wa ubatte nanbo da. Chigau ka? | 海賊は奪って何ぼだ。違うか？ | A pirate is worth what he steals. Am I wrong?
kaizoku | 海賊 | pirate
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
ubatte | 奪って | stealing (te-form of "ubau")
nanbo da | 何ぼだ | is what counts
chigau | 違う | to be wrong, differ
~ka | か | (question: "…?")

> Ore wa nanimo shiranee. | 俺は何も知らねえ。 | I don't know anything.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
nanimo | 何も | nothing (with a negative)
shiranee | 知らねえ | don't know (rough "shiranai")

> Orite dou suru tsumori da? | 下りてどうするつもりだ？ | What do you plan to do after you leave?
orite | 下りて | get off and… (te-form of "oriru")
dou suru | どうする | do what
tsumori | つもり | intention, plan
~da | だ | is

> Jibun no fune wo motsunda. | 自分の船を持つんだ。 | I'll have a ship of my own.
jibun no | 自分の | my own
fune | 船 | ship
~wo | を | (marks the object)
motsu | 持つ | to have, own
~n da | んだ | (explaining)

> Shinrai dekiru nakama wo atsumete sekai wo mite mawarou to omotteru. | 信頼できる仲間を集めて世界を見て回ろうと思ってる。 | I'm thinking of gathering crewmates I can trust and travelling the world.
shinrai dekiru | 信頼できる | trustworthy
nakama | 仲間 | crewmates
~wo | を | (marks the object)
atsumete | 集めて | gather and… (te-form of "atsumeru")
sekai | 世界 | world
~wo | を | (marks the object)
mite mawarou | 見て回ろう | go around seeing (volitional)
to omotteru | と思ってる | am thinking of
