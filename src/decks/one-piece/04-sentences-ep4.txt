# Sentences: Episode 4 | Short lines from the fourth episode
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Konna hiroi umi de hito ni deaeru nante. | こんな広い海で人に出会えるなんて。 | To think I'd run into someone on such a vast sea.
konna | こんな | such, this kind of
hiroi | 広い | wide, vast
umi | 海 | sea
~de | で | on, in
hito | 人 | person
~ni | に | (marks who you meet)
deaeru | 出会える | can meet, come across
nante | なんて | to think that…

> Mizu wo ippai itadakemasen ka? | 水を一杯いただけませんか？ | Could I please have a cup of water?
mizu | 水 | water
~wo | を | (marks the object)
ippai | 一杯 | one cup
itadakemasen ka | いただけませんか | could I receive? (humble)

> Okane nara ikura demo sashiagemasu. | お金ならいくらでも差し上げます。 | As for money, I'll give you as much as you like.
okane nara | お金なら | if it's money
ikura demo | いくらでも | any amount
sashiagemasu | 差し上げます | I'll give (humble)

> Temee, damashita na! | てめえ、だましたな！ | You tricked us!
temee | てめえ | you (very rude)
damashita | だました | tricked (past of "damasu")
~na | な | didn't you!

> Otakara wa moratoku wa. | お宝はもらっとくわ。 | I'll be taking the treasure.
otakara | お宝 | treasure, loot
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
moratoku | もらっとく | I'll take and keep (short for "moratte oku")
~wa | わ | (feminine)

> Itsu ni nattara riku ni tsukunda yo. | いつになったら陸に着くんだよ。 | When are we ever going to reach land?
itsu ni nattara | いつになったら | when (will it finally be that)
riku | 陸 | land
~ni | に | at, to
tsuku | 着く | to arrive
~n da yo | んだよ | (impatient question)

> Jibun no mura e mo kaerenaku nacchimatte… | 自分の村へも帰れなくなっちまって… | I ended up unable to even get back to my own village…
jibun no | 自分の | my own
mura | 村 | village
~e mo | へも | even to
kaerenaku | 帰れなく | unable to return
nacchimatte | なっちまって | ended up becoming (rough "natte shimatte")

> Nanda, omae maigo ka. | 何だ、お前迷子か。 | Oh, so you're just lost.
nanda | 何だ | oh, so that's it
omae | お前 | you (rough)
maigo | 迷子 | someone who is lost
~ka | か | (realising)

> Sono iikata wa yose! | その言い方はよせ！ | Don't put it like that!
sono | その | that
iikata | 言い方 | way of saying it
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
yose | よせ | stop it! (command form of "yosu")

> Tsugi no koukai ni tsuretette kure yo. | 次の航海に連れてってくれよ。 | Take me along on your next voyage.
tsugi no | 次の | next
koukai | 航海 | voyage
~ni | に | on, to
tsuretette | 連れてって | take (me) along (short for "tsurete itte")
kure yo | くれよ | do it for me, come on

> Ore datte kaizoku ni naritainda. | 俺だって海賊になりたいんだ。 | I want to be a pirate too.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
datte | だって | too, even
kaizoku | 海賊 | pirate
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
naritai | なりたい | want to become
~n da | んだ | (explaining, insisting)

> Omae nanka ga kaizoku ni nareru ka. | お前なんかが海賊になれるか。 | As if someone like you could be a pirate.
omae nanka | お前なんか | someone like you (belittling)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
kaizoku | 海賊 | pirate
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
nareru | なれる | can become
~ka | か | (rhetorical: "as if!")

> Ore wa gaki ja nai! Mou otona da. | 俺はガキじゃない！もう大人だ。 | I'm not a kid! I'm already an adult.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
gaki | ガキ | kid, brat
ja nai | じゃない | am not
mou | もう | already
otona | 大人 | adult
~da | だ | is

> Maa, sou okoru na. | まあ、そう怒るな。 | Now, now, don't get so mad.
maa | まあ | now, now
sou | そう | so, like that
okoru | 怒る | to get angry
na | な | don't (after a verb)

> Sake wo utte kure. | 酒を売ってくれ。 | Sell us some booze.
sake | 酒 | alcohol
~wo | を | (marks the object)
utte | 売って | sell (te-form of "uru")
kure | くれ | do it for me (blunt request)
