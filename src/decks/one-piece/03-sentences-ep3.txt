# Sentences: Episode 3 | Short lines from the third episode
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Dakara ittaro? Ore wa tsuyoi tte. | だから言ったろ？俺は強いって。 | Told you, didn't I? I'm strong.
dakara | だから | see, so
ittaro | 言ったろ | I said, didn't I (casual "itta darou")
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
tsuyoi | 強い | strong
~tte | って | (quoting: "that…")

> Temee, ittai nanimono nanda? | てめえ、一体何者なんだ？ | Just who the hell are you?
temee | てめえ | you (very rude)
ittai | 一体 | (who) on earth
nanimono | 何者 | who, what sort of person
~nanda | なんだ | are (you)?!

> Ore wa Rufi. Kaizoku-ou ni naru otoko da. | 俺はルフィ。海賊王になる男だ。 | I'm Luffy. The man who'll become King of the Pirates.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
~Rufi | ルフィ | Luffy (name)
kaizoku-ou | 海賊王 | King of the Pirates
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
naru | なる | to become
otoko | 男 | man
~da | だ | is

> Imi wakatte itten no ka? | 意味分かって言ってんのか？ | Do you even understand what you're saying?
imi | 意味 | meaning
wakatte | 分かって | understanding (te-form of "wakaru")
itten no ka | 言ってんのか | are you saying it? (rough "itte iru no ka")

> Hoka ni imi ga aru ka? | 他に意味があるか？ | Does it have any other meaning?
hoka ni | 他に | other, besides that
imi | 意味 | meaning
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
aru | ある | there is
~ka | か | (question: "…?")

> Rufi-san wa honki desu. | ルフィさんは本気です。 | Luffy is serious.
~Rufi-san | ルフィさん | Mr. Luffy
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
honki | 本気 | serious, for real
~desu | です | is (polite)

> Ore wa santouryuu nan de ne. | 俺は三刀流なんでね。 | I use the three-sword style, you see.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
santouryuu | 三刀流 | three-sword style
~nan de ne | なんでね | you see (giving a reason)

> Kono mama kaigun ni korosareru no to docchi ga ii? | このまま海軍に殺されるのとどっちがいい？ | Or being killed by the Navy as you are — which do you prefer?
kono mama | このまま | like this, as things are
kaigun | 海軍 | the Navy
~ni | に | by
korosareru | 殺される | to be killed (passive of "korosu")
~no to | のと | "…and (the other option)"
docchi | どっち | which (of two)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
ii | いい | good, better

> Natte yarou ja nee ka, kaizoku ni. | なってやろうじゃねえか、海賊に。 | Fine, I'll become one — a pirate.
natte yarou | なってやろう | I'll go and become (one)
ja nee ka | じゃねえか | why not (rough)
kaizoku | 海賊 | pirate
~ni | に | (marks what you become)

> Wakattara sassato kono nawa wo hodoke! | 分かったら さっさとこの縄をほどけ！ | If you get it, hurry up and untie this rope!
wakattara | 分かったら | if you understand
sassato | さっさと | quickly, get on with it
kono | この | this
nawa | 縄 | rope
~wo | を | (marks the object)
hodoke | ほどけ | untie! (command form of "hodoku")

> Tada no ningen ja nee zo. | ただの人間じゃねえぞ。 | He's no ordinary human.
tada no | ただの | ordinary, mere
ningen | 人間 | human
ja nee | じゃねえ | is not (rough)
~zo | ぞ | (emphatic)

> Ore ni sakarau yatsu wa mina shikei da. | 俺に逆らうヤツは皆 死刑だ。 | Anyone who defies me gets the death penalty.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ni | に | against, to
sakarau | 逆らう | to defy
yatsu | ヤツ | guy, anyone
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
mina | 皆 | all
shikei | 死刑 | death penalty
~da | だ | is

> Temeera, ugoku to kiru zo. | てめえら、動くと斬るぞ。 | Move, and I'll cut you down.
temeera | てめえら | you lot (very rude)
ugoku | 動く | to move
~to | と | if, when
kiru | 斬る | to cut down
~zo | ぞ | (threatening)

> Sekai-ichi no kengou ni naru koto da. | 世界一の剣豪になることだ。 | To become the world's greatest swordsman.
sekai-ichi | 世界一 | the best in the world
~no | の | (links to the noun)
kengou | 剣豪 | master swordsman
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
naru | なる | to become
koto | こと | (turns a verb into a thing: "to become")
~da | だ | is

> Ore wa jibun no yabou dake wa tsuranuku zo. | 俺は自分の野望だけは貫くぞ。 | My own ambition is the one thing I will see through.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
jibun | 自分 | oneself, my own
~no | の | 's (possessive)
yabou | 野望 | ambition
dake | だけ | only
~wa | は | (emphasis: "at least this")
tsuranuku | 貫く | to see through, stick to
~zo | ぞ | (emphatic)

> Ore wa Gomu Gomu no Mi wo kuttanda. | 俺はゴムゴムの実を食ったんだ。 | I ate the Gum-Gum Fruit.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
~Gomu Gomu no Mi | ゴムゴムの実 | the Gum-Gum Fruit
~wo | を | (marks the object)
kutta | 食った | ate (rough; past of "kuu")
~n da | んだ | (explaining)
