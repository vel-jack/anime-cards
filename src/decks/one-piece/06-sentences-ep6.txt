# Sentences: Episode 6 | Short lines from the sixth episode
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Ushiro kara sasu nante hikyou da zo! | 後ろから刺すなんて卑怯だぞ！ | Stabbing from behind is a dirty trick!
ushiro kara | 後ろから | from behind
sasu | 刺す | to stab
nante | なんて | such a thing as
hikyou | 卑怯 | cowardly, unfair
~da zo | だぞ | it is! (emphatic)

> Omee wa damatte soko ni iro. | おめえは黙ってそこにいろ。 | You shut up and stay there.
omee | おめえ | you (rough "omae")
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
damatte | 黙って | silently (te-form of "damaru")
soko ni | そこに | there
iro | いろ | stay! (command form of "iru")

> Ore wa ore no yaritee you ni yaru. | 俺は俺のやりてえようにやる。 | I'll do things the way I want to.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
ore no | 俺の | my
yaritee | やりてえ | want to do (rough "yaritai")
you ni | ように | the way, as
yaru | やる | to do

> Joudan ja nai wa yo. | 冗談じゃないわよ。 | You've got to be kidding me.
joudan | 冗談 | joke
ja nai | じゃない | is not
~wa yo | わよ | (feminine, firm)

> Nande soko made suru no? Kaizoku no kuse ni. | 何でそこまでするの？海賊のくせに。 | Why go that far? You're only pirates.
nande | 何で | why
soko made | そこまで | that far
suru | する | to do
~no | の | (soft question)
kaizoku | 海賊 | pirate
no kuse ni | のくせに | even though (you're just)

> Nanda? Kono inu wa. | 何だ？この犬は。 | What's with this dog?
nanda | 何だ | what is it?
kono | この | this
inu | 犬 | dog
~wa | は | (marks the topic)

> Zenzen ugokanee zo. | 全然動かねえぞ。 | It's not moving at all.
zenzen | 全然 | not at all
ugokanee | 動かねえ | doesn't move (rough "ugokanai")
~zo | ぞ | (emphatic)

> Shinden no ka na? | 死んでんのかな？ | I wonder if it's dead?
shinden | 死んでん | is dead (casual "shinde iru")
~no ka na | のかな | I wonder

> Chi ga tarinee… | 血が足りねえ… | I've lost too much blood…
chi | 血 | blood
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
tarinee | 足りねえ | not enough (rough "tarinai")

> Ichiou watashite oku wa. | 一応渡しておくわ。 | I'll hand this over, just in case.
ichiou | 一応 | just in case, for now
watashite oku | 渡しておく | to hand over (for later)
~wa | わ | (feminine)

> Tasukete moratta kara ne. | 助けてもらったからね。 | Since you did save me.
tasukete moratta | 助けてもらった | got saved (by you)
kara ne | からね | because, you know

> Kanchigai shinaide. | 勘違いしないで。 | Don't get the wrong idea.
kanchigai | 勘違い | the wrong idea, misunderstanding
shinaide | しないで | don't (do)

> Anta ni kari wo tsukuritakunakatta dake yo. | あんたに借りを作りたくなかっただけよ。 | I just didn't want to owe you one.
anta | あんた | you (casual)
~ni | に | to
kari | 借り | a debt, a favour owed
~wo | を | (marks the object)
tsukuritakunakatta | 作りたくなかった | didn't want to make
dake | だけ | just, only
~yo | よ | (asserting)

> Washi wa kono machi no chouchou ja. | わしはこの町の町長じゃ。 | I'm the mayor of this town.
washi | わし | I (old man's speech)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
kono | この | this
machi | 町 | town
~no | の | of
chouchou | 町長 | town mayor
~ja | じゃ | is (an old man's "da")

> Hayaku isha ni misen to. | 早く医者に診せんと。 | We must get him to a doctor quickly.
hayaku | 早く | quickly
isha | 医者 | doctor
~ni | に | to
misen to | 診せんと | must show (him to) — old-style "misenai to"

> Konna toko ni suwatte nani yattenda? | こんなとこに座って何やってんだ？ | What's it doing, sitting in a place like this?
konna toko | こんなとこ | a place like this
~ni | に | in, at
suwatte | 座って | sitting (te-form of "suwaru")
nani | 何 | what
yattenda | やってんだ | is (it) doing? (casual "yatte iru n da")
