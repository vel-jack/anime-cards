# Sentences: Episode 2 | Short lines from the second episode
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Kono mama susumeba mousugu shima ga miete kimasu yo. | このまま進めばもうすぐ島が見えてきますよ。 | If we keep going like this, the island will come into view soon.
kono mama | このまま | like this, as things are
susumeba | 進めば | if (we) go on (conditional of "susumu")
mousugu | もうすぐ | very soon
shima | 島 | island
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
miete kimasu | 見えてきます | will come into view (polite)
~yo | よ | (informing)

> Waratteru baai ja arimasen yo. | 笑ってる場合じゃありませんよ。 | This is no time to be laughing.
waratteru | 笑ってる | laughing (short for "waratte iru")
baai | 場合 | time, situation
ja arimasen | じゃありません | is not (polite)
~yo | よ | (asserting)

> Mada kimeta wake ja nai sa. | まだ決めたわけじゃないさ。 | I haven't decided yet.
mada | まだ | (not) yet
kimeta | 決めた | decided (past of "kimeru")
wake ja nai | わけじゃない | it's not that…
~sa | さ | (casual, easygoing)

> Warui yatsu dakara tsukamatterun desu. | 悪いヤツだから捕まってるんです。 | He's locked up because he's a bad guy.
warui | 悪い | bad
yatsu | ヤツ | guy
dakara | だから | because
tsukamatteru | 捕まってる | is caught, locked up (short for "tsukamatte iru")
~n desu | んです | (explaining, polite)

> Tonikaku kichi ni itte miyou. | とにかく基地に行ってみよう。 | Anyway, let's go check out the base.
tonikaku | とにかく | anyway
kichi | 基地 | base
~ni | に | to
itte miyou | 行ってみよう | let's go and see

> Omae, kaihei ni narun daro? | お前、海兵になるんだろ？ | You're going to become a marine, right?
omae | お前 | you (rough)
kaihei | 海兵 | marine
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
naru | なる | to become
~n daro | んだろ | right?

> Demo mada kokoro no junbi ga… | でもまだ心の準備が… | But I'm not mentally ready yet…
demo | でも | but
mada | まだ | still, (not) yet
kokoro | 心 | heart, mind
~no | の | of
junbi | 準備 | preparation
~ga | が | (trailing off)

> Boku, nandaka fuan ni natte kimashita. | 僕、何だか不安になってきました。 | I'm starting to feel kind of uneasy.
boku | 僕 | I (soft, boyish)
nandaka | 何だか | somehow, kind of
fuan | 不安 | uneasy
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
natte kimashita | なってきました | have started to become (polite)

> Koko de owakare desu ne. | ここでお別れですね。 | So this is where we part ways.
koko de | ここで | here, at this point
owakare | お別れ | parting, goodbye
~desu ne | ですね | it is, isn't it (polite)

> Daijoubu! Ore, tsuyoi kara. | 大丈夫！俺、強いから。 | It's fine! I'm strong.
daijoubu | 大丈夫 | fine, all right
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
tsuyoi | 強い | strong
kara | から | because

> Mou zutto nanimo tabetenain desho? | もうずっと何も食べてないんでしょ？ | You haven't eaten anything for ages, have you?
mou zutto | もうずっと | for a long time now
nanimo | 何も | nothing (with a negative)
tabetenai | 食べてない | haven't eaten (short for "tabete inai")
~n desho | んでしょ | have you?

> Isshoukenmei tsukutta kara. | 一生懸命作ったから。 | Because I worked hard making it.
isshoukenmei | 一生懸命 | with all one's effort
tsukutta | 作った | made (past of "tsukuru")
kara | から | because

> Hara nanka hecchainee. | 腹なんか減っちゃいねえ。 | I'm not hungry at all.
hara | 腹 | belly
nanka | なんか | (dismissive: "as if…")
hecchainee | 減っちゃいねえ | isn't empty (rough "hette wa inai")

> Ore no meirei ga kikenai no ka? | 俺の命令が聞けないのか？ | You can't follow my orders?
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
meirei | 命令 | order
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
kikenai | 聞けない | can't obey, can't listen to
~no ka | のか | (pressing question)

> Doko mo itakunai kai? | どこも痛くないかい？ | Nothing hurts anywhere?
doko mo | どこも | anywhere (with a negative: nowhere)
itakunai | 痛くない | doesn't hurt (negative of "itai")
~kai | かい | (gentle question)
