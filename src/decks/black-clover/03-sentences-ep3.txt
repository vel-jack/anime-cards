# Sentences: Episode 3 | Short lines from "To the Royal Capital of the Clover Kingdom!"
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Omae mo nomu ka? | お前も飲むか？ | Want some too?
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~mo | も | too
nomu | 飲む | to drink
~ka | か | (question: "…?")

> Maryoku ga nakutatte noboritsumete miseru ze! | 魔力がなくたって上り詰めてみせるぜ！ | Even without magic power, I'll climb to the top — just watch!
maryoku | 魔力 | magic power
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
nakutatte | なくたって | even without (casual "nakutemo")
noboritsumete | 上り詰めて | climb to the very top (te-form of "noboritsumeru")
miseru ze | みせるぜ | I'll show you (I can do it)

> Kitto Yuno datte tokkun shiteru. | きっとユノだって特訓してる。 | I bet Yuno is training too.
kitto | きっと | surely
~Yuno | ユノ | Yuno (name)
datte | だって | too, even
tokkun shiteru | 特訓してる | is training hard (short for "tokkun shite iru")

> Futari no mokuhyou wa itsuka Mahoutei ni naru koto. | 二人の目標はいつか魔法帝になること。 | Their goal is to become the Wizard King someday.
futari | 二人 | the two of them
~no | の | 's (possessive)
mokuhyou | 目標 | goal
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
itsuka | いつか | someday
Mahoutei | 魔法帝 | Wizard King
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
naru | なる | to become
koto | こと | (turns a verb into a thing: "to become")

> Asuta wa ukeru dake muda desu yo. | アスタは受けるだけムダですよ。 | It's pointless for Asta to even take it.
~Asuta | アスタ | Asta (name)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
ukeru | 受ける | to take (an exam)
dake | だけ | just, merely
muda | ムダ | pointless
~desu yo | ですよ | it is (polite, asserting)

> Saisho kara ukenai hou ga aitsu no tame ja nai ka to… | 最初から受けないほうがあいつのためじゃないかと… | I just think not taking it at all might be better for him…
saisho kara | 最初から | from the start
ukenai | 受けない | not take
hou ga | ほうが | (this option) is better
aitsu | あいつ | him, that guy
~no | の | 's (possessive)
tame | ため | sake, good
~ja nai ka to | じゃないかと | "isn't it, I think…"

> Inaku naru to sabishii desu yo. | いなくなると寂しいですよ。 | I'll be lonely when they're gone.
inaku naru | いなくなる | to be gone (lit. "become not here")
~to | と | when, if
sabishii | 寂しい | lonely
~desu yo | ですよ | it is (polite, asserting)

> Yaritai koto wa yarasete agetain desu. | やりたいことはやらせてあげたいんです。 | I want to let them do what they want to do.
yaritai | やりたい | want to do
koto | こと | thing
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
yarasete | やらせて | let (them) do (causative te-form of "yaru")
agetai | あげたい | want to (do it for them)
~n desu | んです | (explaining, polite)

> Are wa hontou ni gurimowaaru nan deshou ka? | あれは本当に魔導書なんでしょうか？ | Is that really a grimoire, I wonder?
are | あれ | that (over there)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
hontou ni | 本当に | really
gurimowaaru | 魔導書 | grimoire
~nan deshou ka | なんでしょうか | is it, I wonder (polite)

> Docchi ga hayakatta? | どっちが速かった？ | Which of us was faster?
docchi | どっち | which (of two)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
hayakatta | 速かった | was fast (past of "hayai")

> Chanto miteta no ka? | ちゃんと見てたのか？ | Were you really watching properly?
chanto | ちゃんと | properly
miteta | 見てた | were watching (short for "mite ita")
~no ka | のか | (pressing question)

> Ore no kachi da. | 俺の勝ちだ。 | It's my win.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
kachi | 勝ち | a win
~da | だ | is

> Igai to otonagenai yo ne, Yuno-nii. | 意外と大人げないよね、ユノ兄。 | Yuno's surprisingly childish, isn't he.
igai to | 意外と | surprisingly
otonagenai | 大人げない | childish, immature
~yo ne | よね | isn't he
~Yuno-nii | ユノ兄 | big brother Yuno
