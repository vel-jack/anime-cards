# Sentences: Episode 2 | Short lines from "A Young Man's Vow" — learn the words, then read the line
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Yuno ni wa ore ga tsuiteru! | ユノには俺がついてる！ | You've got me by your side, Yuno!
~Yuno | ユノ | Yuno (name)
~ni wa | には | for, to (as the topic)
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
tsuiteru | ついてる | am with you, at your side (short for "tsuite iru")

> Ue de minna ga matteru! | 上でみんなが待ってる！ | Everyone's waiting up top!
ue | 上 | top, above
~de | で | at
minna | みんな | everyone
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
matteru | 待ってる | are waiting (short for "matte iru")

> Otona ni naru made zettai akiramenai kara na! | 大人になるまで絶対諦めないからな！ | I absolutely won't give up until I'm an adult!
otona | 大人 | adult
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
naru | なる | to become
made | まで | until
zettai | 絶対 | absolutely
akiramenai | 諦めない | won't give up
~kara na | からな | (emphatic "so there!")

> Kokuou-sama no tsugi ni erai no yo. | 国王様の次に偉いのよ。 | He's the most important person after the king.
kokuou-sama | 国王様 | His Majesty the king
~no | の | 's, of
tsugi ni | 次に | next (after)
erai | 偉い | important, great
~no yo | のよ | (soft explaining tone, feminine)

> Minna wo motto shiawase ni dekiru darou? | みんなをもっと幸せにできるだろう？ | I'd be able to make everyone happier, right?
minna | みんな | everyone
~wo | を | (marks the object)
motto | もっと | more
shiawase ni | 幸せに | happy ("shiawase ni suru" = to make happy)
dekiru | できる | can do
darou | だろう | right?, probably

> Nande itsumade tattemo mahou tsukaenain da?! | なんでいつまでたっても魔法使えないんだ？！ | Why can't I use magic, no matter how long it's been?!
nande | なんで | why
itsumade tattemo | いつまでたっても | no matter how much time passes
mahou | 魔法 | magic
tsukaenai | 使えない | can't use
~n da | んだ | (demanding an explanation)

> Dareka kono tegami wo todokete kurenai ka? | 誰かこの手紙を届けてくれないか？ | Would someone deliver this letter for me?
dareka | 誰か | someone
kono | この | this
tegami | 手紙 | letter
~wo | を | (marks the object)
todokete | 届けて | deliver (te-form of "todokeru")
kurenai ka | くれないか | won't you (do it for me)?

> Hitori de daijoubu ka? | 一人で大丈夫か？ | Will you be okay on your own?
hitori de | 一人で | alone, by yourself
daijoubu | 大丈夫 | okay, all right
~ka | か | (question: "…?")

> Kon'ya wa hiesou ne. | 今夜は冷えそうね。 | Looks like it'll get cold tonight.
kon'ya | 今夜 | tonight
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
hiesou | 冷えそう | looks like it'll get cold ("hieru" + "sou")
~ne | ね | isn't it

> Saki tabechaou yo. | 先食べちゃおうよ。 | Let's just eat first.
saki | 先 | first, ahead
tabechaou | 食べちゃおう | let's go ahead and eat ("taberu" + "-chau", volitional)
~yo | よ | (urging)

> Akiramenai no ga ore no mahou da! | 諦めないのが俺の魔法だ！ | Not giving up is my magic!
akiramenai | 諦めない | not giving up
~no ga | のが | (turns it into a noun: "…-ing is")
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
mahou | 魔法 | magic
~da | だ | is

> Kono kari wa itsuka kanarazu kaesu. | この借りはいつか必ず返す。 | I'll repay this debt someday, without fail.
kono | この | this
kari | 借り | debt, favour owed
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
itsuka | いつか | someday
kanarazu | 必ず | without fail
kaesu | 返す | to return, repay

> Yakusoku, oboeteru ka? | 約束、覚えてるか？ | Do you remember our promise?
yakusoku | 約束 | promise
oboeteru | 覚えてる | remember (short for "oboete iru")
~ka | か | (question: "…?")

> Ore ni kibou wo kureta no wa omae da. | 俺に希望をくれたのはお前だ。 | The one who gave me hope was you.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ni | に | to
kibou | 希望 | hope
~wo | を | (marks the object)
kureta | くれた | gave (to me)
~no wa | のは | "the one who…"
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~da | だ | is

> Docchi ga Mahoutei ni naru ka, shoubu da! | どっちが魔法帝になるか、勝負だ！ | Which of us becomes the Wizard King — it's a contest!
docchi | どっち | which (of two)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
Mahoutei | 魔法帝 | Wizard King
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
naru | なる | to become
~ka | か | ("which…", an embedded question)
shoubu | 勝負 | contest, showdown
~da | だ | is
