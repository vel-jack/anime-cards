# Sentences: Episode 7 | Short lines from "The Other New Recruit"
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Koko ga omae no heya da. | ここがお前の部屋だ。 | This is your room.
koko | ここ | here, this place
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
heya | 部屋 | room
~da | だ | is

> Jibun no heya toka nakatta nde… | 自分の部屋とかなかったんで… | I never had a room of my own, so…
jibun no | 自分の | my own
heya | 部屋 | room
toka | とか | or anything like that
nakatta | なかった | didn't have (past of "nai")
~nde | んで | so, because (casual "node")

> Minna issho no heya datta nde. | みんな一緒の部屋だったんで。 | We all shared one room, you see.
minna | みんな | everyone
issho no | 一緒の | the same, shared
heya | 部屋 | room
datta | だった | was
~nde | んで | so, because (casual "node")

> Ninmu ga aru made jiyuu da. | 任務があるまで自由だ。 | You're free until there's a mission.
ninmu | 任務 | mission
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
aru | ある | there is
made | まで | until
jiyuu | 自由 | free
~da | だ | is

> Chanto tegami kaitoke yo. | ちゃんと手紙書いとけよ。 | Make sure you write them a letter.
chanto | ちゃんと | properly
tegami | 手紙 | letter
kaitoke | 書いとけ | write it (and have it done) — short for "kaite oke"
~yo | よ | (urging)

> Kitto dou natta ka shinpai shiteru ze. | きっとどうなったか心配してるぜ。 | I bet they're worried about how it went.
kitto | きっと | surely
dou natta ka | どうなったか | how it turned out
shinpai shiteru | 心配してる | are worried (short for "shinpai shite iru")
~ze | ぜ | (rough, masculine)

> Nanika attara ore n toko koi ya. | 何かあったら俺んとこ来いや。 | If anything comes up, come to me.
nanika attara | 何かあったら | if something happens
ore n toko | 俺んとこ | my place, me (short for "ore no tokoro")
koi | 来い | come! (command form of "kuru")
~ya | や | (rough but friendly)

> Omae nado ni wa mottainai heya da. | お前などにはもったいない部屋だ。 | This room is too good for the likes of you.
omae | お前 | you (rough)
nado | など | the likes of
~ni wa | には | for
mottainai | もったいない | too good for, wasted on
heya | 部屋 | room
~da | だ | is

> Kiite iru no ka? | 聞いているのか？ | Are you listening?
kiite iru | 聞いている | are listening
~no ka | のか | (pressing question)

> Watashi no koto wa Kurausu-senpai to yobe. | 私のことはクラウス先輩と呼べ。 | Call me "Klaus-senpai".
watashi no koto | 私のこと | me (lit. "the matter of me")
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
~Kurausu-senpai | クラウス先輩 | Klaus-senpai
~to | と | (quoting: "as")
yobe | 呼べ | call! (command form of "yobu")

> Okureru na yo. | 遅れるなよ。 | Don't be late.
okureru | 遅れる | to be late
na | な | don't (after a verb)
~yo | よ | (warning)

> Asuta kara no tegami ni wa nante kaite aru no? | アスタからの手紙には何て書いてあるの？ | What does Asta's letter say?
~Asuta | アスタ | Asta (name)
kara no | からの | from
tegami | 手紙 | letter
~ni wa | には | in
nante | 何て | what
kaite aru | 書いてある | is written
~no | の | (soft question)

> Itsu kaette kuru tte? | いつ帰ってくるって？ | When does he say he's coming back?
itsu | いつ | when
kaette kuru | 帰ってくる | to come back
~tte | って | (does he say?)

> Sasuga ni ochikonde iru darou shi. | さすがに落ち込んでいるだろうし。 | Even he must be feeling down, after all.
sasuga ni | さすがに | even (he), as you would expect
ochikonde iru | 落ち込んでいる | is feeling down
darou | だろう | probably
~shi | し | and so

> Futari tomo goukaku ka… | 二人とも合格か… | So both of them passed…
futari tomo | 二人とも | both of them
goukaku | 合格 | passing (an exam)
~ka | か | (taking it in)

> Yume ja nakatta! | 夢じゃなかった！ | It wasn't a dream!
yume | 夢 | dream
ja nakatta | じゃなかった | was not
