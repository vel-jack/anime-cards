# Sentences: Episode 8 | Short lines from "Go! Go! First Mission"
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Danchou, asa desu yo. | 団長、朝ですよ。 | Captain, it's morning.
danchou | 団長 | captain
asa | 朝 | morning
~desu yo | ですよ | it is (polite, informing)

> Okosun ja nee, korosu zo! | 起こすんじゃねえ、殺すぞ！ | Don't wake me up — I'll kill you!
okosu | 起こす | to wake (someone) up
~n ja nee | んじゃねえ | don't! (rough)
korosu | 殺す | to kill
~zo | ぞ | (threatening)

> Jibun ga maiasa okose tte itta kuse ni! | 自分が毎朝起こせって言ったくせに！ | Even though you're the one who said to wake you every morning!
jibun ga | 自分が | (you) yourself
maiasa | 毎朝 | every morning
okose | 起こせ | wake (me)! (command form of "okosu")
~tte | って | (quoting: "that…")
itta | 言った | said
kuse ni | くせに | even though (resentful)

> Kyou mo asa kara yoku hataraita. | 今日も朝からよく働いた。 | I worked hard from the morning again today.
kyou mo | 今日も | today too
asa kara | 朝から | from the morning
yoku | よく | a lot, well
hataraita | 働いた | worked (past of "hataraku")

> Mahou Kishidan no shigoto wa takusan aru naa. | 魔法騎士団の仕事はたくさんあるなぁ。 | The Magic Knights sure have a lot of work.
~Mahou Kishidan | 魔法騎士団 | the Magic Knights
~no | の | 's (possessive)
shigoto | 仕事 | work, job
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
takusan | たくさん | a lot
aru | ある | there is
~naa | なぁ | (musing)

> Nande omae souji toka tetsudatte kunnai no? | なんでお前掃除とか手伝ってくんないの？ | Why won't you help with the cleaning or anything?
nande | なんで | why
omae | お前 | you (rough)
souji | 掃除 | cleaning
toka | とか | and such
tetsudatte | 手伝って | help (te-form of "tetsudau")
kunnai no | くんないの | won't you (do it for me)? — casual "kurenai no"

> Watashi wa ouzoku yo. | 私は王族よ。 | I am royalty.
watashi | 私 | I (polite)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
ouzoku | 王族 | royalty
~yo | よ | (asserting, feminine)

> Souji ya sentaku nante shita koto ga nai wa. | 掃除や洗濯なんてしたことがないわ。 | I've never done anything like cleaning or laundry.
souji | 掃除 | cleaning
~ya | や | and (among other things)
sentaku | 洗濯 | laundry
nante | なんて | things like
shita koto ga nai | したことがない | have never done
~wa | わ | (feminine)

> Soko ibaru toko ja nai yo ne! | そこ威張るとこじゃないよね！ | That's not something to brag about!
soko | そこ | that (point)
ibaru | 威張る | to brag, act big
toko | とこ | place, thing (casual "tokoro")
ja nai | じゃない | is not
~yo ne | よね | right?!

> Shikkari kyouryoku suru beki da to omoun desu kedo! | しっかり協力するべきだと思うんですけど！ | I think we really ought to cooperate!
shikkari | しっかり | properly, firmly
kyouryoku suru | 協力する | to cooperate
beki da | べきだ | should, ought to
to omou | と思う | I think
~n desu kedo | んですけど | (polite, pointed "…you know!")

> Somosomo Mahou Kishidan tte nani surun su ka? | そもそも魔法騎士団って何するんスか？ | What do the Magic Knights even do in the first place?
somosomo | そもそも | in the first place
~Mahou Kishidan | 魔法騎士団 | the Magic Knights
~tte | って | (casual topic marker)
nani | 何 | what
suru | する | to do
~n su ka | んスか | (casual-polite question)

> Maji de itten no ka? | マジで言ってんのか？ | Are you seriously asking that?
maji de | マジで | seriously
itten no ka | 言ってんのか | are you saying? (rough "itte iru no ka")

> Nanimo shiranaide nande hairou to omotta? | 何も知らないで なんで入ろうと思った？ | Why did you want to join without knowing anything?
nanimo | 何も | nothing (with a negative)
shiranaide | 知らないで | without knowing
nande | なんで | why
hairou | 入ろう | (I'll) join ("hairu", volitional)
to omotta | と思った | thought

> Asa kara genki da nee. | 朝から元気だねえ。 | You're lively first thing in the morning.
asa kara | 朝から | from the morning
genki | 元気 | lively, energetic
~da nee | だねえ | aren't you

> Dore dake bokoboko ni shitemo okorarenai shi ne. | どれだけボコボコにしても怒られないしね。 | And you don't get told off no matter how badly you beat them up.
dore dake | どれだけ | however much
bokoboko ni shitemo | ボコボコにしても | even if you beat (them) to a pulp
okorarenai | 怒られない | don't get scolded (passive negative of "okoru")
~shi ne | しね | (and besides, you know)

> Kyuuryou de suki na mon katte yareru shi na. | 給料で好きなもん買ってやれるしな。 | And with my salary I can buy her whatever she likes.
kyuuryou | 給料 | salary
~de | で | with
suki na mon | 好きなもん | things (she) likes
katte yareru | 買ってやれる | can buy (for her)
~shi na | しな | (and besides)
