# Sentences: Episode 5 | Short lines from "The Road to the Wizard King"
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Tekagen nashi da ze. | 手加減なしだぜ。 | No holding back.
tekagen | 手加減 | going easy on someone
nashi | なし | without, none
~da ze | だぜ | it is (rough, masculine)

> Enryo shinaide kakatte kina. | 遠慮しないでかかってきな。 | Don't hold back — come at me.
enryo shinaide | 遠慮しないで | without holding back
kakatte kina | かかってきな | come at me (casual command)

> Are wo yaburu no wa muri da. | あれを破るのはムリだ。 | Breaking through that is impossible.
are | あれ | that
~wo | を | (marks the object)
yaburu | 破る | to break through
~no wa | のは | ("…-ing" as the topic)
muri | ムリ | impossible
~da | だ | is

> Ore mo aitsu to tatakaitakatta na. | 俺もあいつと戦いたかったな。 | I wanted to fight him too.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~mo | も | too
aitsu | あいつ | him, that guy
~to | と | with
tatakaitakatta | 戦いたかった | wanted to fight (past of "tatakaitai")
~na | な | (musing)

> Omae nanka doko no dan ni mo hairenee yo. | お前なんかどこの団にも入れねえよ。 | Someone like you won't get into any squad.
omae | お前 | you (rough)
nanka | なんか | someone like (belittling)
doko no | どこの | which, any
dan | 団 | squad
~ni mo | にも | into (any at all)
hairenee | 入れねえ | can't get in (rough "hairenai")
~yo | よ | (asserting)

> Kore wa tada shintai nouryoku ga takai dake da. | これはただ身体能力が高いだけだ。 | This is simply high physical ability.
kore | これ | this
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
tada | ただ | just, simply
shintai nouryoku | 身体能力 | physical ability
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
takai | 高い | high
dake | だけ | only
~da | だ | is

> Omae ni katsu no wa ore da. | お前に勝つのは俺だ。 | The one who'll beat you is me.
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~ni | に | against
katsu | 勝つ | to win, beat
~no wa | のは | "the one who…"
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~da | だ | is

> Monku aru ka? | 文句あるか？ | Got a problem with that?
monku | 文句 | complaint
aru | ある | to have, there is
~ka | か | (question: "…?")

> Souiya zenzen ma wo kanjinakatta na. | そういや全然魔を感じなかったな。 | Come to think of it, I sensed no magic at all.
souiya | そういや | come to think of it
zenzen | 全然 | not at all
ma | 魔 | magic
~wo | を | (marks the object)
kanjinakatta | 感じなかった | didn't sense (negative past of "kanjiru")
~na | な | (musing)

> Myou na no ga arawareyagatta naa. | 妙なのが現れやがったなぁ。 | Now a strange one has shown up.
myou na no | 妙なの | a strange one
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
arawareyagatta | 現れやがった | has gone and shown up ("arawareru" + rough "-yagaru")
~naa | なぁ | (musing)

> Yappari kizoku ga tsuyoi yo na. | やっぱり貴族が強いよな。 | Nobles really are strong, huh.
yappari | やっぱり | as expected, after all
kizoku | 貴族 | noble
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
tsuyoi | 強い | strong
~yo na | よな | aren't they

> Aite ga yudan shisugi dattanda yo. | 相手が油断しすぎだったんだよ。 | His opponent was just far too careless.
aite | 相手 | opponent
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
yudan | 油断 | carelessness, lowering one's guard
shisugi | しすぎ | doing too much
~dattanda yo | だったんだよ | it was (explaining)

> Boku ga aite wo shite ageyou ka? | 僕が相手をしてあげようか？ | Shall I be your opponent?
boku | 僕 | I (soft, boyish)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
aite wo shite | 相手をして | be (your) opponent
ageyou ka | あげようか | shall I (do it for you)?
