# Sentences: Episode 10 | Short lines from "Those Who Protect"
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Murabito wo mamoru no de te ippai… | 村人を守るので手いっぱい… | I've got my hands full protecting the villagers…
murabito | 村人 | villagers
~wo | を | (marks the object)
mamoru | 守る | to protect
~no de | ので | with (doing that)
te ippai | 手いっぱい | hands full

> Ore no maryoku wa mou tsukikaketeru. | 俺の魔力はもう尽きかけてる。 | My magic is nearly used up.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
maryoku | 魔力 | magic power
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
mou | もう | already
tsukikaketeru | 尽きかけてる | is about to run out ("tsukiru" + "kakeru")

> Dakara to itte nagedashicha otoko ja nee! | だからといって投げ出しちゃ漢じゃねえ！ | But giving up because of that wouldn't be manly!
dakara to itte | だからといって | even so, just because of that
nagedashicha | 投げ出しちゃ | if (I) give up — casual "nagedashite wa"
otoko | 漢 | a (real) man
ja nee | じゃねえ | is not (rough)

> Shikkari mamotte yaru kara na! | しっかり守ってやるからな！ | I'll protect you properly!
shikkari | しっかり | properly, firmly
mamotte yaru | 守ってやる | I'll protect (you)
~kara na | からな | (emphatic "so there!")

> Kono naka de ichiban maryoku wo motteru noni… | この中でいちばん魔力を持ってるのに… | Even though I have the most magic of anyone here…
kono naka de | この中で | among these (people)
ichiban | いちばん | the most
maryoku | 魔力 | magic power
~wo | を | (marks the object)
motteru | 持ってる | have (short for "motte iru")
noni | のに | even though

> Kanpeki ni ashidematoi ja nai! | 完璧に足手まといじゃない！ | I'm nothing but dead weight!
kanpeki ni | 完璧に | completely
ashidematoi | 足手まとい | a burden, dead weight
~ja nai | じゃない | aren't I! (exclaiming)

> Watashi ga mamoru no yo! | 私が守るのよ！ | I'm the one who'll protect her!
watashi | 私 | I (polite)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
mamoru | 守る | to protect
~no yo | のよ | (determined, feminine)

> Nigeru wake ni wa ikanai! | 逃げるわけにはいかない！ | I can't just run away!
nigeru | 逃げる | to run away
wake ni wa ikanai | わけにはいかない | can't possibly, mustn't

> Taishita koto nai wa ne. | 大したことないわね。 | That was nothing much.
taishita koto nai | 大したことない | no big deal
~wa ne | わね | was it (feminine)

> Yappari yareba dekin ja nee ka. | やっぱりやればできんじゃねえか。 | See, you can do it when you try.
yappari | やっぱり | just as I thought
yareba | やれば | if (you) try
dekin | できん | can do (casual "dekiru n")
~ja nee ka | じゃねえか | can't you! (rough)

> Ore ni maryoku nante nee! | 俺に魔力なんてねえ！ | I don't have any magic!
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~ni | に | (marks who has it)
maryoku | 魔力 | magic power
nante | なんて | (such a thing as)
nee | ねえ | there is none (rough "nai")

> Kondo wa kochira no ban da. | 今度はこちらの番だ。 | Now it's my turn.
kondo | 今度 | this time, now
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
kochira | こちら | this side, me
~no | の | 's (possessive)
ban | 番 | turn
~da | だ | is

> Daga jikan no mondai. | だが時間の問題。 | But it's only a matter of time.
daga | だが | but
jikan | 時間 | time
~no | の | of
mondai | 問題 | matter, problem

> Sono daishou wa ookii zo. | その代償は大きいぞ。 | The price for that will be high.
sono | その | that
daishou | 代償 | price, cost
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
ookii | 大きい | big
~zo | ぞ | (threatening)

> Aite ga warukatta na… | 相手が悪かったな… | You picked the wrong opponent…
aite | 相手 | opponent
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
warukatta | 悪かった | was bad (past of "warui")
~na | な | huh

> Yoku mada ikite ita na. | よくまだ生きていたな。 | I'm surprised you're still alive.
yoku | よく | (surprise: "how on earth")
mada | まだ | still
ikite ita | 生きていた | were alive
~na | な | huh
