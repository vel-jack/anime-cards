# Sentences: Episode 4 | Short lines from "The Magic Knights Entrance Exam"
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Ore-tachi no mura to wa oochigai da na. | 俺たちの村とは大違いだな。 | It's totally different from our village.
ore-tachi | 俺たち | we, us (masculine)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
mura | 村 | village
~to wa | とは | compared with
oochigai | 大違い | a huge difference
~da na | だな | it is, huh

> Asoko ni Mahoutei ga iru no ka? | あそこに魔法帝がいるのか？ | Is the Wizard King in there?
asoko | あそこ | over there
~ni | に | in, at
Mahoutei | 魔法帝 | Wizard King
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
iru | いる | to be (there)
~no ka | のか | (wondering question)

> Itsuka asoko ga ore no shiro ni naru tte wake da. | いつかあそこが俺の城になるってわけだ。 | So someday, that'll be my castle.
itsuka | いつか | someday
asoko | あそこ | over there, that place
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
shiro | 城 | castle
~ni | に | (marks what it becomes)
naru | なる | to become
tte wake da | ってわけだ | "so that means…"

> Sore wa kocchi no serifu da. | それはこっちのセリフだ。 | That's my line.
sore | それ | that
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
kocchi | こっち | this side, me
~no | の | 's (possessive)
serifu | セリフ | line (of dialogue)
~da | だ | is

> Hayaku kaette omise no junbi suru wa yo. | 早く帰ってお店の準備するわよ。 | Let's hurry home and get the shop ready.
hayaku | 早く | quickly
kaette | 帰って | go home and… (te-form of "kaeru")
omise | お店 | shop
~no | の | 's, of
junbi suru | 準備する | to prepare
~wa yo | わよ | (feminine, firm)

> Kyoukai no minna wo omoidasu na. | 教会のみんなを思い出すな。 | This reminds me of everyone at the church.
kyoukai | 教会 | church
~no | の | 's, of
minna | みんな | everyone
~wo | を | (marks the object)
omoidasu | 思い出す | to remember, be reminded of
~na | な | (musing)

> Sorosoro outo ni tsuita koro yo ne. | そろそろ王都に着いたころよね。 | They should have reached the capital about now.
sorosoro | そろそろ | about now, soon
outo | 王都 | the royal capital
~ni | に | at, to
tsuita | 着いた | arrived (past of "tsuku")
koro | ころ | about the time
~yo ne | よね | right?

> Umaku iku to ii kedo. | うまくいくといいけど。 | I hope it goes well.
umaku iku | うまくいく | to go well
to ii | といい | I hope (lit. "it would be good if")
~kedo | けど | though…

> Nyuudan shiken tte doko de yarunda? | 入団試験ってどこでやるんだ？ | Where's the entrance exam being held?
nyuudan shiken | 入団試験 | (squad) entrance exam
~tte | って | (casual topic marker)
doko | どこ | where
~de | で | at
yarunda | やるんだ | are (they) doing it? ("yaru" + "n da")

> Oukoku no mirai wa omae-tachi ni kakatteru! | 王国の未来はお前たちに懸かってる！ | The kingdom's future depends on you!
oukoku | 王国 | kingdom
~no | の | 's (possessive)
mirai | 未来 | future
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
omae-tachi | お前たち | you (plural, rough)
~ni | に | on
kakatteru | 懸かってる | depends on, rides on (short for "kakatte iru")

> Zuibun yogoreteru na. | 随分汚れてるな。 | It's awfully dirty.
zuibun | 随分 | awfully, quite
yogoreteru | 汚れてる | is dirty (short for "yogorete iru")
~na | な | huh

> Kinchou shite kita ze. | 緊張してきたぜ。 | I'm starting to get nervous.
kinchou shite | 緊張して | getting nervous (te-form of "kinchou suru")
kita ze | きたぜ | have started to ("kuru", past, + rough "ze")

> Hantoshi-kan no ore no shugyou no seika, misete yaru kara na. | 半年間の俺の修行の成果、見せてやるからな。 | I'll show you the results of my six months of training.
hantoshi-kan | 半年間 | (for) half a year
~no | の | of
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
shugyou | 修行 | training
~no | の | of
seika | 成果 | results
misete yaru | 見せてやる | I'll show you
~kara na | からな | (emphatic "so there!")
