# Sentences: Episode 1 | Short lines from "Asta and Yuno" — learn the words, then read the line
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Kyoudai ka, soretomo futago ka. | 兄弟か、それとも双子か。 | Brothers... or maybe twins?
kyoudai | 兄弟 | brothers
~ka | か | (question: "…?")
soretomo | それとも | or
futago | 双子 | twins
~ka | か | (question: "…?")

> Kyou kara koko ga omae-tachi no ie da. | 今日からここがお前たちの家だ。 | From today, this is your home.
kyou | 今日 | today
kara | から | from
koko | ここ | here, this place
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
omae-tachi | お前たち | you (plural, rough)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
ie | 家 | home, house
~da | だ | is

> Ore to kekkon shite kudasai! | 俺と結婚してください！ | Please marry me!
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~to | と | with
kekkon shite | 結婚して | marry (te-form of "kekkon suru")
kudasai | ください | please (do)

> Watashi wa minna no shisutaa dakara. | 私はみんなのシスターだから。 | Because I'm everyone's Sister.
watashi | 私 | I (polite)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
minna | みんな | everyone
~no | の | 's (possessive)
shisutaa | シスター | Sister (nun)
dakara | だから | because, so

> Jikan ga mottainai kara. | 時間がもったいないから。 | Because it would be a waste of time.
jikan | 時間 | time
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
mottainai | もったいない | wasteful
kara | から | because

> Kono sekai wa mahou ga subete nan da yo. | この世界は魔法が全てなんだよ。 | In this world, magic is everything.
kono | この | this
sekai | 世界 | world
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
mahou | 魔法 | magic
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
subete | 全て | everything
~nan da yo | なんだよ | (explaining: "you see, it is")

> Yuno wa kono kyoukai no kibou ja! | ユノはこの教会の希望じゃ！ | Yuno is the hope of this church!
~Yuno | ユノ | Yuno (name)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
kono | この | this
kyoukai | 教会 | church
~no | の | 's, of
kibou | 希望 | hope
~ja | じゃ | is (an old man's "da")

> Ore wa Mahoutei ni naru. | 俺は魔法帝になる。 | I'm going to become the Wizard King.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
Mahoutei | 魔法帝 | Wizard King
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
naru | なる | to become

> Kore dake wa yuzurenee! | これだけは譲れねえ！ | This is the one thing I can't give up!
kore | これ | this
dake | だけ | only, just
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
yuzurenee | 譲れねえ | can't give up, can't hand over (rough "yuzurenai")

> Gurimowaaru ga konain su kedo… | グリモワールが来ないんすけど… | Um, my grimoire isn't coming…
gurimowaaru | グリモワール | grimoire
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
konai | 来ない | doesn't come (negative of "kuru")
~n su kedo | んすけど | (casual: "it's that… but", trailing off)

> Sugu oitsuku. Ore wa omae no raibaru dakara na! | すぐ追いつく。俺はお前のライバルだからな！ | I'll catch up soon. Because I'm your rival!
sugu | すぐ | soon, right away
oitsuku | 追いつく | to catch up
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~no | の | 's (possessive)
raibaru | ライバル | rival
dakara na | だからな | because (emphatic)

> Dare ga akirameru ka! | 誰が諦めるか！ | Like hell I'd give up!
dare | 誰 | who
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
akirameru | 諦める | to give up
~ka | か | (rhetorical: "as if anyone would!")

> Gurimowaaru wo tsukaeru no wa, erabareta honnin dake. | グリモワールを使えるのは、選ばれた本人だけ。 | Only the person it chose can use a grimoire.
gurimowaaru | グリモワール | grimoire
~wo | を | (marks the object)
tsukaeru | 使える | can use
~no wa | のは | "the one who…"
erabareta | 選ばれた | was chosen (passive past of "erabu")
honnin | 本人 | the person themself
dake | だけ | only

> Kimi no densetsu wa hajimaru mae ni owari da. | 君の伝説は始まる前に終わりだ。 | Your legend ends before it even begins.
kimi | 君 | you
~no | の | 's (possessive)
densetsu | 伝説 | legend
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
hajimaru | 始まる | to begin
mae ni | 前に | before
owari | 終わり | the end
~da | だ | is

> Omae wa maryoku ga issai nai! | お前は魔力が一切ない！ | You have no magic power whatsoever!
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
maryoku | 魔力 | magic power
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
issai | 一切 | (not) at all
nai | ない | there is none

> Mahoutei ni naru no wa ore dakara da! | 魔法帝になるのは俺だからだ！ | Because the one who'll become Wizard King is me!
Mahoutei | 魔法帝 | Wizard King
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
naru | なる | to become
~no wa | のは | "the one who…"
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
dakara da | だからだ | it's because

> Ima, koitsu wo taosu! | 今、こいつを倒す！ | Right now, I'm taking this guy down!
ima | 今 | now
koitsu | こいつ | this guy
~wo | を | (marks the object)
taosu | 倒す | to defeat, knock down

> Go-mai-me ni wa akuma ga sumu. | 五枚目には悪魔が住む。 | Within the fifth leaf dwells a devil.
go-mai-me | 五枚目 | the fifth (leaf) — "mai" counts flat things, "me" makes it "-th"
~ni wa | には | in (as the topic)
akuma | 悪魔 | devil
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
sumu | 住む | to live, dwell
