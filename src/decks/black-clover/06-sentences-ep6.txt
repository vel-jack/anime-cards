# Sentences: Episode 6 | Short lines from "The Black Bulls"
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Daremo hoshigaranee. | 誰も欲しがらねえ。 | Nobody wants you.
daremo | 誰も | nobody (with a negative)
hoshigaranee | 欲しがらねえ | doesn't want (rough "hoshigaranai")

> Omae, omoshiroi! Uchi no dan ni koi. | お前、面白い！ウチの団に来い。 | You're interesting! Join my squad.
omae | お前 | you (rough)
omoshiroi | 面白い | interesting, funny
uchi no | ウチの | our, my
dan | 団 | squad
~ni | に | to
koi | 来い | come! (command form of "kuru")

> Choushi ni noru na yo. | 調子に乗るなよ。 | Don't get carried away.
choushi ni noru | 調子に乗る | to get cocky, carried away
na | な | don't (after a verb)
~yo | よ | (warning)

> Sou kantan ni mitomerare wa shinai. | そう簡単に認められはしない。 | You won't be accepted that easily.
sou | そう | that, so
kantan ni | 簡単に | easily
mitomerare | 認められ | be accepted (passive stem of "mitomeru")
wa shinai | はしない | will not (emphatic)

> Sugu ni mitomesasete misemasu. | すぐに認めさせてみせます。 | I'll make you acknowledge me soon.
sugu ni | すぐに | soon, right away
mitomesasete | 認めさせて | make (you) acknowledge (causative te-form)
misemasu | みせます | I'll show you (polite)

> Kuchi wa tassha da na. | 口は達者だな。 | You sure can talk.
kuchi | 口 | mouth
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
tassha | 達者 | skilled, glib
~da na | だな | it is, huh

> Kakugo wa ii na? | 覚悟はいいな？ | Are you ready for this?
kakugo | 覚悟 | resolve, readiness
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
ii | いい | good, ready
~na | な | right?

> Nan no sawagi? | 何の騒ぎ？ | What's all this commotion?
nan no | 何の | what (kind of)
sawagi | 騒ぎ | commotion

> Aa, nomisugita. | あ〜飲み過ぎた。 | Ugh, I drank too much.
~aa | あ〜 | ugh
nomisugita | 飲み過ぎた | drank too much ("nomu" + "sugiru", past)

> Kyou kara ore mo Kuro no Bougyuu no nakama. | 今日から俺も黒の暴牛の仲間。 | From today, I'm one of the Black Bulls too.
kyou kara | 今日から | from today
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~mo | も | too
~Kuro no Bougyuu | 黒の暴牛 | the Black Bulls
~no | の | 's (possessive)
nakama | 仲間 | comrade, member

> Yoroshiku onegai shimasu! | よろしくお願いします！ | I look forward to working with you!
yoroshiku | よろしく | favourably, kindly
onegai shimasu | お願いします | I ask of you (polite)

> Imouto ga okitara dou sunda? | 妹が起きたらどうすんだ？ | What will you do if my little sister wakes up?
imouto | 妹 | younger sister
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
okitara | 起きたら | if (she) wakes up
dou sunda | どうすんだ | what will you do (rough "dou suru n da")

> Yosomi shiteru to yacchau yo. | よそ見してるとやっちゃうよ。 | Look away and I'll get you.
yosomi shiteru | よそ見してる | looking away
~to | と | if, when
yacchau | やっちゃう | I'll do you in ("yaru" + "-chau")
~yo | よ | (warning)

> Yareru mon nara yatte miyagare! | やれるもんならやってみやがれ！ | Just try it, if you think you can!
yareru mon nara | やれるもんなら | if you can do it
yatte miyagare | やってみやがれ | go on and try (rough command)

> Mono wo kowasun ja nee! | 物を壊すんじゃねえ！ | Stop breaking things!
mono | 物 | things
~wo | を | (marks the object)
kowasu | 壊す | to break
~n ja nee | んじゃねえ | don't! (rough)
