# Sentences: Episode 9 | Short lines from "Beasts"
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Nani? Sono dasai ninmu. | なに？そのダサイ任務。 | What? That lame mission.
nani | なに | what
sono | その | that
dasai | ダサイ | lame, uncool
ninmu | 任務 | mission

> Iku no ka shinu no ka docchi da? | 行くのか死ぬのかどっちだ？ | Are you going, or dying — which is it?
iku | 行く | to go
~no ka | のか | (or… — offering a choice)
shinu | 死ぬ | to die
~no ka | のか | (or… — offering a choice)
docchi | どっち | which (of two)
~da | だ | is it

> Daremo monku nante itte nai wa. | 誰も文句なんて言ってないわ。 | Nobody said a word of complaint.
daremo | 誰も | nobody (with a negative)
monku | 文句 | complaint
nante | なんて | (such a thing as)
itte nai | 言ってない | hasn't said (short for "itte inai")
~wa | わ | (feminine)

> Watashi ga itte mo ii no ka na tte… | 私が行ってもいいのかなって… | I just wondered if it's all right for me to go…
watashi | 私 | I (polite)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
itte mo ii | 行ってもいい | okay to go
~no ka na | のかな | I wonder
~tte | って | (…is what I thought)

> Otoko ni nigon wa nee! | 漢に二言はねえ！ | A real man never goes back on his word!
otoko | 漢 | a (real) man
~ni | に | for, in
nigon | 二言 | going back on one's word
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
nee | ねえ | there is none (rough "nai")

> Shinitakunakattara motto kuttsuke! | 死にたくなかったらもっとくっつけ！ | If you don't want to die, hold on tighter!
shinitakunakattara | 死にたくなかったら | if you don't want to die
motto | もっと | more
kuttsuke | くっつけ | stick close! (command form of "kuttsuku")

> Koko ni aru no ka. | ここにあるのか。 | So it's here.
koko ni | ここに | here
aru | ある | to be, exist
~no ka | のか | (realising)

> Are, tasukenakute ii no? | あれ、助けなくていいの？ | Is it okay not to help him?
are | あれ | that (him over there)
tasukenakute ii | 助けなくていい | okay not to help
~no | の | (soft question)

> Ore wa chotto kyuukeichuu da. | 俺はちょっと休憩中だ。 | I'm taking a little break.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
chotto | ちょっと | a little
kyuukeichuu | 休憩中 | on a break
~da | だ | is

> Daijoubu darou. Aitsu wa ganjou dakara. | 大丈夫だろう。あいつは頑丈だから。 | He'll be fine. He's tough.
daijoubu | 大丈夫 | fine, all right
darou | だろう | probably
aitsu | あいつ | he, that guy
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
ganjou | 頑丈 | sturdy, tough
dakara | だから | because

> Shinpai nara omae ga tasukete yare. | 心配ならお前が助けてやれ。 | If you're worried, you go help him.
shinpai nara | 心配なら | if (you're) worried
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
tasukete yare | 助けてやれ | help (him)! (command)

> Yatto shitometa ze! | やっとしとめたぜ！ | Finally took it down!
yatto | やっと | finally
shitometa | しとめた | brought down (prey) — past of "shitomeru"
~ze | ぜ | (rough, masculine)

> Yoku yatta. Daga mada owattenee zo. | よくやった。だがまだ終わってねえぞ。 | Well done. But it's not over yet.
yoku yatta | よくやった | well done
daga | だが | but
mada | まだ | (not) yet
owattenee | 終わってねえ | isn't over (rough "owatte inai")
~zo | ぞ | (emphatic)

> Shiriai na no? | 知り合いなの？ | Do you know him?
shiriai | 知り合い | acquaintance
~na no | なの | is he? (soft question)

> Mukashi kara sewa ni nattete na. | 昔から世話になっててな。 | He's looked after me since way back.
mukashi kara | 昔から | since long ago
sewa ni nattete | 世話になってて | being looked after (by him)
~na | な | you see

> Itai me ni au no wa omae ja! | 痛い目に遭うのはお前じゃ！ | The one who's going to get hurt is you!
itai me ni au | 痛い目に遭う | to have a painful experience
~no wa | のは | "the one who…"
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~ja | じゃ | is (an old man's "da")
