# Sentences: Episode 21 | Short lines from Against Corps Rules
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Nani yatte iru no? Soto ni dete wa dame desho? | 何やっているの？外に出てはダメでしょ？ | What are you doing? You're not supposed to go outside.
nani | 何 | what
yatte iru | やっている | are doing (yaru + te iru)
~no | の | of; links nouns
soto | 外 | outside
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
dete | 出て | go out (te-form of deru)
~wa | は | topic marker
dame | ダメ | not allowed, no good
~desho | でしょ | right? (shortened deshou)

> Ore no oya wa ore wo korosou to shita. | 俺の親は俺を殺そうとした。 | My parents tried to kill me.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~no | の | of; links nouns
oya | 親 | parent
~wa | は | topic marker
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wo | を | object marker
korosou | 殺そう | kill (volitional of korosu)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
shita | した | did (past of suru)

> Sono shunkan toutotsu ni rikai shita. | その瞬間唐突に理解した。 | At that moment, I suddenly understood.
sono | その | that (before a noun)
shunkan | 瞬間 | moment
toutotsu ni | 唐突に | suddenly, unexpectedly
rikai shita | 理解した | understood (past of rikai suru)

> Mainichi mainichi chichi to haha ga koishikute tamaranakatta. | 毎日毎日父と母が恋しくてたまらなかった。 | Every day, I missed my father and mother unbearably.
mainichi | 毎日 | every day
mainichi | 毎日 | every day
chichi | 父 | father (one's own)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
haha | 母 | mother (one's own)
~ga | が | subject marker
koishikute | 恋しくて | longing for (te-form of koishii)
tamaranakatta | たまらなかった | could not bear it (past negative of tamaru)

> Dare mo ore wo mamorenai kabaenai. | 誰も俺を守れないかばえない。 | No one can protect me or shield me.
dare mo | 誰も | anyone (with a negative: no one)
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wo | を | object marker
mamorenai | 守れない | cannot protect (negative potential of mamoru)
kabaenai | かばえない | cannot shield (negative potential of kabau)

> Omoidashita hakkiri to. | 思い出したはっきりと。 | I remember now, clearly.
omoidashita | 思い出した | remembered (past of omoidasu)
hakkiri to | はっきりと | clearly

> Tousan to kaasan to onaji tokoro e wa ikenai yo ne? | 父さんと母さんと同じ所へは行けないよね？ | I can't go to the same place as Father and Mother, can I?
tousan | 父さん | Dad, father
~to | と | with; quotation marker
kaasan | 母さん | Mom, mother
~to | と | with; quotation marker
onaji | 同じ | same
tokoro | 所 | place
~e | へ | toward
~wa | は | topic marker
ikenai | 行けない | cannot go (negative potential of iku)
~yo ne | よね | seeks confirmation

> Hito wo kutta oni ni nasake wo kakeru na. | 人を食った鬼に情けをかけるな。 | Don't show mercy to a demon that ate people.
hito | 人 | person, people
~wo | を | object marker
kutta | 食った | ate (past of rough kuu)
oni | 鬼 | demon
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
nasake | 情け | mercy, compassion
~wo | を | object marker
kakeru na | かけるな | don't show (kakeru + prohibitive na)

> Minikui bakemono nanka ja nai. | 醜い化け物なんかじゃない。 | They aren't ugly monsters.
minikui | 醜い | ugly
bakemono | 化け物 | monster
~nanka | なんか | something like (dismissive)
~ja nai | じゃない | is not (plain negative)

> Kirawarete iru jikaku ga nakatta n desu ne. | 嫌われている自覚がなかったんですね。 | You hadn't realized that people dislike you.
kirawarete iru | 嫌われている | are disliked (kirau, passive + te iru)
jikaku | 自覚 | awareness
~ga | が | subject marker
~nakatta | なかった | was not (past of nai)
~n desu ne | んですね | polite explanatory observation

> Ore wa Kisatsutai wo nukenakereba naranaku naru no ka? | 俺は鬼殺隊を抜けなければならなくなるのか？ | Does this mean I'll have to leave the Corps?
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
~Kisatsutai | 鬼殺隊 | Demon Slayer Corps (organization)
~wo | を | object marker
nukenakereba naranaku naru | 抜けなければならなくなる | will come to have to leave (nukeru + obligation + naru)
~no ka | のか | explanatory question

> Kangaeru hitsuyou wa nai iwareta toori ni oni wo kiru dake. | 考える必要はない言われたとおりに鬼を斬るだけ。 | You don't need to think. Just cut down demons as instructed.
kangaeru | 考える | to think
hitsuyou | 必要 | necessary
~wa | は | topic marker
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist
iwareta | 言われた | were told (past passive of iu)
toori | とおり | as, in the manner
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
oni | 鬼 | demon
~wo | を | object marker
kiru | 斬る | to cut with a blade
~dake | だけ | only

> Riyuu kurai hanashite kudasai. | 理由くらい話してください。 | At least explain your reason.
riyuu | 理由 | reason
~kurai | くらい | at least
hanashite | 話して | tell (te-form of hanasu)
kudasai | ください | please do (request form of kudasaru)

> Ore tte anmashi tsuyokunai no kamo. | 俺ってあんまし強くないのかも。 | Maybe I'm not all that strong.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~tte | って | informal quotation marker
anmashi | あんまし | very much (informal amari, with a negative)
tsuyokunai | 強くない | not strong (negative of tsuyoi)
~no kamo | のかも | maybe (shortened no kamo shirenai)

> Fukin no oni wa watashi ga karu kara anshin shite sagyou shite. | 付近の鬼は私が狩るから安心して作業して。 | I'll hunt nearby demons, so you can work without worry.

~ga | が | subject marker
karu | 狩る | to hunt
~kara | から | because; from

sagyou shite | 作業して | work (te-form of sagyou suru)
