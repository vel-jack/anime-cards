# Sentences: Episode 20 | Short lines from Pretend Family
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Ore wa mada tatakawanakereba naranai. | 俺はまだ戦わなければならない。 | I still have to fight.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
mada | まだ | still, yet
tatakawanakereba naranai | 戦わなければならない | must fight (tatakau + negative conditional obligation)

> Sou ieba ano hai no you na nioi ga shinakatta. | そういえばあの灰のようなにおいがしなかった。 | Come to think of it, there wasn't that ash-like smell.
sou ieba | そういえば | come to think of it
ano | あの | that over there (before a noun)
hai | 灰 | ash
~no | の | of; links nouns
~you na | ような | like, such that
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~ga | が | subject marker
shinakatta | しなかった | did not make (past negative of suru)

> Boku wa jibun no ito de kubi wo kitta n da yo. | 僕は自分の糸で首を切ったんだよ。 | I cut my own neck with my threads.
boku | 僕 | I, me (often masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
jibun | 自分 | oneself
~no | の | of; links nouns
ito | 糸 | thread
~de | で | at, with; means marker
kubi | 首 | neck, head
~wo | を | object marker
kitta | 切った | cut (past of kiru)
~n da yo | んだよ | emphatic explanation

> Konna ni hara ga tatta no wa hisashiburi da yo. | こんなに腹が立ったのは久しぶりだよ。 | I haven't been this angry in a long time.
konna ni | こんなに | this much
hara | 腹 | belly (hara ga tatsu: get angry)
~ga | が | subject marker
tatta | 立った | rose (past of tatsu, here became angry)
~no | の | nominalizer: turns a clause into a noun
~wa | は | topic marker
hisashiburi | 久しぶり | first time in a long while
~da yo | だよ | emphatic plain ending

> Ippon mo todokanakatta no ka? | １本も届かなかったのか？ | Not a single one reached him?
ippon | １本 | one long thin object
~mo | も | also, even
todokanakatta | 届かなかった | did not reach (past negative of todoku)
~no ka | のか | explanatory question

> Shikashi mitame ga kodomo darou to oni ni wa kawaran. | しかし見た目が子供だろうと鬼には変わらん。 | But even if it looks like a child, it's still a demon.
shikashi | しかし | however
mitame | 見た目 | appearance
~ga | が | subject marker
kodomo | 子供 | child
~darou to | だろうと | even if it is
oni | 鬼 | demon
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~wa | は | topic marker
kawaran | 変わらん | does not change (contracted kawaranai, from kawaru)

> Jaa boku no kazoku ni natte yo. | じゃあ僕の家族になってよ。 | Then become part of my family.
jaa | じゃあ | then, in that case
boku | 僕 | I, me (often masculine)
~no | の | of; links nouns
kazoku | 家族 | family
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
natte | なって | become (te-form of naru)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Watashitachi kazoku no nouryoku wa zenbu Rui no mono. | 私たち家族の能力は全部累のもの。 | All our family's abilities belong to Rui.
watashitachi | 私たち | we
kazoku | 家族 | family
~no | の | of; links nouns
nouryoku | 能力 | ability
~wa | は | topic marker
zenbu | 全部 | all, everything
~Rui | 累 | Rui (name)
~no | の | of; links nouns
mono | もの | thing, possession

> Gomennasai jouzu ni dekinakute. | ごめんなさい上手にできなくて。 | I'm sorry I couldn't do it well.
gomennasai | ごめんなさい | I'm sorry
jouzu ni | 上手に | skillfully, well
dekinakute | できなくて | unable to do (negative te-form of dekiru)

> Kikanakatta koto ni suru wa watashi. | 聞かなかったことにするわ私。 | I'll pretend I didn't hear that.
kikanakatta | 聞かなかった | did not hear (past negative of kiku)
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
suru | する | to do
~wa | わ | softening sentence ending
watashi | 私 | I, me

> Aitsura wa baka da kedo watashi wa chigau. | あいつらはバカだけど私は違う。 | Whereas they're fools, I'm different.
aitsura | あいつら | those people (rough)
~wa | は | topic marker
baka | バカ | fool
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
~kedo | けど | but, although
watashi | 私 | I, me
~wa | は | topic marker
chigau | 違う | to differ

> Te no hira kara ito wo dashite iru n desu ka? | 手のひらから糸を出しているんですか？ | Are you producing threads from your palms?
te no hira | 手のひら | palm of the hand
~kara | から | because; from
ito | 糸 | thread
~wo | を | object marker
dashite iru | 出している | are putting out (dasu + te iru)
~n desu ka | んですか | polite explanatory question

> Watashi wa muriyari shitagawasareteru no. | 私は無理やり従わされてるの。 | I'm being forced to obey.
watashi | 私 | I, me
~wa | は | topic marker
muriyari | 無理やり | by force
shitagawasareteru | 従わされてる | am being forced to obey (contracted causative passive shitagawasarete iru, from shitagau)
~no | の | question or explanatory ending

> Anata wa nannin koroshimashita ka? | あなたは何人殺しましたか？ | How many people have you killed?
anata | あなた | you
~wa | は | topic marker
nannin | 何人 | how many people
koroshimashita | 殺しました | killed (polite past of korosu)
~ka | か | question marker

> Korosareta hito ga mukuwaremasen. | 殺された人が報われません。 | The people who were killed won't have justice.
korosareta | 殺された | were killed (past passive of korosu)
hito | 人 | person, people
~ga | が | subject marker
mukuwaremasen | 報われません | will not be rewarded (polite negative of mukuwareru)
