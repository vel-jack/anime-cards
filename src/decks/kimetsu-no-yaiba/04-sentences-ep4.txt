# Sentences: Episode 4 | Short lines from Final Selection
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Ore no yaiba wa tsuyoku hikarete suki wo kirikomu. | 俺の刃は強く引かれて隙を斬り込む。 | My blade is pulled hard toward the opening and cuts into it.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~no | の | of; links nouns
yaiba | 刃 | blade
~wa | は | topic marker
tsuyoku | 強く | strongly (adverbial form of tsuyoi)
hikarete | 引かれて | being pulled (te-form of passive hikareru, from hiku)
suki | 隙 | opening, gap in defense
~wo | を | object marker
kirikomu | 斬り込む | to cut into

> Washi mo imouto mo koko de matte iru. | わしも妹もここで待っている。 | My sister and I will be waiting here.
washi | わし | I (elderly speech)
~mo | も | also, even
imouto | 妹 | younger sister
~mo | も | also, even
koko | ここ | here
~de | で | at, with; means marker
matte iru | 待っている | are waiting (matsu + te iru)

> Tsuraku kibishii hibi ga matte iru. | つらく厳しい日々が待っている。 | Difficult, harsh days await you.
tsuraku | つらく | painful (linking form of tsurai)
kibishii | 厳しい | harsh, strict
hibi | 日々 | days, daily life
~ga | が | subject marker
matte iru | 待っている | are waiting (matsu + te iru)

> Takusan tabetara tsuyoku naru n desu ka? | たくさん食べたら強くなるんですか？ | Will eating a lot make me stronger?
takusan | たくさん | a lot
tabetara | 食べたら | if I eat (conditional of taberu)
tsuyoku | 強く | strongly (adverbial form of tsuyoi)
naru | なる | to become
~n desu ka | んですか | polite explanatory question

> Imouto no koto wa shinpai suru na. | 妹のことは心配するな。 | Don't worry about your sister.
imouto | 妹 | younger sister
~no | の | of; links nouns
koto | こと | situation, thing
~wa | は | topic marker
shinpai suru na | 心配するな | don't worry (shinpai suru + prohibitive na)

> Saku jiki ja nai hazu na noni. | 咲く時期じゃないはずなのに。 | Even though it shouldn't be their blooming season.
saku | 咲く | to bloom
jiki | 時期 | season, time
~ja nai | じゃない | is not (plain negative)
hazu | はず | expectation, should be
~na noni | なのに | even though

> Mazu wa kono yoru wo norikiru. | まずはこの夜を乗り切る。 | First I need to make it through tonight.
mazu | まず | first of all
~wa | は | topic marker
kono | この | this (before a noun)
yoru | 夜 | night
~wo | を | object marker
norikiru | 乗り切る | to get through, overcome

> Mottomo hayaku asahi no ataru basho wo mezasu! | 最も早く朝日の当たる場所を目指す！ | I'll aim for the place that gets sunlight first!
mottomo | 最も | most
hayaku | 早く | early, quickly (adverbial form of hayai)
asahi | 朝日 | morning sun
~no | の | of; links nouns
ataru | 当たる | to strike, fall on
basho | 場所 | place
~wo | を | object marker
mezasu | 目指す | to aim for

> Nioi no houkou ga sadamaranai. | においの方向が定まらない。 | I can't pin down the direction of the scent.
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~no | の | of; links nouns
houkou | 方向 | direction
~ga | が | subject marker
sadamaranai | 定まらない | will not settle (negative of sadamaru)

> Tanren wa muda ja nakatta. | 鍛錬はムダじゃなかった。 | The training wasn't wasted.
tanren | 鍛錬 | intensive training
~wa | は | topic marker
muda | ムダ | waste, uselessness
~ja nakatta | じゃなかった | was not (past negative ending)

> Jaa dou yatte taoseba… | じゃあどうやって倒せば… | Then how am I supposed to defeat it?
jaa | じゃあ | then, in that case
dou yatte | どうやって | how, by what means
taoseba | 倒せば | if I defeat it (conditional of taosu)

> Mata ore ga konna tokoro ni tojikomerarete iru aida ni! | また俺がこんな所に閉じ込められている間に！ | Again, while I've been locked away in this place!
mata | また | again
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ga | が | subject marker
konna | こんな | this kind of
tokoro | 所 | place
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
tojikomerarete iru | 閉じ込められている | am locked up (tojikomeru, passive + te iru)
aida | 間 | while, interval
~ni | に | to, at; target marker

> Te wo kitteru dake ja ore wa taosenai zo. | 手を斬ってるだけじゃ俺は倒せないぞ。 | You can't defeat me just by cutting off my arms.
te | 手 | hand
~wo | を | object marker
kitteru | 斬ってる | cutting (contracted kitte iru, from kiru)
~dake | だけ | only
~ja | じゃ | if it is only (contracted de wa)
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
taosenai | 倒せない | cannot defeat (negative potential of taosu)
~zo | ぞ | forceful emphasis

> Demo na kuuchuu de wa kono kougeki wo kawasenai! | でもな空中ではこの攻撃をかわせない！ | But you can't dodge this attack in midair!
~demo | でも | even; but
~na | な | emphasis; prohibition after a verb
kuuchuu | 空中 | midair
~de | で | at, with; means marker
~wa | は | topic marker
kono | この | this (before a noun)
kougeki | 攻撃 | attack
~wo | を | object marker
kawasenai | かわせない | cannot dodge (negative potential of kawasu)

> Makeru kamo shirenai shi katsu kamo shirenai. | 負けるかもしれないし勝つかもしれない。 | You might lose, or you might win.
makeru | 負ける | to lose
~kamo shirenai | かもしれない | might, may
~shi | し | and; gives a reason
katsu | 勝つ | to win
~kamo shirenai | かもしれない | might, may
