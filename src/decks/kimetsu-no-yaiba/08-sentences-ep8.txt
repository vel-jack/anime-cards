# Sentences: Episode 8 | Short lines from The Smell of Enchanting Blood
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Okusan no kizu wa chimeishou ja nai. | 奥さんの傷は致命傷じゃない。 | His wife's wound isn't fatal.
okusan | 奥さん | wife (respectful)
~no | の | of; links nouns
kizu | 傷 | wound
~wa | は | topic marker
chimeishou | 致命傷 | fatal wound
~ja nai | じゃない | is not (plain negative)

> Kisamara nani wo shite iru! | 貴様ら何をしている！ | What are you people doing?!
kisamara | 貴様ら | you lot (hostile)
nani | 何 | what
~wo | を | object marker
shite iru | している | are doing (suru + te iru)

> Naraba watashi mo anata wo tedasuke shimashou. | ならば私もあなたを手助けしましょう。 | In that case, I'll help you too.
~naraba | ならば | if that is so
watashi | 私 | I, me
~mo | も | also, even
anata | あなた | you
~wo | を | object marker
tedasuke shimashou | 手助けしましょう | let me help (polite volitional of tedasuke suru)

> Otousan hayaku kaette kite ne. | お父さん早く帰ってきてね。 | Dad, come home soon.
otousan | お父さん | Dad, father
hayaku | 早く | early, quickly (adverbial form of hayai)
kaette kite | 帰ってきて | come back home (kaeru + te kuru, request)
~ne | ね | seeks agreement

> Nantoka ie yo. Shinjimatta ka? | なんとか言えよ。死んじまったか？ | Say something. Are you dead?
nantoka | なんとか | something, somehow
ie | 言え | say it! (command of iu)
~yo | よ | emphasis
shinjimatta | 死んじまった | ended up dying (shinu + rough contracted te shimatta)
~ka | か | question marker

> Shinisou ni mieru ka? | 死にそうに見えるか？ | Do I look like I'm dying?
shinisou | 死にそう | looks about to die (shinu + sou)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
mieru | 見える | to look, appear
~ka | か | question marker

> Ore wa nioi wo tadoremasu. | 俺はにおいをたどれます。 | I can follow scents.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~wo | を | object marker
tadoremasu | たどれます | can follow (polite potential of tadoru)

> Kono kuchikase no sei kamo shirenai. | この口枷のせいかもしれない。 | It might be because of this muzzle.
kono | この | this (before a noun)
kuchikase | 口枷 | muzzle, gag
~no | の | of; links nouns
sei | せい | fault, cause of something bad
~kamo shirenai | かもしれない | might, may

> Ii ka ano kata ni shitsurei no nai you ni shiro yo. | いいかあの方に失礼のないようにしろよ。 | Make sure you show that person proper respect.
~ii ka | いいか | listen, understood?
ano | あの | that over there (before a noun)
kata | 方 | person (respectful)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
shitsurei | 失礼 | rudeness
~no | の | of; links nouns
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist
~you ni | ように | so that
shiro | しろ | do it! (command of suru)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Kamaimasen yo. Raku ni shite kudasai. | かまいませんよ。楽にしてください。 | It's all right. Please make yourself comfortable.
kamaimasen | かまいません | it does not matter (polite negative of kamau)
~yo | よ | emphasis
raku ni | 楽に | at ease
shite | して | doing (te-form of suru)
kudasai | ください | please do (request form of kudasaru)

> Demo yappari hito no chi wa hitsuyou… | でもやっぱり人の血は必要… | But we still need human blood.
~demo | でも | even; but
yappari | やっぱり | after all, as expected
hito | 人 | person, people
~no | の | of; links nouns
chi | 血 | blood
~wa | は | topic marker
hitsuyou | 必要 | necessary

> Watashi wa oni wo fuyasou to wa shite imasen. | 私は鬼を増やそうとはしていません。 | I am not trying to create more demons.
watashi | 私 | I, me
~wa | は | topic marker
oni | 鬼 | demon
~wo | を | object marker
fuyasou | 増やそう | increase (volitional of fuyasu)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
~wa | は | topic marker
shite imasen | していません | am not doing (polite negative of suru + te iru)

> Uso itsuwari no nai kiyoraka na nioi ga suru. | うそ偽りのない清らかなにおいがする。 | I smell honesty and purity, without any deceit.
uso itsuwari | うそ偽り | lies and deception
~no | の | of; links nouns
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist
kiyoraka na | 清らかな | pure (kiyoraka + na)
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~ga | が | subject marker
suru | する | to do

> Donna kizu ni mo yamai ni mo kanarazu kusuri ya chiryouhou ga aru no desu. | どんな傷にも病にも必ず薬や治療法があるのです。 | Every wound and illness has medicine or a treatment.
donna | どんな | what kind of, any
kizu | 傷 | wound
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~mo | も | also, even
yamai | 病 | illness
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~mo | も | also, even
kanarazu | 必ず | without fail
kusuri | 薬 | medicine
~ya | や | and, among other things
chiryouhou | 治療法 | treatment method
~ga | が | subject marker
aru | ある | to exist, be present
~no desu | のです | polite explanation

> Shikashi odoroku beki koto ni Nezuko-san ni wa sono shoujou ga nai. | しかし驚くべきことに禰豆子さんにはその症状がない。 | But surprisingly, Nezuko has none of those symptoms.
shikashi | しかし | however
odoroku beki | 驚くべき | surprising (odoroku + beki)
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~Nezuko-san | 禰豆子さん | Nezuko (name, respectful)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~wa | は | topic marker
sono | その | that (before a noun)
shoujou | 症状 | symptoms
~ga | が | subject marker
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist
