# Sentences: Episode 23 | Short lines from Hashira Meeting
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Ningen wa mina omae no kazoku da. | 人間は皆お前の家族だ。 | Humans are all your family.
ningen | 人間 | human, human being
~wa | は | topic marker
mina | 皆 | everyone, all
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~no | の | of; links nouns
kazoku | 家族 | family
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Shoumei shinakereba naranai kore kara. | 証明しなければならないこれから。 | You must prove it from now on.
shoumei shinakereba naranai | 証明しなければならない | must prove (shoumei suru + negative conditional obligation)
kore kara | これから | from now on

> Juuni Kizuki wo taoshite oide. | 十二鬼月を倒しておいで。 | Go and defeat one of the Twelve Kizuki.
~Juuni Kizuki | 十二鬼月 | Twelve Kizuki (group name)
~wo | を | object marker
taoshite | 倒して | defeat (te-form of taosu)
oide | おいで | go and do (affectionate command from iku or kuru)

> Kanashimi no rensa wo tachikiru yaiba wo furuu! | 悲しみの連鎖を断ち切る刃を振るう！ | I'll wield a blade that breaks the chain of sorrow!
kanashimi | 悲しみ | sadness
~no | の | of; links nouns
rensa | 連鎖 | chain
~wo | を | object marker
tachikiru | 断ち切る | to sever
yaiba | 刃 | blade
~wo | を | object marker
furuu | 振るう | to wield

> Tanjirou mo kuchi no kikikata ni wa ki wo tsukeru you ni. | 炭治郎も口の利き方には気をつけるように。 | Tanjirou, mind how you speak, too.
~Tanjirou | 炭治郎 | Tanjirou (name)
~mo | も | also, even
kuchi | 口 | mouth
~no | の | of; links nouns
kikikata | 利き方 | manner of speaking (kiku + kata)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~wa | は | topic marker
ki wo tsukeru | 気をつける | to take care
~you ni | ように | so that

> Deshitara Kamado-kun wa watashi no yashiki de oazukari itashimashou. | でしたら竈門君は私の屋敷でお預かりいたしましょう。 | Then I'll take young Kamado into my care at my estate.
~deshitara | でしたら | if that is so (polite)
~Kamado-kun | 竈門君 | young Kamado (name)
~wa | は | topic marker
watashi | 私 | I, me
~no | の | of; links nouns
yashiki | 屋敷 | estate, mansion
~de | で | at, with; means marker
oazukari itashimashou | お預かりいたしましょう | let me take into my care (humble polite volitional of azukaru)

> Damatte! Otonashiku shinasai! | 黙って！おとなしくしなさい！ | Be quiet! Behave yourself!
damatte | 黙って | be quiet (te-form of damaru, request)
otonashiku | おとなしく | quietly, obediently (adverbial form of otonashii)
shinasai | しなさい | do as told (instruction from suru)

> Anta no sei de okorareta desho! | あんたのせいで怒られたでしょ！ | We got scolded because of you!
anta | あんた | you (informal anata)
~no | の | of; links nouns
sei | せい | fault, cause of something bad
~de | で | at, with; means marker
okorareta | 怒られた | were scolded (past passive of okoru)
~desho | でしょ | right? (shortened deshou)

> Omae jibun de aruke yo na. | お前自分で歩けよな。 | Hey, walk on your own.
omae | お前 | you (rough)
jibun | 自分 | oneself
~de | で | at, with; means marker
aruke | 歩け | walk! (command of aruku)
~yo na | よな | informal emphasis seeking agreement

> Kore nondara meshi kuenai yo! | これ飲んだら飯食えないよ！ | If I drink this, I won't be able to eat!
kore | これ | this
nondara | 飲んだら | if I drink (conditional of nomu)
meshi | 飯 | food, meal (informal)
kuenai | 食えない | cannot eat (negative potential of rough kuu)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Chanto setsumei shite hoshii wake! | ちゃんと説明してほしいわけ！ | I want you to explain it properly!
chanto | ちゃんと | properly
setsumei shite | 説明して | explain (te-form of setsumei suru)
hoshii | ほしい | want (after te: want someone to do)
wake | わけ | reason, situation

> Shizuka ni nasatte kudasai! | 静かになさってください！ | Please be quiet!
shizuka ni | 静かに | quietly
nasatte | なさって | do (te-form of respectful nasaru)
kudasai | ください | please do (request form of kudasaru)

> Ikitete kurete ore wa ureshii n da! | 生きててくれて俺はうれしいんだ！ | I'm happy you're alive!
ikitete kurete | 生きててくれて | for being alive (contracted ikite ite kurete, from ikiru)
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
ureshii | うれしい | happy
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Ano hito no koe fushigi na kouyoukan datta n da. | あの人の声不思議な高揚感だったんだ。 | His voice gave me a strange feeling of uplift.
ano | あの | that over there (before a noun)
hito | 人 | person, people
~no | の | of; links nouns
koe | 声 | voice
fushigi na | 不思議な | mysterious (fushigi + na)
kouyoukan | 高揚感 | feeling of uplift
~datta | だった | was (past of da)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Itai shi tsurai kedo madamada ganbaranakya. | 痛いしつらいけどまだまだ頑張らなきゃ。 | It hurts and it's hard, but I've got to keep trying.
itai | 痛い | painful, hurts
~shi | し | and; gives a reason
tsurai | つらい | painful, difficult
~kedo | けど | but, although
madamada | まだまだ | still more, a long way to go
ganbaranakya | 頑張らなきゃ | have to keep trying (contracted ganbaranakereba, from ganbaru)
