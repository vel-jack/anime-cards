# Sentences: Episode 15 | Short lines from Mount Natagumo
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Dono you na toki demo hokori takaku ikite kudasaimase. | どのようなときでも誇り高く生きてくださいませ。 | Please live with pride, whatever happens.
dono you na | どのような | whatever kind of
toki | とき | time, occasion
~demo | でも | even; but
hokori takaku | 誇り高く | proudly (adverbial form of hokori takai)
ikite | 生きて | live (te-form of ikiru)
kudasaimase | くださいませ | please (especially courteous request, from kudasaru)

> Jibun no tachiba wo kichinto rikai shite. | 自分の立場をきちんと理解して。 | Understand your own position properly.
jibun | 自分 | oneself
~no | の | of; links nouns
tachiba | 立場 | position, situation
~wo | を | object marker
kichinto | きちんと | properly
rikai shite | 理解して | understand (te-form of rikai suru)

> Hazukashikunai tte dou iu koto da! | 恥ずかしくないってどういうことだ！ | What do you mean by having nothing to be ashamed of?!
hazukashikunai | 恥ずかしくない | not ashamed (negative of hazukashii)
~tte | って | informal quotation marker
dou iu | どういう | what kind of
koto | こと | situation, thing
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Mokutekichi ga chikazuite kite totemo kowai! | 目的地が近づいてきてとても怖い！ | Our destination is getting closer, and I'm terrified!
mokutekichi | 目的地 | destination
~ga | が | subject marker
chikazuite kite | 近づいてきて | getting closer (chikazuku + te kuru, te-form)
totemo | とても | very
kowai | 怖い | frightening

> Me no mae no ano yama kara nani mo kanjinee no ka yo? | 目の前のあの山から何も感じねえのかよ？ | Don't you sense anything from that mountain ahead of us?
me no mae | 目の前 | right in front
~no | の | of; links nouns
ano | あの | that over there (before a noun)
yama | 山 | mountain
~kara | から | because; from
nani mo | 何も | anything (with a negative: nothing)
kanjinee | 感じねえ | don't feel (rough kanjinai, from kanjiru)
~no ka yo | のかよ | emphatic question

> Yoku ganbatte modotta ne. | よく頑張って戻ったね。 | You did well to make it back.
yoku | よく | well
ganbatte | 頑張って | do one's best (te-form of ganbaru)
modotta | 戻った | returned (past of modoru)
~ne | ね | seeks agreement

> Hashira wo ikasenakute wa naranai you da. | 柱を行かせなくてはならないようだ。 | It seems we'll have to send a Hashira.
~Hashira | 柱 | Hashira (Corps rank)
~wo | を | object marker
ikasenakute wa naranai | 行かせなくてはならない | must send (causative ikaseru, from iku, + obligation)
~you da | ようだ | it is like

> Muri na hanashi da oni ga hito wo kurau kagiri wa. | 無理な話だ鬼が人を食らうかぎりは。 | It's impossible as long as demons eat people.
muri na | 無理な | impossible (muri + na)
hanashi | 話 | matter, story
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
oni | 鬼 | demon
~ga | が | subject marker
hito | 人 | person, people
~wo | を | object marker
kurau | 食らう | to devour
~kagiri | かぎり | as long as
~wa | は | topic marker

> Nani ni mo wakannai yo na ningen no koto nante. | 何にも分かんないよな人間のことなんて。 | You don't understand anything about humans.
nani ni mo | 何にも | anything at all (with a negative: nothing)
wakannai | 分かんない | don't understand (contracted wakaranai, from wakaru)
~yo na | よな | informal emphasis seeking agreement
ningen | 人間 | human, human being
~no | の | of; links nouns
koto | こと | situation, thing
~nante | なんて | something like

> Jaa buttagitte yaru ze! | じゃあぶった斬ってやるぜ！ | I'll slash them apart, then!
jaa | じゃあ | then, in that case
buttagitte yaru | ぶった斬ってやる | will slash apart (buttagiru + te yaru)
~ze | ぜ | forceful informal emphasis

> Teki wa doko da ayatsutte iru oni no ichi? | 敵はどこだ操っている鬼の位置？ | Where's the enemy? Where's the demon controlling them?
teki | 敵 | enemy
~wa | は | topic marker
doko | どこ | where
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
ayatsutte iru | 操っている | is controlling (ayatsuru + te iru)
oni | 鬼 | demon
~no | の | of; links nouns
ichi | 位置 | position

> Nan da kore wa kaze ni notte nagarete kiteru. | 何だこれは風に乗って流れてきてる。 | What is this? It's drifting on the wind.
nan da | 何だ | what is it (nani + da)
kore | これ | this
~wa | は | topic marker
kaze | 風 | wind
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
notte | 乗って | ride on (te-form of noru)
nagarete kiteru | 流れてきてる | is drifting toward us (contracted nagarete kite iru, from nagareru)

> Ayatsurarete iru hitotachi wa nantoka suru. | 操られている人たちはなんとかする。 | We'll do something about the people being controlled.
ayatsurarete iru | 操られている | are being controlled (ayatsuru, passive + te iru)
hitotachi | 人たち | people
~wa | は | topic marker
nantoka suru | なんとかする | to manage, do something about

> Omaera nante sugu ni kaasan ga korosu kara. | お前らなんてすぐに母さんが殺すから。 | Mother will kill you all in no time.
omaera | お前ら | you lot (rough)
~nante | なんて | something like
sugu ni | すぐに | immediately
kaasan | 母さん | Mom, mother
~ga | が | subject marker
korosu | 殺す | to kill
~kara | から | because; from

> Nan no tame ni dete kita n da? | 何のために出てきたんだ？ | What did you come out here for?
nan no tame | 何のため | for what purpose
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
dete kita | 出てきた | came out (deru + te kuru, past)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending
