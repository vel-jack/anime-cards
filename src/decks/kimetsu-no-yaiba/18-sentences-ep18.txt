# Sentences: Episode 18 | Short lines from A Forged Bond
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Ore wa oni wo kiri ni kita dake da. | 俺は鬼を斬りに来ただけだ。 | I only came here to cut down demons.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
oni | 鬼 | demon
~wo | を | object marker
kiri ni | 斬りに | to cut down (purpose form of kiru)
kita | 来た | came (past of kuru)
~dake | だけ | only
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Kata wo tsukatte mo kirenai. | 型を使っても斬れない。 | I can't cut it even using a form.
kata | 型 | form, set movement
~wo | を | object marker
tsukatte mo | 使っても | even if I use (tsukau + te mo)
kirenai | 斬れない | cannot cut (negative potential of kiru)

> Umaku kokyuu ga dekinaku natte kita. | うまく呼吸ができなくなってきた。 | My breathing is getting difficult.
umaku | うまく | well, skillfully (adverbial form of umai)
kokyuu | 呼吸 | breathing
~ga | が | subject marker
dekinaku | できなく | unable (linking negative of dekiru)
natte kita | なってきた | has begun to become (naru + te kuru)

> Nani miteru no? Misemono ja nai n da kedo. | なに見てるの？見せ物じゃないんだけど。 | What are you staring at? This isn't a show.
nani | なに | what
miteru | 見てる | are looking (contracted mite iru, from miru)
~no | の | of; links nouns
misemono | 見せ物 | spectacle
~ja nai | じゃない | is not (plain negative)
~n da kedo | んだけど | explanatory complaint

> Sonna usuppera na mono to onaji ni suru na. | そんな薄っぺらなものと同じにするな。 | Don't compare it to something so shallow.
sonna | そんな | that kind of
usuppera na | 薄っぺらな | shallow, flimsy (usuppera + na)
mono | もの | thing, possession
~to | と | with; quotation marker
onaji | 同じ | same
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
suru na | するな | don't do it (suru + prohibitive na)

> Boku to neesan no mondai da yo. | 僕と姉さんの問題だよ。 | It's between me and my sister.
boku | 僕 | I, me (often masculine)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
neesan | 姉さん | older sister (familiar)
~no | の | of; links nouns
mondai | 問題 | problem, matter
~da yo | だよ | emphatic plain ending

> Dochira mo onaji you ni toutoi. | どちらも同じように尊い。 | Both are equally precious.
dochira | どちら | which one, either
~mo | も | also, even
onaji | 同じ | same
~you ni | ように | so that
toutoi | 尊い | precious, worthy of respect

> Konna mono wo kizuna to wa iwanai. | こんなものを絆とは言わない。 | This is no bond.
konna | こんな | this kind of
mono | もの | thing, possession
~wo | を | object marker
kizuna | 絆 | bond
~to wa | とは | surprised realization
iwanai | 言わない | do not call (negative of iu)

> Ore wa anzen ni shusse shitai n da yo. | 俺は安全に出世したいんだよ。 | I want to rise through the ranks safely.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
anzen ni | 安全に | safely
shusse shitai | 出世したい | want to advance in rank (desire form of shusse suru)
~n da yo | んだよ | emphatic explanation

> Omae ima nante itta no? | お前今何て言ったの？ | What did you just say?
omae | お前 | you (rough)
ima | 今 | now
nante | 何て | what (in quoting speech)
itta | 言った | said (past of iu)
~no | の | question or explanatory ending

> Kanarazu tasuke ni iku kara! | 必ず助けに行くから！ | I'll definitely come to help you!
kanarazu | 必ず | without fail
tasuke ni | 助けに | to help (purpose form of tasukeru)
iku | 行く | to go
~kara | から | because; from

> Nando demo itte yaru. | 何度でも言ってやる。 | I'll say it as many times as it takes.
nando demo | 何度でも | as many times as needed
itte yaru | 言ってやる | will tell you (iu + te yaru)

> Okashii no wa omae da! | おかしいのはお前だ！ | You're the one who's wrong!
okashii | おかしい | wrong, strange
~no | の | nominalizer: turns a clause into a noun
~wa | は | topic marker
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Ore ga modoru made shinu na! | 俺が戻るまで死ぬな！ | Don't die before I get back!
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ga | が | subject marker
modoru | 戻る | to return
~made | まで | until, as far as
shinu na | 死ぬな | don't die (shinu + prohibitive na)

> Ito no nioi mo wakaru zo. | 糸のにおいも分かるぞ。 | I can smell the threads, too.
ito | 糸 | thread
~no | の | of; links nouns
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~mo | も | also, even
wakaru | 分かる | to know, understand
~zo | ぞ | forceful emphasis
