# Sentences: Episode 22 | Short lines from Master of the Mansion
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Itsu made neten da sassato okinee ka! | いつまで寝てんださっさと起きねえか！ | How long are you going to sleep? Get up already!
itsu made | いつまで | until when
neten da | 寝てんだ | are sleeping (contracted nete iru n da, from neru)
sassato | さっさと | quickly, without delay
okinee ka | 起きねえか | won't you get up? (rough negative of okiru, urging action)

> Koko wa Kisatsutai no honbu desu. | ここは鬼殺隊の本部です。 | This is the Corps headquarters.
koko | ここ | here
~wa | は | topic marker
~Kisatsutai | 鬼殺隊 | Demon Slayer Corps (organization)
~no | の | of; links nouns
honbu | 本部 | headquarters
~desu | です | is (polite ending)

> Dou shobun suru dou sekinin wo toraseru? | どう処分するどう責任を取らせる？ | How should we deal with him? How do we make him take responsibility?
dou | どう | how
shobun suru | 処分する | to deal with, punish
dou | どう | how
sekinin | 責任 | responsibility
~wo | を | object marker
toraseru | 取らせる | to make someone take (causative of toru)

> Sono koto ni tsuite tounin kara setsumei wo kikitai. | そのことについて当人から説明を聞きたい。 | I'd like to hear his own explanation of that.
sono | その | that (before a noun)
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ni tsuite | について | about
tounin | 当人 | the person concerned
~kara | から | because; from
setsumei | 説明 | explanation
~wo | を | object marker
kikitai | 聞きたい | want to ask (desire form of kiku)

> Yukkuri de daijoubu desu kara, hanashite kudasai. | ゆっくりで大丈夫ですから、話してください。 | Take your time. Please tell us.
yukkuri | ゆっくり | slowly, at ease
~de | で | at, with; means marker
daijoubu | 大丈夫 | all right
~desu | です | is (polite ending)
~kara | から | because; from
hanashite | 話して | tell (te-form of hanasu)
kudasai | ください | please do (request form of kudasaru)

> Kega ga naotta wake dewa nai node muri wa ikemasen yo. | ケガが治ったわけではないので無理はいけませんよ。 | Your wounds haven't healed, so don't strain yourself.
kega | ケガ | injury
~ga | が | subject marker
naotta | 治った | healed (past of naoru)
~wake dewa nai | わけではない | it is not the case that
~node | ので | because
muri | 無理 | unreasonable demand
~wa | は | topic marker
~ikemasen | いけません | must not (polite)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Somosomo miuchi nara kabatte atarimae. | そもそも身内ならかばって当たり前。 | It's only natural to defend your own family.
somosomo | そもそも | to begin with
miuchi | 身内 | one's family, close circle
~nara | なら | if
kabatte | かばって | protect, shield (te-form of kabau)
atarimae | 当たり前 | natural, to be expected

> Ore wa Nezuko wo naosu tame ni kenshi ni natta n desu! | 俺は禰豆子を治すために剣士になったんです！ | I became a swordsman so I could cure Nezuko!
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
~wo | を | object marker
naosu | 治す | to cure
tame | ため | purpose, sake
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
kenshi | 剣士 | swordsman
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
natta | なった | became (past of naru)
~n desu | んです | polite explanation

> Katte ni shobun shichatte ii n deshou ka? | 勝手に処分しちゃっていいんでしょうか？ | Is it all right to dispose of her without permission?
katte ni | 勝手に | without permission
shobun shichatte | 処分しちゃって | dispose of (shobun suru + contracted te shimau, te-form)
ii | いい | good
~n deshou ka | んでしょうか | polite tentative question

> Kisatsutai toshite hito wo mamoru tame ni tatakaeru n desu! | 鬼殺隊として人を守るために戦えるんです！ | She can fight to protect people as part of the Corps!
~Kisatsutai | 鬼殺隊 | Demon Slayer Corps (organization)
~toshite | として | as
hito | 人 | person, people
~wo | を | object marker
mamoru | 守る | to protect
tame | ため | purpose, sake
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
tatakaeru | 戦える | can fight (potential of tatakau)
~n desu | んです | polite explanation

> Mattaku hannou dekinakatta. | 全く反応できなかった。 | I couldn't react at all.
mattaku | 全く | completely, at all (with a negative)
hannou dekinakatta | 反応できなかった | could not react (past negative potential of hannou suru)

> Niwaka ni wa shinjigatai joukyou desu ga magire mo nai jijitsu desu. | にわかには信じ難い状況ですが紛れもない事実です。 | It's hard to believe at first, but it is an undeniable fact.
niwaka ni | にわかに | suddenly, at first
~wa | は | topic marker
shinjigatai | 信じ難い | hard to believe (shinjiru + gatai)
joukyou | 状況 | situation
~desu ga | ですが | however (polite)
magire mo nai | 紛れもない | unmistakable
jijitsu | 事実 | fact
~desu | です | is (polite ending)

> Hito wo osou to iu koto mo mata shoumei ga dekinai. | 人を襲うということもまた証明ができない。 | We can't prove that she will attack people, either.
hito | 人 | person, people
~wo | を | object marker
osou | 襲う | to attack
~to iu | という | that (introduces an idea)
koto | こと | situation, thing
~mo | も | also, even
mata | また | again
shoumei | 証明 | proof
~ga | が | subject marker
dekinai | できない | cannot do (negative of dekiru)

> Hashira de sura dare mo sesshoku shita koto ga nai to iu noni. | 柱ですら誰も接触したことがないというのに。 | Even none of the Hashira have ever made contact with him.
~Hashira | 柱 | Hashira (Corps rank)
~de sura | ですら | even
dare mo | 誰も | anyone (with a negative: no one)
sesshoku shita | 接触した | made contact (past of sesshoku suru)
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ga | が | subject marker
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist
~to iu noni | というのに | even though

> Damare! Ore ga saki ni kiiteru n da! | 黙れ！俺が先に聞いてるんだ！ | Shut up! I'm asking first!
damare | 黙れ | shut up! (command of damaru)
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ga | が | subject marker
saki | 先 | ahead, first
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
kiiteru | 聞いてる | am asking (contracted kiite iru, from kiku)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending
