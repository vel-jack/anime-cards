# Sentences: Episode 9 | Short lines from Temari Demon and Arrow Demon
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Koumyou ni mono wo kakusu kekkijutsu ga tsukawarete ita you da na. | 巧妙に物を隠す血鬼術が使われていたようだな。 | They seem to have used a demon art that cleverly conceals things.
koumyou ni | 巧妙に | skillfully, cleverly
mono | 物 | things, objects
~wo | を | object marker
kakusu | 隠す | to hide
kekkijutsu | 血鬼術 | demonic supernatural art
~ga | が | subject marker
tsukawarete ita | 使われていた | had been used (tsukau, passive + past te iru)
~you da na | ようだな | it seems

> Mari wo nagete ie wo kore dake hakai shita no ka! | 毬を投げて家をこれだけ破壊したのか！ | You threw a ball and wrecked this much of the house?!
mari | 毬 | traditional ball
~wo | を | object marker
nagete | 投げて | throw (te-form of nageru)
ie | 家 | house, home
~wo | を | object marker
kore dake | これだけ | this much
hakai shita | 破壊した | destroyed (past of hakai suru)
~no ka | のか | explanatory question

> Soto wa kiken desu! | 外は危険です！ | It's dangerous outside!
soto | 外 | outside
~wa | は | topic marker
kiken | 危険 | dangerous
~desu | です | is (polite ending)

> Tamayo-san mi wo kakuseru basho made sagatte kudasai. | 珠世さん身を隠せる場所まで下がってください。 | Ms Tamayo, please retreat somewhere you can hide.
~Tamayo-san | 珠世さん | Ms Tamayo (name)
mi | 身 | one's body, oneself
~wo | を | object marker
kakuseru | 隠せる | can hide (potential of kakusu)
basho | 場所 | place
~made | まで | until, as far as
sagatte | 下がって | step back (te-form of sagaru)
kudasai | ください | please do (request form of kudasaru)

> Mamotte itadakanakute daijoubu desu. | 守っていただかなくて大丈夫です。 | You don't need to protect us.
mamotte itadakanakute | 守っていただかなくて | without your protecting us (mamoru + humble itadaku, negative te-form)
daijoubu | 大丈夫 | all right
~desu | です | is (polite ending)

> Naze ugoku n da kono mari? | なぜ動くんだこの毬？ | Why is this ball moving?
naze | なぜ | why
ugoku | 動く | to move
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending
kono | この | this (before a noun)
mari | 毬 | traditional ball

> Kougeki sareru made nioi ga shinakatta. | 攻撃されるまでにおいがしなかった。 | I didn't smell anything until we were attacked.
kougeki sareru | 攻撃される | to be attacked (passive of kougeki suru)
~made | まで | until, as far as
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~ga | が | subject marker
shinakatta | しなかった | did not make (past negative of suru)

> Kitte shimaeba iryoku wa ochiru. | 斬ってしまえば威力は落ちる。 | Once they're cut, their force drops.
kitte shimaeba | 斬ってしまえば | if I cut them completely (kiru + conditional of te shimau)
iryoku | 威力 | power, force
~wa | は | topic marker
ochiru | 落ちる | to fall, deteriorate

> Nioi de ichi mo wakatteru. | においで位置も分かってる。 | I know where they are from the smell, too.
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~de | で | at, with; means marker
ichi | 位置 | position
~mo | も | also, even
wakatteru | 分かってる | know (contracted wakatte iru, from wakaru)

> Watashitachi wa naorimasu kara ki ni shinaide! | 私たちは治りますから気にしないで！ | We'll heal, so don't worry about us!
watashitachi | 私たち | we
~wa | は | topic marker
naorimasu | 治ります | will heal (polite naoru)
~kara | から | because; from
ki ni shinaide | 気にしないで | don't worry (negative request of ki ni suru)

> Kono futari kara chi wo totte misemasu! | この２人から血をとってみせます！ | I'll get blood from these two!
kono | この | this (before a noun)
futari | ２人 | two people
~kara | から | because; from
chi | 血 | blood
~wo | を | object marker
totte misemasu | とってみせます | will certainly take (toru + te miseru, polite)

> Honno sukoshi no aida ni sugu atarashii ude ga haeru. | ほんの少しの間にすぐ新しい腕が生える。 | A new arm grows back in no time.
honno | ほんの | mere, only
sukoshi | 少し | a little
~no | の | of; links nouns
aida | 間 | while, interval
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
sugu | すぐ | soon, immediately
atarashii | 新しい | new
ude | 腕 | arm
~ga | が | subject marker
haeru | 生える | to grow, sprout

> Nezuko zettai ni mucha wo suru na yo. | 禰豆子絶対にむちゃをするなよ。 | Nezuko, don't push yourself too far.
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
zettai ni | 絶対に | absolutely, under any circumstances
mucha | むちゃ | recklessness
~wo | を | object marker
suru na | するな | don't do it (suru + prohibitive na)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Kono kusuri de sugu ashi wa naorimasu kara ne. | この薬ですぐ脚は治りますからね。 | This medicine will heal your leg right away.
kono | この | this (before a noun)
kusuri | 薬 | medicine
~de | で | at, with; means marker
sugu | すぐ | soon, immediately
ashi | 脚 | leg
~wa | は | topic marker
naorimasu | 治ります | will heal (polite naoru)
~kara | から | because; from
~ne | ね | seeks agreement

> Chokusetsu furenai you ni ano yajirushi no muki wo kaeru n da! | 直接触れないようにあの矢印の向きを変えるんだ！ | Change the direction of those arrows without touching them directly!
chokusetsu | 直接 | directly
furenai | 触れない | do not touch (negative of fureru)
~you ni | ように | so that
ano | あの | that over there (before a noun)
yajirushi | 矢印 | arrow mark
~no | の | of; links nouns
muki | 向き | direction, orientation
~wo | を | object marker
kaeru | 変える | to change something
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending
