# Sentences: Episode 1 | Short lines from Cruelty
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Nande konna koto ni natta n da. | なんでこんなことになったんだ。 | How did it end up like this?
nande | なんで | why (informal)
konna | こんな | this kind of
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
natta | なった | became (past of naru)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Kao ga makkuro ja nai no. | 顔が真っ黒じゃないの。 | Your face is completely black.
kao | 顔 | face
~ga | が | subject marker
makkuro | 真っ黒 | pitch-black
~ja nai no | じゃないの | isn't it? (informal ending)

> Sukoshi demo sumi wo utte kuru yo. | 少しでも炭を売ってくるよ。 | I'll sell at least a little charcoal.
sukoshi | 少し | a little
~demo | でも | even; but
sumi | 炭 | charcoal
~wo | を | object marker
utte kuru | 売ってくる | go sell and return (uru + te kuru)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Sono kawari umai mon ippai katte kuru kara. | そのかわりうまいもんいっぱい買ってくるから。 | I'll bring back plenty of tasty things instead.
sono kawari | そのかわり | in return, instead
umai | うまい | tasty (informal)
mon | もん | things (contracted mono)
ippai | いっぱい | plenty
katte kuru | 買ってくる | go buy and return (kau + te kuru)
~kara | から | because; from

> Ore ja nai tte itta daro! | 俺じゃないって言っただろ！ | You said it wasn't me!
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ja nai | じゃない | is not (plain negative)
~tte | って | informal quotation marker
itta | 言った | said (past of iu)
~daro | だろ | right? (rough darou)

> Omee yama ni kaeru tsumori ka? | おめえ山に帰るつもりか？ | Are you planning to go back up the mountain?
omee | おめえ | you (rough omae)
yama | 山 | mountain
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
kaeru | 帰る | to return home
tsumori | つもり | intention
~ka | か | question marker

> Dakara yoru arukimawaru mon ja nee. | だから夜歩き回るもんじゃねえ。 | That's why you shouldn't wander around at night.
dakara | だから | so, that is why
yoru | 夜 | night
arukimawaru | 歩き回る | to walk around
~mon ja nee | もんじゃねえ | shouldn't (rough mono ja nai)

> Nezuko dake wa mada nukumori ga aru. | 禰豆子だけはまだぬくもりがある。 | Only Nezuko is still warm.
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
~dake | だけ | only
~wa | は | topic marker
mada | まだ | still, yet
nukumori | ぬくもり | warmth
~ga | が | subject marker
aru | ある | to exist, be present

> Niichan ga tasukete yaru! | 兄ちゃんが助けてやる！ | My big brother will help you!
niichan | 兄ちゃん | big brother (familiar)
~ga | が | subject marker
tasukete yaru | 助けてやる | will help you (tasukeru + te yaru)

> Ore no shigoto wa oni wo kiru koto da. | 俺の仕事は鬼を斬ることだ。 | My job is to cut down demons.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~no | の | of; links nouns
shigoto | 仕事 | job, work
~wa | は | topic marker
oni | 鬼 | demon
~wo | を | object marker
kiru | 斬る | to cut with a blade
koto | こと | situation, thing
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Kitto Nezuko wo ningen ni modosu. | きっと禰豆子を人間に戻す。 | I'll turn Nezuko back into a human.
kitto | きっと | surely
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
~wo | を | object marker
ningen | 人間 | human, human being
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
modosu | 戻す | to return something to its former state

> Ore ga zenbu chanto suru kara. | 俺が全部ちゃんとするから。 | I'll put everything right.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ga | が | subject marker
zenbu | 全部 | all, everything
chanto | ちゃんと | properly
suru | する | to do
~kara | から | because; from

> Naze ore ni senaka wo miseta? | なぜ俺に背中を見せた？ | Why did you turn your back on me?
naze | なぜ | why
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
senaka | 背中 | back (of the body)
~wo | を | object marker
miseta | 見せた | showed (past of miseru)

> Sonna no wa ima suru koto ja nai. | そんなのは今することじゃない。 | This isn't the time for that.
sonna | そんな | that sort of
~no | の | nominalizer: turns a clause into a noun
~wa | は | topic marker
ima | 今 | now
suru | する | to do
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ja nai | じゃない | is not (plain negative)

> Hito wo kuttari shinai! | 人を食ったりしない！ | She won't eat people!
hito | 人 | person, people
~wo | を | object marker
kuttari | 食ったり | eat and such (listing form of rough kuu)
shinai | しない | do not (negative of suru)
