# Sentences: Episode 12 | Short lines from The Boar Bares Its Fangs, Zenitsu Sleeps
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Konna chiisai ko wo fumu nante dou iu tsumori da! | こんな小さい子を踏むなんてどういうつもりだ！ | What do you think you're doing, stepping on such a small child?!
konna | こんな | this kind of
chiisai | 小さい | small
ko | 子 | child
~wo | を | object marker
fumu | 踏む | to step on
~nante | なんて | something like
dou iu | どういう | what kind of
tsumori | つもり | intention
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Yameru n da! Soko ni oni ga iru n da zo! | やめるんだ！そこに鬼がいるんだぞ！ | Stop it! There's a demon right there!
yameru | やめる | to stop
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending
soko | そこ | there
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
oni | 鬼 | demon
~ga | が | subject marker
iru | いる | to be, exist (living beings)
~n da zo | んだぞ | forceful explanation

> Ore no ushiro ni iru n da yo. | 俺の後ろにいるんだよ。 | Stay behind me.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~no | の | of; links nouns
ushiro | 後ろ | behind
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
iru | いる | to be, exist (living beings)
~n da yo | んだよ | emphatic explanation

> Hanashikakeru nara kyuu ni konaide kure yo. | 話しかけるなら急にこないでくれよ。 | Don't come at me so suddenly when you talk to me.
hanashikakeru | 話しかける | to speak to someone
~nara | なら | if
kyuu ni | 急に | suddenly
konaide kure | こないでくれ | please don't come (negative request of kuru)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Tada chotto ase iki furue ga hidosugite… | ただちょっと汗息震えがひどすぎて… | But the sweating, panting, and shaking are a bit too much.
tada | ただ | just, but
chotto | ちょっと | a little, briefly
ase | 汗 | sweat
iki | 息 | breath
furue | 震え | trembling
~ga | が | subject marker
hidosugite | ひどすぎて | being too severe (te-form of hidosugiru, from hidoi)

> Nante ii ko nan da konna obieta oto ni natteru noni. | なんていい子なんだこんなおびえた音になってるのに。 | He's such a good kid, even though he sounds so frightened.
nante | なんて | how (exclamation)
ii | いい | good
ko | 子 | child
~nan da | なんだ | emphatic explanation
konna | こんな | this kind of
obieta | おびえた | frightened (past of obieru)
oto | 音 | sound
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
natteru | なってる | is becoming (contracted natte iru, from naru)
~noni | のに | even though

> Koitsu kehai ga kawatta. | こいつ気配が変わった。 | His presence has changed.
koitsu | こいつ | this guy (rough)
kehai | 気配 | presence, aura
~ga | が | subject marker
kawatta | 変わった | changed (past of kawaru)

> Kono on wa wasurenai yo. | この恩は忘れないよ。 | I'll never forget this kindness!
kono | この | this (before a noun)
on | 恩 | kindness owed, favor
~wa | は | topic marker
wasurenai | 忘れない | will not forget (negative of wasureru)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Yoketa na zuibun iki no ii ningen da. | よけたな随分生きのいい人間だ。 | You dodged it. You're quite a lively human.
yoketa | よけた | dodged (past of yokeru)
~na | な | emphasis; prohibition after a verb
zuibun | 随分 | quite, considerably
iki | 生き | liveliness, freshness
~no | の | of; links nouns
ii | いい | good
ningen | 人間 | human, human being
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Kono kizugusuri wa sugoi n da zo! | この傷薬はすごいんだぞ！ | This wound medicine is really good!
kono | この | this (before a noun)
kizugusuri | 傷薬 | wound medicine
~wa | は | topic marker
sugoi | すごい | amazing
~n da zo | んだぞ | forceful explanation

> Dou itami ga hiita ro? | どう痛みが引いたろ？ | How is it? The pain has eased, right?
dou | どう | how
itami | 痛み | pain
~ga | が | subject marker
hiita | 引いた | subsided (past of hiku)
~ro | ろ | shortened darou, right?

> Oni wo taoshi ni itte kuru kara. | 鬼を倒しに行ってくるから。 | I'm going to defeat the demon and come back.
oni | 鬼 | demon
~wo | を | object marker
taoshi ni | 倒しに | to defeat (purpose form of taosu)
itte kuru | 行ってくる | go and return (iku + te kuru)
~kara | から | because; from

> Ore wa mou honto ni zutto gaman shiteta! | 俺はもうホントにずっと我慢してた！ | I've really been holding back this whole time!
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
mou | もう | already; any more
honto ni | ホントに | really (informal hontou ni)
zutto | ずっと | all along, continuously
gaman shiteta | 我慢してた | had been enduring (contracted gaman shite ita, from gaman suru)

> Kega no sei de warui souzou bakari shite shimau. | ケガのせいで悪い想像ばかりしてしまう。 | My injuries keep making me imagine the worst.
kega | ケガ | injury
~no | の | of; links nouns
sei | せい | fault, cause of something bad
~de | で | at, with; means marker
warui | 悪い | bad
souzou | 想像 | imagination
~bakari | ばかり | nothing but
shite shimau | してしまう | end up doing (suru + te shimau)

> Donna teki to mo tatakaeru n da. | どんな敵とも戦えるんだ。 | I can fight any enemy.
donna | どんな | what kind of, any
teki | 敵 | enemy
~to | と | with; quotation marker
~mo | も | also, even
tatakaeru | 戦える | can fight (potential of tatakau)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending
