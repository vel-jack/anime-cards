# Sentences: Episode 7 | Short lines from Muzan Kibutsuji
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Fukaoi suru na. Kocchi e modore! | 深追いするな。こっちへ戻れ！ | Don't chase too far. Come back here!
fukaoi suru na | 深追いするな | don't pursue too far (fukaoi suru + prohibitive na)
kocchi | こっち | this way (informal)
~e | へ | toward
modore | 戻れ | come back! (command of modoru)

> Futari wo mamotte kurereba kougeki ni sennen dekiru. | ２人を守ってくれれば攻撃に専念できる。 | If you protect those two, I can focus on attacking.
futari | ２人 | two people
~wo | を | object marker
mamotte kurereba | 守ってくれれば | if you protect them for me (mamoru + conditional of kureru)
kougeki | 攻撃 | attack
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
sennen dekiru | 専念できる | can concentrate (potential of sennen suru)

> Kono numa no naka ni wa hotondo kuuki mo nai. | この沼の中にはほとんど空気もない。 | There's hardly any air in this swamp.
kono | この | this (before a noun)
numa | 沼 | swamp
~no | の | of; links nouns
naka | 中 | inside
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~wa | は | topic marker
hotondo | ほとんど | almost, hardly (with a negative)
kuuki | 空気 | air
~mo | も | also, even
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist

> Ittai ore ga doko de tanren shita to omotteru n da? | 一体俺がどこで鍛錬したと思ってるんだ？ | Where do you think I trained?
ittai | 一体 | on earth (emphasis in a question)
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ga | が | subject marker
doko | どこ | where
~de | で | at, with; means marker
tanren shita | 鍛錬した | trained intensely (past of tanren suru)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
omotteru | 思ってる | think (contracted omotte iru, from omou)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Mizu no naka de koso chikara wo hakki suru waza ga aru! | 水の中でこそ力を発揮する技がある！ | Some techniques show their true power in water!
mizu | 水 | water
~no | の | of; links nouns
naka | 中 | inside
~de | で | at, with; means marker
~koso | こそ | precisely, especially
chikara | 力 | strength, power
~wo | を | object marker
hakki suru | 発揮する | to display, bring out
waza | 技 | technique, skill
~ga | が | subject marker
aru | ある | to exist, be present

> Da ga tanchou na kougeki ni narete kita zo! | だが単調な攻撃に慣れてきたぞ！ | But I'm getting used to these predictable attacks!
~da ga | だが | but, however
tanchou na | 単調な | monotonous, predictable (tanchou + na)
kougeki | 攻撃 | attack
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
narete kita | 慣れてきた | have begun to get used to (nareru + te kuru)
~zo | ぞ | forceful emphasis

> Ittai dore dake no hito wo koroshita! | 一体どれだけの人を殺した！ | How many people have you killed?!
ittai | 一体 | on earth (emphasis in a question)
dore dake | どれだけ | how many, how much
~no | の | of; links nouns
hito | 人 | person, people
~wo | を | object marker
koroshita | 殺した | killed (past of korosu)

> Shabette wa ikenai. | しゃべってはいけない。 | You mustn't speak about it.
shabette | しゃべって | speak (te-form of shaberu)
~wa | は | topic marker
~ikenai | いけない | must not (after te wa)

> Shabettara sugu ni wakaru. | しゃべったらすぐに分かる。 | He'll know as soon as you speak.
shabettara | しゃべったら | if you speak (conditional of shaberu)
sugu ni | すぐに | immediately
wakaru | 分かる | to know, understand

> Mata nani mo kikidasenai. | また何も聞き出せない。 | Once again, I couldn't get any answers.
mata | また | again
nani mo | 何も | anything (with a negative: nothing)
kikidasenai | 聞き出せない | cannot get information out (negative potential of kikidasu)

> Mou sukoshi matte kure. | もう少し待ってくれ。 | Please wait a little longer.
mou sukoshi | もう少し | a little more
matte kure | 待ってくれ | wait for me (matsu + te kure request)

> Kon'yakusha wo ushinatte daijoubu da to omou ka? | 婚約者を失って大丈夫だと思うか？ | Do you think I'd be all right after losing my fiancee?
kon'yakusha | 婚約者 | fiance, fiancee
~wo | を | object marker
ushinatte | 失って | losing (te-form of ushinau)
daijoubu | 大丈夫 | all right
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
omou | 思う | to think
~ka | か | question marker

> Hidoi koto wo itta. Douka yurushite kure. | ひどいことを言った。どうか許してくれ。 | I said something awful. Please forgive me.
hidoi | ひどい | terrible, cruel
koto | こと | situation, thing
~wo | を | object marker
itta | 言った | said (past of iu)
douka | どうか | please (earnest request)
yurushite kure | 許してくれ | please forgive me (yurusu + te kure)

> Shounen no te de wa nakatta. | 少年の手ではなかった。 | These weren't the hands of a boy.
shounen | 少年 | boy
~no | の | of; links nouns
te | 手 | hand
~de | で | at, with; means marker
~wa | は | topic marker
~nakatta | なかった | was not (past of nai)

> Ningen no furi wo shite kurashite iru n da! | 人間のふりをして暮らしているんだ！ | He's living here pretending to be human!
ningen | 人間 | human, human being
~no | の | of; links nouns
furi | ふり | pretense
~wo | を | object marker
shite | して | doing (te-form of suru)
kurashite iru | 暮らしている | is living (kurasu + te iru)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending
