# Sentences: Episode 16 | Short lines from Letting Someone Else Go First
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Tsuyoi kehai wo binbin kanjiru ze! | 強い気配をビンビン感じるぜ！ | I can feel a powerful presence!
tsuyoi | 強い | strong
kehai | 気配 | presence, aura
~wo | を | object marker
binbin | ビンビン | intensely, strongly
kanjiru | 感じる | to feel, sense
~ze | ぜ | forceful informal emphasis

> Ito wo kireba ii to iu no ga wakatta shi. | 糸を斬ればいいというのが分かったし。 | I figured out that we just need to cut the threads.
ito | 糸 | thread
~wo | を | object marker
kireba | 斬れば | if we cut (conditional of kiru)
ii | いい | good
~to iu | という | that (introduces an idea)
~no | の | nominalizer: turns a clause into a noun
~ga | が | subject marker
wakatta | 分かった | understood (past of wakaru)
~shi | し | and; gives a reason

> Modotte kitara zettai naguru kara na! | 戻ってきたら絶対殴るからな！ | I'll definitely punch you when you get back!
modotte kitara | 戻ってきたら | when I return (modoru + conditional of te kuru)
zettai | 絶対 | absolutely
naguru | 殴る | to punch
~kara na | からな | forceful warning

> Sou ja nai to minna koroshite shimau! | そうじゃないとみんな殺してしまう！ | Otherwise we'll end up killing everyone!
~sou ja nai to | そうじゃないと | if that is not the case
minna | みんな | everyone
koroshite shimau | 殺してしまう | end up killing (korosu + te shimau)

> Oningyou mo tsuyoku naru no yo. | お人形も強くなるのよ。 | The dolls get stronger too.
oningyou | お人形 | doll (polite ningyou)
~mo | も | also, even
tsuyoku | 強く | strongly (adverbial form of tsuyoi)
naru | なる | to become
~no yo | のよ | explanatory emphasis

> Oni ga ito de muriyari karada wo ugokashite iru kara. | 鬼が糸で無理やり体を動かしているから。 | The demon is forcing their bodies to move with threads.
oni | 鬼 | demon
~ga | が | subject marker
ito | 糸 | thread
~de | で | at, with; means marker
muriyari | 無理やり | by force
karada | 体 | body
~wo | を | object marker
ugokashite iru | 動かしている | is moving something (ugokasu + te iru)
~kara | から | because; from

> Ugokasareru to gekitsuu de taerarenai. | 動かされると激痛で耐えられない。 | It hurts unbearably when I'm moved.
ugokasareru | 動かされる | to be forced to move (passive of ugokasu)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
gekitsuu | 激痛 | excruciating pain
~de | で | at, with; means marker
taerarenai | 耐えられない | cannot endure (negative potential of taeru)

> Wakatteru! Kangaeru kara mattete kure! | 分かってる！考えるから待っててくれ！ | I know! I'll think of something, so wait!
wakatteru | 分かってる | know (contracted wakatte iru, from wakaru)
kangaeru | 考える | to think
~kara | から | because; from
mattete kure | 待っててくれ | please keep waiting (contracted matte ite kure, from matsu)

> Sumanai chotto mitenakatta! | すまないちょっと見てなかった！ | Sorry! I wasn't watching for a moment!
sumanai | すまない | sorry (apologetic expression)
chotto | ちょっと | a little, briefly
mitenakatta | 見てなかった | was not watching (contracted mite inakatta, from miru)

> Kocchi da kanari chikazuite iru ze! | こっちだかなり近づいているぜ！ | This way. We're getting very close!
kocchi | こっち | this way (informal)
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
kanari | かなり | quite, considerably
chikazuite iru | 近づいている | are approaching (chikazuku + te iru)
~ze | ぜ | forceful informal emphasis

> Ore no hou ga saki ni kizuiteta! | 俺のほうが先に気付いてた！ | I noticed first!
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~no | の | of; links nouns
hou | ほう | side (in a comparison)
~ga | が | subject marker
saki | 先 | ahead, first
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
kizuiteta | 気付いてた | had noticed (contracted kizuite ita, from kizuku)

> Nanka dandan hara tatte kita. | なんかだんだん腹立ってきた。 | I'm starting to get angry.
nanka | なんか | somehow (informal)
dandan | だんだん | gradually
hara tatte kita | 腹立ってきた | have begun to get angry (hara ga tatsu + te kuru)

> Aitsu kyuusho ga nee zo. Nai mono wa kirenee! | あいつ急所がねえぞ。ないものは斬れねえ！ | He doesn't have a weak point. You can't cut what isn't there!
aitsu | あいつ | that guy (rough)
kyuusho | 急所 | vital spot, weak point
~ga | が | subject marker
nee | ねえ | there isn't (rough nai)
~zo | ぞ | forceful emphasis
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist
mono | もの | thing, possession
~wa | は | topic marker
kirenee | 斬れねえ | can't cut (rough kirenai, negative potential of kiru)

> Kono oni wo taosu tame ni chikara wo awaseyou! | この鬼を倒すために力を合わせよう！ | Let's work together to defeat this demon!
kono | この | this (before a noun)
oni | 鬼 | demon
~wo | を | object marker
taosu | 倒す | to defeat
tame | ため | purpose, sake
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
chikara | 力 | strength, power
~wo | を | object marker
awaseyou | 合わせよう | let's combine (volitional of awaseru)

> Sukoshi mo itakunai kurushiku mo nai. | 少しも痛くない苦しくもない。 | It doesn't hurt at all. I'm not suffering either.
sukoshi mo | 少しも | even a little (with a negative: not at all)
itakunai | 痛くない | not painful (negative of itai)
kurushiku mo nai | 苦しくもない | not distressing either (kurushii + mo nai)
