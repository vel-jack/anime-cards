# Sentences: Episode 3 | Short lines from Sabito and Makomo
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Saishuu Senbetsu wo ukete ii ka douka wa washi ga kimeru. | 最終選別を受けていいかどうかはわしが決める。 | I'll decide whether you may take the final selection.
~Saishuu Senbetsu | 最終選別 | Final Selection (exam name)
~wo | を | object marker
ukete | 受けて | take an exam (te-form of ukeru)
ii | いい | good
~ka | か | question marker
~douka | どうか | whether or not
~wa | は | topic marker
washi | わし | I (elderly speech)
~ga | が | subject marker
kimeru | 決める | to decide

> Wana no nan'ido wa dondon agaru. | 罠の難易度はどんどん上がる。 | The traps keep getting more difficult.
wana | 罠 | trap
~no | の | of; links nouns
nan'ido | 難易度 | degree of difficulty
~wa | は | topic marker
dondon | どんどん | steadily, more and more
agaru | 上がる | to rise

> Tate no chikara ni wa tsuyoi kedo yoko no chikara ni wa yowai. | 縦の力には強いけど横の力には弱い。 | It's strong against vertical force but weak against sideways force.
tate | 縦 | vertical direction
~no | の | of; links nouns
chikara | 力 | strength, power
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~wa | は | topic marker
tsuyoi | 強い | strong
~kedo | けど | but, although
yoko | 横 | sideways direction
~no | の | of; links nouns
chikara | 力 | strength, power
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~wa | は | topic marker
yowai | 弱い | weak

> Donna taisei ni natte mo ukemi wo totte. | どんな体勢になっても受け身を取って。 | No matter what position you're in, break your fall.
donna | どんな | what kind of, any
taisei | 体勢 | body position
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
natte mo | なっても | even if you become (naru + te mo)
ukemi | 受け身 | breakfall
~wo | を | object marker
totte | 取って | take (te-form of toru, here a request)

> Kahanshin wa dosshiri kamaeru. | 下半身はどっしり構える。 | Plant your lower body firmly.
kahanshin | 下半身 | lower body
~wa | は | topic marker
dosshiri | どっしり | firmly, solidly
kamaeru | 構える | to take a stance

> Tsuzuite mizu to hitotsu ni nare to iwareru. | 続いて水とひとつになれと言われる。 | Next, he tells me to become one with the water.
tsuzuite | 続いて | next (te-form of tsuzuku)
mizu | 水 | water
~to | と | with; quotation marker
hitotsu | ひとつ | one
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
nare | なれ | become! (command of naru)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
iwareru | 言われる | to be told (passive of iu)

> Mou oshieru koto wa nai. | もう教えることはない。 | There is nothing more to teach you.
mou | もう | already; any more
oshieru | 教える | to teach
koto | こと | situation, thing
~wa | は | topic marker
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist

> Ore wa Urokodaki-san ni naratta koto wo mainichi kurikaeshita. | 俺は鱗滝さんに習ったことを毎日繰り返した。 | I repeated what Mr Urokodaki taught me every day.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
~Urokodaki-san | 鱗滝さん | Mr Urokodaki (name)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
naratta | 習った | learned (past of narau)
koto | こと | situation, thing
~wo | を | object marker
mainichi | 毎日 | every day
kurikaeshita | 繰り返した | repeated (past of kurikaesu)

> Donna kurushimi ni mo damatte taero. | どんな苦しみにも黙って耐えろ。 | Endure every hardship without a word.
donna | どんな | what kind of, any
kurushimi | 苦しみ | suffering
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~mo | も | also, even
damatte | 黙って | silently (te-form of damaru)
taero | 耐えろ | endure! (command of taeru)

> Nani mo jibun no mono ni shite inai! | 何も自分のものにしていない！ | You haven't made any of it your own!
nani mo | 何も | anything (with a negative: nothing)
jibun | 自分 | oneself
~no | の | of; links nouns
mono | もの | thing, possession
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
shite inai | していない | have not made (suru + te inai)

> Omae wa chishiki toshite sore wo oboeta dake da. | お前は知識としてそれを覚えただけだ。 | You only memorized it as information.
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~wa | は | topic marker
chishiki | 知識 | knowledge
~toshite | として | as
sore | それ | that
~wo | を | object marker
oboeta | 覚えた | memorized (past of oboeru)
~dake | だけ | only
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Omae no chikara wo misete miro! | お前の力を見せてみろ！ | Show me your strength!
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~no | の | of; links nouns
chikara | 力 | strength, power
~wo | を | object marker
misete miro | 見せてみろ | try showing me! (miseru + te miru command)

> Nareru kana anna fuu ni! | なれるかなあんなふうに！ | Could I become like that too?
nareru | なれる | can become (potential of naru)
~kana | かな | I wonder
anna | あんな | like that over there
fuu | ふう | manner, way
~ni | に | to, at; target marker

> Kodomotachi wa hoka ni mo mada iru n da yo. | 子供たちはほかにもまだいるんだよ。 | There are other children here too.
kodomotachi | 子供たち | children
~wa | は | topic marker
hoka | ほか | other
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~mo | も | also, even
mada | まだ | still, yet
iru | いる | to be, exist (living beings)
~n da yo | んだよ | emphatic explanation

> Tonikaku hai wo ookiku suru koto. | とにかく肺を大きくすること。 | Anyway, the key is to expand your lungs.
tonikaku | とにかく | anyway, above all
hai | 肺 | lungs
~wo | を | object marker
ookiku suru | 大きくする | make bigger (ookii + suru)
koto | こと | situation, thing
