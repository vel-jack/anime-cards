# Sentences: Episode 26 | Short lines from New Mission
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Ano onna wo chuushin ni kuukan ga yugande iru you da. | あの女を中心に空間がゆがんでいるようだ。 | It looks as though space is warping around that woman.
ano | あの | that over there (before a noun)
onna | 女 | woman
~wo | を | object marker
chuushin | 中心 | center
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
kuukan | 空間 | space
~ga | が | subject marker
yugande iru | ゆがんでいる | is warping (yugamu + te iru)
~you da | ようだ | it is like

> Koube wo tarete tsukubae. | こうべを垂れてつくばえ。 | Bow your head and prostrate yourself.
koube | こうべ | head (literary)
~wo | を | object marker
tarete | 垂れて | lower, hang down (te-form of tareru)
tsukubae | つくばえ | crouch low! (command of archaic tsukubau)

> Koutei shite mo hitei shite mo korosareru. | 肯定しても否定しても殺される。 | Even if I agree or disagree, he'll kill me.
koutei shite mo | 肯定しても | even if I affirm (koutei suru + te mo)
hitei shite mo | 否定しても | even if I deny (hitei suru + te mo)
korosareru | 殺される | to be killed (passive of korosu)

> Watashi wa mada oyaku ni tatemasu. | 私はまだお役に立てます。 | I can still be useful to you.
watashi | 私 | I, me
~wa | は | topic marker
mada | まだ | still, yet
oyaku | お役 | usefulness, service (polite yaku)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
tatemasu | 立てます | can stand (polite potential of tatsu, here be useful)

> Ima no omae no chikara de dore hodo no koto ga dekiru? | 今のお前の力でどれほどのことができる？ | How much can you accomplish with your current strength?
ima | 今 | now
~no | の | of; links nouns
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~no | の | of; links nouns
chikara | 力 | strength, power
~de | で | at, with; means marker
dore hodo | どれほど | how much
~no | の | of; links nouns
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ga | が | subject marker
dekiru | できる | to be able to

> Watashi ga tadashii to itta koto ga tadashii no da. | 私が正しいと言ったことが正しいのだ。 | What I say is right is right.
watashi | 私 | I, me
~ga | が | subject marker
tadashii | 正しい | right, correct
~to | と | with; quotation marker
itta | 言った | said (past of iu)
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ga | が | subject marker
tadashii | 正しい | right, correct
~no da | のだ | explanatory ending

> Watashi wa yumemigokochi de gozaimasu. | 私は夢見心地でございます。 | I feel as though I'm dreaming.
watashi | 私 | I, me
~wa | は | topic marker
yumemigokochi | 夢見心地 | dreamlike feeling
~de gozaimasu | でございます | is (very polite ending)

> Sore ni tsuite wa watashi mo onaji iken da. | それについては私も同じ意見だ。 | I share that opinion.
sore | それ | that
~ni tsuite | について | about
~wa | は | topic marker
watashi | 私 | I, me
~mo | も | also, even
onaji | 同じ | same
iken | 意見 | opinion
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Oretachi shuppatsu suru yo. Iroiro arigatou. | 俺たち出発するよ。いろいろありがとう。 | We're leaving. Thanks for everything.
oretachi | 俺たち | we (rough, masculine)
shuppatsu suru | 出発する | to depart
~yo | よ | emphasis
iroiro | いろいろ | all sorts of things
arigatou | ありがとう | thank you

> Shiji saretenai koto wa kore wo nagete kimeru. | 指示されてないことはこれを投げて決める。 | I toss this to decide things I haven't been instructed about.
shiji saretenai | 指示されてない | have not been instructed (contracted shiji sarete inai, passive of shiji suru)
koto | こと | situation, thing
~wa | は | topic marker
kore | これ | this
~wo | を | object marker
nagete | 投げて | throw (te-form of nageru)
kimeru | 決める | to decide

> Kanao wa dou shitakatta? | カナヲはどうしたかった？ | What did you want to do, Kanao?
~Kanao | カナヲ | Kanao (name)
~wa | は | topic marker
dou | どう | how
shitakatta | したかった | wanted to do (past desire of suru)

> Omote ga detara Kanao wa kokoro no mama ni ikiru! | 表が出たらカナヲは心のままに生きる！ | If it's heads, Kanao will live by her own heart!
omote | 表 | heads (of a coin)
~ga | が | subject marker
detara | 出たら | if it comes out (conditional of deru)
~Kanao | カナヲ | Kanao (name)
~wa | は | topic marker
kokoro | 心 | heart
~no | の | of; links nouns
mama | まま | as, in accordance with
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
ikiru | 生きる | to live

> Hito wa kokoro ga gendouryoku da kara. | 人は心が原動力だから。 | The heart is what drives people.
hito | 人 | person, people
~wa | は | topic marker
kokoro | 心 | heart
~ga | が | subject marker
gendouryoku | 原動力 | driving force
~da kara | だから | because it is

> Omote ga deru made nando demo nagetsuzukeyou to omotteta kara. | 表が出るまで何度でも投げ続けようと思ってたから。 | I planned to keep tossing it until heads came up.
omote | 表 | heads (of a coin)
~ga | が | subject marker
deru | 出る | to come out
~made | まで | until, as far as
nando demo | 何度でも | as many times as needed
nagetsuzukeyou | 投げ続けよう | keep throwing (volitional of nagetsuzukeru)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
omotteta | 思ってた | was thinking (contracted omotte ita, from omou)
~kara | から | because; from

> Ore to Nezuko wa doko e iku toki mo issho da. | 俺と禰豆子はどこへ行くときも一緒だ。 | Nezuko and I go everywhere together.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
~wa | は | topic marker
doko | どこ | where
~e | へ | toward
iku | 行く | to go
toki | とき | time, occasion
~mo | も | also, even
issho | 一緒 | together
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
