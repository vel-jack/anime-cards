# Sentences: Episode 6 | Short lines from Swordsman Accompanying a Demon
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Tsuukisei wa yoi ga nurenikuku moenikui. | 通気性は良いがぬれにくく燃えにくい。 | It breathes well but resists water and fire.
tsuukisei | 通気性 | breathability
~wa | は | topic marker
yoi | 良い | good
~ga | が | subject marker
nurenikuku | ぬれにくく | hard to wet (linking form of nureru + nikui)
moenikui | 燃えにくい | hard to burn (moeru + nikui)

> Kanarazu Nezuko wo ningen ni modoshite misemasu. | 必ず禰豆子を人間に戻してみせます。 | I'll make sure I turn Nezuko back into a human.
kanarazu | 必ず | without fail
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
~wo | を | object marker
ningen | 人間 | human, human being
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
modoshite misemasu | 戻してみせます | will certainly turn back (modosu + te miseru, polite)

> Nezuko kore kara wa itsumo issho da kara na. | 禰豆子これからはいつも一緒だからな。 | From now on we'll always be together, Nezuko.
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
kore kara | これから | from now on
~wa | は | topic marker
itsumo | いつも | always
issho | 一緒 | together
~da kara na | だからな | emphatic assurance

> Nemuru koto de tairyoku wo kaifuku shite iru no kamo shirenai. | 眠ることで体力を回復しているのかもしれない。 | She may be recovering her strength through sleep.
nemuru | 眠る | to sleep
koto | こと | situation, thing
~de | で | at, with; means marker
tairyoku | 体力 | physical strength
~wo | を | object marker
kaifuku shite iru | 回復している | is recovering (kaifuku suru + te iru)
~no kamo shirenai | のかもしれない | it may be that

> Hokusei no machi tte koko na no kana? | 北西の町ってここなのかな？ | I wonder if this is the northwestern town.
hokusei | 北西 | northwest
~no | の | of; links nouns
machi | 町 | town
~tte | って | informal quotation marker
koko | ここ | here
~na no kana | なのかな | I wonder if it is

> Chotto ohanashi wo kikitai no desu ga ii desu ka? | ちょっとお話を聞きたいのですがいいですか？ | May I ask you a few questions?
chotto | ちょっと | a little, briefly
ohanashi | お話 | conversation (polite hanashi)
~wo | を | object marker
kikitai | 聞きたい | want to ask (desire form of kiku)
~no desu ga | のですが | polite lead-in
ii | いい | good
~desu ka | ですか | polite question ending

> Shinjite moraenai kamo shirenai ga… | 信じてもらえないかもしれないが… | You may not believe me, but...
shinjite moraenai | 信じてもらえない | may not be believed (shinjiru + negative potential of morau)
~kamo shirenai | かもしれない | might, may
~ga | が | subject marker

> Sono tame ni ore wa koko ni kimashita kara. | そのために俺はここに来ましたから。 | That's why I've come here.
sono | その | that (before a noun)
tame | ため | purpose, sake
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
koko | ここ | here
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
kimashita | 来ました | came (polite past of kuru)
~kara | から | because; from

> Dakara yasumu wake ni wa ikanai. | だから休むわけにはいかない。 | That's why I can't afford to rest.
dakara | だから | so, that is why
yasumu | 休む | to rest
~wake ni wa ikanai | わけにはいかない | cannot afford to, must not

> Kanarazu chikaku ni iru hazu desu. | 必ず近くにいるはずです。 | It must be close by.
kanarazu | 必ず | without fail
chikaku | 近く | nearby
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
iru | いる | to be, exist (living beings)
hazu | はず | expectation, should be
~desu | です | is (polite ending)

> Sarawareta kotachi wa dou natta no kashira? | 攫われた娘たちはどうなったのかしら？ | I wonder what happened to the abducted girls.
sarawareta | 攫われた | were abducted (past passive of sarau)
kotachi | 娘たち | girls (dialogue reads 娘 as ko)
~wa | は | topic marker
dou | どう | how
natta | なった | became (past of naru)
~no kashira | のかしら | I wonder (often feminine)

> Kihonteki ni oni wa murenai to kiita. | 基本的に鬼は群れないと聞いた。 | I heard demons generally don't form groups.
kihonteki ni | 基本的に | generally, basically
oni | 鬼 | demon
~wa | は | topic marker
murenai | 群れない | do not form groups (negative of mureru)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
kiita | 聞いた | heard (past of kiku)

> Futari wo mamorinagara sannin no oni wo kiru. | ２人を守りながら３人の鬼を斬る。 | Cut down three demons while protecting two people.
futari | ２人 | two people
~wo | を | object marker
mamorinagara | 守りながら | while protecting (mamoru + nagara)
sannin | ３人 | three people
~no | の | of; links nouns
oni | 鬼 | demon
~wo | を | object marker
kiru | 斬る | to cut with a blade

> Hayaku kuwanai to koku ikkoku de aji ga ochiru n da! | 早く食わないと刻一刻で味が落ちるんだ！ | The taste gets worse by the moment if I don't eat quickly!
hayaku | 早く | early, quickly (adverbial form of hayai)
kuwanai | 食わない | do not eat (negative of rough kuu)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
koku ikkoku | 刻一刻 | moment by moment
~de | で | at, with; means marker
aji | 味 | taste
~ga | が | subject marker
ochiru | 落ちる | to fall, deteriorate
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Demo hako no naka semakunai ka? | でも箱の中狭くないか？ | Isn't it cramped in the box?
~demo | でも | even; but
hako | 箱 | box
~no | の | of; links nouns
naka | 中 | inside
semakunai | 狭くない | isn't cramped (negative of semai)
~ka | か | question marker
