# Sentences: Episode 24 | Short lines from Rehabilitation Training
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Sukoshi jikan ga kakaru mitai desu. | 少し時間がかかるみたいです。 | It seems it will take a little time.
sukoshi | 少し | a little
jikan | 時間 | time
~ga | が | subject marker
kakaru | かかる | to take (time)
mitai | みたい | like, resembling
~desu | です | is (polite ending)

> Tanoshisou de ii na. | 楽しそうでいいな。 | He's having fun. That's nice.
tanoshisou | 楽しそう | looks fun (tanoshii + sou)
~de | で | at, with; means marker
ii | いい | good
~na | な | emphasis; prohibition after a verb

> Dou desu ka? Karada no hou wa? | どうですか？体のほうは？ | How is your body feeling?
dou | どう | how
~desu ka | ですか | polite question ending
karada | 体 | body
~no | の | of; links nouns
hou | ほう | side (in a comparison)
~wa | は | topic marker

> Shinobu-san tte iu hito no oto wa dokutoku nan da yo na. | しのぶさんっていう人の音は独特なんだよな。 | The woman called Shinobu has a distinctive sound.
~Shinobu-san | しのぶさん | Ms Shinobu (name)
~tte iu | っていう | called (informal to iu)
hito | 人 | person, people
~no | の | of; links nouns
oto | 音 | sound
~wa | は | topic marker
dokutoku | 独特 | distinctive
~nan da yo na | なんだよな | informal explanatory observation

> Soshite mechakucha kawaii n da yo. | そしてめちゃくちゃかわいいんだよ。 | And she's incredibly cute.
soshite | そして | and then
mechakucha | めちゃくちゃ | extremely (informal)
kawaii | かわいい | cute
~n da yo | んだよ | emphatic explanation

> Sumimasen chotto ii desu ka? | すみませんちょっといいですか？ | Excuse me, may I have a moment?
sumimasen | すみません | excuse me (polite expression)
chotto | ちょっと | a little, briefly
ii | いい | good
~desu ka | ですか | polite question ending

> Nante koto suru n da Zen'itsu Inosuke ni ayamare! | なんてことするんだ善逸伊之助に謝れ！ | What are you doing, Zenitsu?! Apologize to Inosuke!
nante | なんて | what on earth
koto | こと | situation, thing
suru | する | to do
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending
~Zen'itsu | 善逸 | Zenitsu (name)
~Inosuke | 伊之助 | Inosuke (name)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
ayamare | 謝れ | apologize! (command of ayamaru)

> Aitsu yaru yatsu da ze. | あいつやるヤツだぜ。 | He really can deliver.
aitsu | あいつ | that guy (rough)
yaru | やる | to do, perform
yatsu | ヤツ | guy, person (rough)
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
~ze | ぜ | forceful informal emphasis

> Kanao no kami no ke ippon sura furenakatta. | カナヲの髪の毛１本すら触れなかった。 | I couldn't touch even one of Kanao's hairs.
~Kanao | カナヲ | Kanao (name)
~no | の | of; links nouns
kami no ke | 髪の毛 | hair
ippon | １本 | one long thin object
~sura | すら | even
furenakatta | 触れなかった | could not touch (past negative of fureru)

> Arigatou yatte miru yo. | ありがとうやってみるよ。 | Thanks! I'll give it a try.
arigatou | ありがとう | thank you
yatte miru | やってみる | try doing (yaru + te miru)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Ganbare ganbaru koto shika dekinai n da kara. | 頑張れ頑張ることしかできないんだから。 | Keep trying! That's all you can do.
ganbare | 頑張れ | keep trying! (command of ganbaru)
ganbaru | 頑張る | to do one's best
koto | こと | situation, thing
~shika | しか | only (with a negative)
dekinai | できない | cannot do (negative of dekiru)
~n da kara | んだから | emphatic because

> Tsuujou no hyoutan yori mo katai desu. | 通常のひょうたんよりも硬いです。 | It's harder than an ordinary gourd.
tsuujou | 通常 | ordinary, usual
~no | の | of; links nouns
hyoutan | ひょうたん | gourd
~yori | より | rather than
~mo | も | also, even
katai | 硬い | hard
~desu | です | is (polite ending)

> Kanari tairyoku ga modotte kita. | かなり体力が戻ってきた。 | I've recovered quite a lot of strength.
kanari | かなり | quite, considerably
tairyoku | 体力 | physical strength
~ga | が | subject marker
modotte kita | 戻ってきた | has returned (modoru + te kuru)

> Doushite oretachi wo koko e tsurete kite kureta n desu ka? | どうして俺たちをここへ連れてきてくれたんですか？ | Why did you bring us here?
doushite | どうして | why
oretachi | 俺たち | we (rough, masculine)
~wo | を | object marker
koko | ここ | here
~e | へ | toward
tsurete kite kureta | 連れてきてくれた | brought us (tsureru + te kuru + te kureru)
~n desu ka | んですか | polite explanatory question

> Zenshuuchuu no kokyuu ga tomattemasu yo. | 全集中の呼吸が止まってますよ。 | Your total concentration breathing has stopped.
~Zenshuuchuu no kokyuu | 全集中の呼吸 | Total Concentration Breathing (technique name)
~ga | が | subject marker
tomattemasu | 止まってます | has stopped (contracted tomatte imasu, from tomaru)
~yo | よ | emphasis
