# Sentences: Episode 11 | Short lines from Tsuzumi Mansion
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Wakatta kara mou sukoshi damattete kure. | 分かったからもう少し黙っててくれ。 | I get it, so please be quiet a little longer.
wakatta | 分かった | understood (past of wakaru)
~kara | から | because; from
mou sukoshi | もう少し | a little more
damattete kure | 黙っててくれ | please stay quiet (contracted damatte ite kure, from damaru)

> Tasukete kure! Kekkon shite kure! | 助けてくれ！結婚してくれ！ | Help me! Marry me!
tasukete kure | 助けてくれ | help me (tasukeru + te kure)
kekkon shite kure | 結婚してくれ | marry me (kekkon suru + te kure)

> Omae mitai na yatsu wa chijin ni sonzai shinai! | お前みたいなヤツは知人に存在しない！ | I don't know anyone like you!
omae | お前 | you (rough)
mitai na | みたいな | like (before a noun)
yatsu | ヤツ | guy, person (rough)
~wa | は | topic marker
chijin | 知人 | acquaintance
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
sonzai shinai | 存在しない | does not exist (negative of sonzai suru)

> Omae no sei de kekkon dekinakatta n da kara! | お前のせいで結婚できなかったんだから！ | It's your fault I couldn't get married!
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~no | の | of; links nouns
sei | せい | fault, cause of something bad
~de | で | at, with; means marker
kekkon dekinakatta | 結婚できなかった | could not marry (past negative potential of kekkon suru)
~n da kara | んだから | emphatic because

> Nande sonna ni haji wo sarasu n da? | なんでそんなに恥をさらすんだ？ | Why do you make such a spectacle of yourself?
nande | なんで | why (informal)
sonna ni | そんなに | so much
haji | 恥 | shame
~wo | を | object marker
sarasu | さらす | to expose, display
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Saishuu Senbetsu de shineru to omotta noni sa. | 最終選別で死ねると思ったのにさ。 | I thought I could die in the final selection.
~Saishuu Senbetsu | 最終選別 | Final Selection (exam name)
~de | で | at, with; means marker
shineru | 死ねる | can die (potential of shinu)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
omotta | 思った | thought (past of omou)
~noni sa | のにさ | even though (informal)

> Nanika taberu mono mottenai no ka? | 何か食べるもの持ってないのか？ | Don't you have anything to eat?
nanika | 何か | something
taberu | 食べる | to eat
mono | もの | thing, possession
mottenai | 持ってない | do not have (contracted motte inai, from motsu)
~no ka | のか | explanatory question

> Suzume wo komarasetara dame da. | スズメを困らせたらダメだ。 | Don't give the sparrow trouble.
suzume | スズメ | sparrow
~wo | を | object marker
komarasetara | 困らせたら | if you trouble it (conditional of causative komaraseru, from komaru)
dame | ダメ | not allowed, no good
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Oni no nioi ga sukoshi zutsu tsuyoku natteru. | 鬼のにおいが少しずつ強くなってる。 | The demon's scent is gradually getting stronger.
oni | 鬼 | demon
~no | の | of; links nouns
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~ga | が | subject marker
sukoshi zutsu | 少しずつ | little by little
tsuyoku | 強く | strongly (adverbial form of tsuyoi)
natteru | なってる | is becoming (contracted natte iru, from naru)

> Oshiete kure. Nanika atta no ka? | 教えてくれ。何かあったのか？ | Tell me, did something happen?
oshiete kure | 教えてくれ | tell me (oshieru + te kure)
nanika | 何か | something
atta | あった | happened (past of aru)
~no ka | のか | explanatory question

> Futari de ato wo tsuketa no ka? | ２人で後をつけたのか？ | Did the two of you follow them?
futari | ２人 | two people
~de | で | at, with; means marker
ato | 後 | behind, afterward
~wo | を | object marker
tsuketa | つけた | followed (past of tsukeru in ato wo tsukeru)
~no ka | のか | explanatory question

> Modotte kitara kanarazu maisou shimasu. | 戻ってきたら必ず埋葬します。 | I'll make sure to bury him when I get back.
modotte kitara | 戻ってきたら | when I return (modoru + conditional of te kuru)
kanarazu | 必ず | without fail
maisou shimasu | 埋葬します | will bury (polite maisou suru)

> Murijii suru tsumori wa nai. | 無理強いするつもりはない。 | I don't intend to force you.
murijii suru | 無理強いする | to force someone against their will
tsumori | つもり | intention
~wa | は | topic marker
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist

> Mae no tatakai de ore wa abara to ashi ga orete iru. | 前の戦いで俺はあばらと脚が折れている。 | My ribs and leg were broken in the last fight.
mae | 前 | before
~no | の | of; links nouns
tatakai | 戦い | fight
~de | で | at, with; means marker
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
abara | あばら | ribs
~to | と | with; quotation marker
ashi | 脚 | leg
~ga | が | subject marker
orete iru | 折れている | are broken (oreru + te iru)

> Sagatte tana no ushiro ni kakureru n da. | 下がって棚の後ろに隠れるんだ。 | Step back and hide behind the shelves.
sagatte | 下がって | step back (te-form of sagaru)
tana | 棚 | shelf
~no | の | of; links nouns
ushiro | 後ろ | behind
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
kakureru | 隠れる | to hide oneself
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending
