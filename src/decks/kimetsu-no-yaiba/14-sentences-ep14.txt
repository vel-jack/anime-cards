# Sentences: Episode 14 | Short lines from The House with the Wisteria Family Crest
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Iya mattaku wakattenai kanji ga suru! | いや全く分かってない感じがする！ | No, I feel like he doesn't understand at all!
iya | いや | no, well
mattaku | 全く | completely, at all (with a negative)
wakattenai | 分かってない | does not understand (contracted wakatte inai, from wakaru)
kanji | 感じ | feeling, impression
~ga | が | subject marker
suru | する | to do

> Shisokujuu to tatakatte iru you da! | 四足獣と戦っているようだ！ | It's like fighting a four-legged beast!
shisokujuu | 四足獣 | four-legged beast
~to | と | with; quotation marker
tatakatte iru | 戦っている | am fighting (tatakau + te iru)
~you da | ようだ | it is like

> Ima kono setsuna no yuetsu ni masaru mono nashi! | 今この刹那の愉悦に勝るものなし！ | Nothing beats the joy of this very instant!
ima | 今 | now
kono | この | this (before a noun)
setsuna | 刹那 | instant
~no | の | of; links nouns
yuetsu | 愉悦 | delight
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
masaru | 勝る | to surpass
mono | もの | thing, possession
nashi | なし | there is none (literary nai)

> Dame da mou kakatte ikanai! | ダメだもうかかっていかない！ | No! I'm not going to attack him again!
dame | ダメ | not allowed, no good
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
mou | もう | already; any more
kakatte ikanai | かかっていかない | will not go attack (kakaru + negative of te iku)

> Shindenai. Tabun noushintou da. | 死んでない。多分脳震盪だ。 | He's not dead. It's probably a concussion.
shindenai | 死んでない | is not dead (contracted shinde inai, from shinu)
tabun | 多分 | probably
noushintou | 脳震盪 | concussion
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Inosuke mo tetsudatte kure. | 伊之助も手伝ってくれ。 | Inosuke, help us too.
~Inosuke | 伊之助 | Inosuke (name)
~mo | も | also, even
tetsudatte kure | 手伝ってくれ | help me (tetsudau + te kure)

> Sonna koto yori ore to tatakae! | そんなことより俺と戦え！ | Forget that and fight me!
sonna | そんな | that kind of
koto | こと | situation, thing
~yori | より | rather than
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
tatakae | 戦え | fight! (command of tatakau)

> Kizu ga itamu kara dekinai n da na? | 傷が痛むからできないんだな？ | You can't because your wounds hurt, right?
kizu | 傷 | wound
~ga | が | subject marker
itamu | 痛む | to ache
~kara | から | because; from
dekinai | できない | cannot do (negative of dekiru)
~n da na | んだな | confirming an understanding

> Tsuchi wo hotte maisou suru no wa hontou ni taihen da shi. | 土を掘って埋葬するのは本当に大変だし。 | Digging a hole and burying them is really hard.
tsuchi | 土 | earth, soil
~wo | を | object marker
hotte | 掘って | dig (te-form of horu)
maisou suru | 埋葬する | to bury
~no | の | nominalizer: turns a clause into a noun
~wa | は | topic marker
hontou ni | 本当に | really
taihen | 大変 | difficult, demanding
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
~shi | し | and; gives a reason

> Muri itte sumanakatta na. | 無理言ってすまなかったな。 | Sorry for asking too much of you.
muri | 無理 | unreasonable demand
itte | 言って | say (te-form of iu)
sumanakatta | すまなかった | was sorry (past of sumanai)
~na | な | emphasis; prohibition after a verb

> Ore wo hotte ikanaide! | 俺を放っていかないで！ | Don't leave me behind!
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wo | を | object marker
hotte ikanaide | 放っていかないで | don't leave behind (houru + negative request of te iku)

> Ore wa kanarazu suki wo mite omae ni katsu zo! | 俺は必ず隙を見てお前に勝つぞ！ | I'll catch an opening and beat you for sure!
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
kanarazu | 必ず | without fail
suki | 隙 | opening, gap in defense
~wo | を | object marker
mite | 見て | see, watch for (te-form of miru)
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
katsu | 勝つ | to win
~zo | ぞ | forceful emphasis

> Abara yori kobu ga itee. | あばらよりコブが痛え。 | My bump hurts more than my ribs.
abara | あばら | ribs
~yori | より | rather than
kobu | コブ | bump, swelling
~ga | が | subject marker
itee | 痛え | hurts (rough itai)

> Ore ni wa oya mo kyoudai mo inee ze. | 俺には親も兄弟もいねえぜ。 | I don't have any parents or siblings.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~wa | は | topic marker
oya | 親 | parent
~mo | も | also, even
kyoudai | 兄弟 | siblings
~mo | も | also, even
inee | いねえ | there aren't (rough inai, negative of iru)
~ze | ぜ | forceful informal emphasis

> Oni wo tsurete iru no wa dou iu koto nan da? | 鬼を連れているのはどういうことなんだ？ | What do you mean, you're traveling with a demon?
oni | 鬼 | demon
~wo | を | object marker
tsurete iru | 連れている | are taking along (tsureru + te iru)
~no | の | nominalizer: turns a clause into a noun
~wa | は | topic marker
dou iu | どういう | what kind of
koto | こと | situation, thing
~nan da | なんだ | emphatic explanation
