# Sentences: Episode 17 | Short lines from You Must Master a Single Thing
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Nanka chikutto shita. | なんかチクッとした。 | Something just stung me.
nanka | なんか | somehow (informal)
chikutto | チクッと | with a prick or sting
shita | した | did (past of suru)

> Kumo ga kasakasa suru oto sugoi kimochi warui shi! | 蜘蛛がカサカサする音すごい気持ち悪いし！ | I hate the sound of spiders scurrying!
kumo | 蜘蛛 | spider
~ga | が | subject marker
kasakasa suru | カサカサする | to rustle, scurry
oto | 音 | sound
sugoi | すごい | amazing
kimochi warui | 気持ち悪い | creepy, unpleasant
~shi | し | and; gives a reason

> Ningen ga kumo ni sareten no? | 人間が蜘蛛にされてんの？ | Are humans being turned into spiders?
ningen | 人間 | human, human being
~ga | が | subject marker
kumo | 蜘蛛 | spider
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
sareten no | されてんの | are being made? (contracted sarete iru no, passive of suru)

> Nigete mo muda da ze omae wa mou maketeru. | 逃げてもムダだぜお前はもう負けてる。 | There's no point running. You've already lost.
nigete mo | 逃げても | even if you run (nigeru + te mo)
muda | ムダ | waste, uselessness
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
~ze | ぜ | forceful informal emphasis
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~wa | は | topic marker
mou | もう | already; any more
maketeru | 負けてる | have lost (contracted makete iru, from makeru)

> Doku da yo. Kamareta ro kumo ni? | 毒だよ。かまれたろ蜘蛛に？ | It's poison. A spider bit you, right?
doku | 毒 | poison
~da yo | だよ | emphatic plain ending
kamareta | かまれた | were bitten (past passive of kamu)
~ro | ろ | shortened darou, right?
kumo | 蜘蛛 | spider
~ni | に | to, at; target marker

> Anna fuu ni naritakunai! | あんなふうになりたくない！ | I don't want to end up like that!
anna | あんな | like that over there
fuu | ふう | manner, way
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
naritakunai | なりたくない | do not want to become (negative desire of naru)

> Shini wa sen kono teido de! | 死にはせんこの程度で！ | This much won't kill you!
shini | 死に | die (stem of shinu)
~wa | は | topic marker
~sen | せん | will not do (old-fashioned negative of suru)
kono | この | this (before a noun)
teido | 程度 | degree, extent
~de | で | at, with; means marker

> Shakkin mamire ni natta ore wo tasukete kureta shi ne. | 借金まみれになった俺を助けてくれたしね。 | He saved me when I was buried in debt.
shakkin mamire | 借金まみれ | buried in debt
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
natta | なった | became (past of naru)
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wo | を | object marker
tasukete kureta | 助けてくれた | helped me (tasukeru + past of te kureru)
~shi | し | and; gives a reason
~ne | ね | seeks agreement

> Jiichan ni kakurete shugyou mo shiten da yo! | じいちゃんに隠れて修行もしてんだよ！ | I train when Grandpa isn't looking, too!
jiichan | じいちゃん | Grandpa (familiar)
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
kakurete | 隠れて | hidden from (te-form of kakureru)
shugyou | 修行 | training, discipline
~mo | も | also, even
shiten da yo | してんだよ | am doing (contracted shite iru n da yo, from suru)

> Sakki kara zutto onaji kamae wo nando mo shite iru. | さっきからずっと同じ構えを何度もしている。 | He keeps taking the same stance over and over.
sakki | さっき | a little while ago
~kara | から | because; from
zutto | ずっと | all along, continuously
onaji | 同じ | same
kamae | 構え | stance
~wo | を | object marker
nando mo | 何度も | many times
shite iru | している | are doing (suru + te iru)

> Hitotsu dekireba banzai da. | １つできれば万々歳だ。 | Knowing one thing is reason enough to celebrate.
hitotsu | １つ | one thing
dekireba | できれば | if you can do (conditional of dekiru)
banzai | 万々歳 | a great success
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Katana no uchikata wo shitteru ka? | 刀の打ち方を知ってるか？ | Do you know how a sword is forged?
katana | 刀 | sword
~no | の | of; links nouns
uchikata | 打ち方 | way of forging (utsu + kata)
~wo | を | object marker
shitteru | 知ってる | know (contracted shitte iru, from shiru)
~ka | か | question marker

> Dakara jiichan wa ore no koto mainichi buttataku no ka yo? | だからじいちゃんは俺のこと毎日ぶったたくのかよ？ | Is that why Grandpa beats me every day?
dakara | だから | so, that is why
jiichan | じいちゃん | Grandpa (familiar)
~wa | は | topic marker
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~no | の | of; links nouns
koto | こと | situation, thing
mainichi | 毎日 | every day
buttataku | ぶったたく | to beat, strike hard
~no ka yo | のかよ | emphatic question

> Naite ii nigete mo ii. | 泣いていい逃げてもいい。 | You can cry, and you can run away.
naite | 泣いて | cry (te-form of naku)
ii | いい | good
nigete mo | 逃げても | even if you run (nigeru + te mo)
ii | いい | good

> Dare mo nozonde wa kurenai. | 誰も望んではくれない。 | No one wishes that for me.
dare mo | 誰も | anyone (with a negative: no one)
nozonde wa kurenai | 望んではくれない | will not wish it for me (nozomu + negative te kureru)
