# Sentences: Episode 10 | Short lines from Together Forever
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Konna tokoro de yarareru na! | こんな所でやられるな！ | Don't let yourself be beaten here!
konna | こんな | this kind of
tokoro | 所 | place
~de | で | at, with; means marker
yarareru na | やられるな | don't be defeated (passive yarareru, from yaru, + prohibitive na)

> Sugu iku kara buji de ite kure. | すぐ行くから無事でいてくれ。 | I'll be there soon, so stay safe.
sugu | すぐ | soon, immediately
iku | 行く | to go
~kara | から | because; from
buji | 無事 | safe, unharmed
~de | で | at, with; means marker
ite kure | いてくれ | please remain (iru + te kure)

> Sore wa kochira mo onaji koto ja. | それはこちらも同じことじゃ。 | It's just the same for us.
sore | それ | that
~wa | は | topic marker
kochira | こちら | our side, here (polite)
~mo | も | also, even
onaji | 同じ | same
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ja | じゃ | is (old-fashioned da)

> Watashi no mari wo kerikaesu you ni natte kita to wa… | 私の毬を蹴り返すようになってきたとは… | She's started kicking my ball back at me.
watashi | 私 | I, me
~no | の | of; links nouns
mari | 毬 | traditional ball
~wo | を | object marker
kerikaesu | 蹴り返す | to kick back
you ni natte kita | ようになってきた | has started to (you ni naru + te kuru)
~to wa | とは | surprised realization

> Nezuko-san jishin no chikara desu. | 禰豆子さん自身の力です。 | That's Nezuko's own strength.
~Nezuko-san | 禰豆子さん | Nezuko (name, respectful)
jishin | 自身 | oneself, one's own
~no | の | of; links nouns
chikara | 力 | strength, power
~desu | です | is (polite ending)

> Shikashi aite mo tsuwamono. | しかし相手もつわもの。 | But her opponent is formidable too.
shikashi | しかし | however
aite | 相手 | opponent
~mo | も | also, even
tsuwamono | つわもの | formidable fighter

> Anata wa Kibutsuji no shoutai wo gozonji na no desu ka? | あなたは鬼舞辻の正体をご存じなのですか？ | Do you know Kibutsuji's true identity?
anata | あなた | you
~wa | は | topic marker
~Kibutsuji | 鬼舞辻 | Kibutsuji (name)
~no | の | of; links nouns
shoutai | 正体 | true identity
~wo | を | object marker
gozonji | ご存じ | knowing (respectful)
~na no desu ka | なのですか | polite explanatory question

> Watashi wa Nezuko-san wo mimasu. | 私は禰豆子さんを診ます。 | I'll examine Nezuko.
watashi | 私 | I, me
~wa | は | topic marker
~Nezuko-san | 禰豆子さん | Nezuko (name, respectful)
~wo | を | object marker
mimasu | 診ます | will examine medically (polite miru)

> Ore wa Tamayo-sama kara hanaretakunai. | 俺は珠世様から離れたくない。 | I don't want to leave Lady Tamayo.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
~Tamayo-sama | 珠世様 | Lady Tamayo (name, respectful)
~kara | から | because; from
hanaretakunai | 離れたくない | do not want to leave (negative desire of hanareru)

> Hikari ga todokanai kono chika ni idou shimashita. | 光が届かないこの地下に移動しました。 | We moved down into this basement where light can't reach.
hikari | 光 | light
~ga | が | subject marker
todokanai | 届かない | does not reach (negative of todoku)
kono | この | this (before a noun)
chika | 地下 | underground, basement
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
idou shimashita | 移動しました | moved (polite past of idou suru)

> Demo Nezuko wa ofutari wo ningen da to handan shitemasu. | でも禰豆子はお二人を人間だと判断してます。 | But Nezuko regards both of you as human.
~demo | でも | even; but
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
~wa | は | topic marker
ofutari | お二人 | the two of you (polite)
~wo | を | object marker
ningen | 人間 | human, human being
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
handan shitemasu | 判断してます | judges (contracted handan shite imasu, from handan suru)

> Anata wa sore demo ikitai to omoimasu ka? | あなたはそれでも生きたいと思いますか？ | Do you still want to live?
anata | あなた | you
~wa | は | topic marker
sore demo | それでも | even so
ikitai | 生きたい | want to live (desire form of ikiru)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
omoimasu | 思います | think (polite omou)
~ka | か | question marker

> Desu ga hito de nakunaru koto wa tsuraku kanashii. | ですが人でなくなることはつらく悲しい。 | But ceasing to be human is painful and sad.
~desu ga | ですが | however (polite)
hito | 人 | person, people
~de | で | at, with; means marker
nakunaru | なくなる | to cease to be
koto | こと | situation, thing
~wa | は | topic marker
tsuraku | つらく | painful (linking form of tsurai)
kanashii | 悲しい | sad

> Zettai ni anzen to wa iikiremasen ga. | 絶対に安全とは言い切れませんが。 | I can't guarantee it's completely safe, though.
zettai ni | 絶対に | absolutely, under any circumstances
anzen | 安全 | safe
~to wa | とは | surprised realization
iikiremasen | 言い切れません | cannot state with certainty (polite negative potential of iikiru)
~ga | が | subject marker

> Sore dewa Tamayo-san mo Yushirou-san mo ogenki de. | それでは珠世さんも愈史郎さんもお元気で。 | Please keep well, both of you.
sore dewa | それでは | well then
~Tamayo-san | 珠世さん | Ms Tamayo (name)
~mo | も | also, even
~Yushirou-san | 愈史郎さん | Mr Yushirou (name)
~mo | も | also, even
ogenki de | お元気で | take care, keep well
