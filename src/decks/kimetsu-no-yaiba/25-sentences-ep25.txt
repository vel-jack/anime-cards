# Sentences: Episode 25 | Short lines from Tsuguko, Kanao Tsuyuri
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Mou ikkai onegai shimasu! | もう１回お願いします！ | One more time, please!
mou ikkai | もう１回 | one more time
onegai shimasu | お願いします | please (polite request from onegai suru)

> Maa watashi wa kamaimasen ga. | まあ私はかまいませんが。 | Well, I don't mind.
maa | まあ | well
watashi | 私 | I, me
~wa | は | topic marker
kamaimasen | かまいません | it does not matter (polite negative of kamau)
~ga | が | subject marker

> Kiso tairyoku ga hiyakuteki ni agarimasu. | 基礎体力が飛躍的に上がります。 | Your basic physical strength will improve dramatically.
kiso tairyoku | 基礎体力 | basic physical strength
~ga | が | subject marker
hiyakuteki ni | 飛躍的に | dramatically
agarimasu | 上がります | will rise (polite agaru)

> Ato wa shinu hodo kitaeru! | あとは死ぬほど鍛える！ | After that, train like your life depends on it!
ato | あと | afterward
~wa | は | topic marker
shinu hodo | 死ぬほど | to an extreme degree (literally enough to die)
kitaeru | 鍛える | to train, strengthen

> Inosuke-kun nara kantan ka to omotte ita no desu ga. | 伊之助君なら簡単かと思っていたのですが。 | I thought it would be easy for you, Inosuke.
~Inosuke-kun | 伊之助君 | Inosuke (name)
~nara | なら | if
kantan | 簡単 | easy
~ka | か | question marker
~to | と | with; quotation marker
omotte ita | 思っていた | had thought (omou + past te iru)
~no desu ga | のですが | polite lead-in

> Ichiban ouen shite imasu yo. | いちばん応援していますよ。 | I'm cheering for you the most.
ichiban | いちばん | most, number one
ouen shite imasu | 応援しています | am cheering for you (polite ouen suru + te iru)
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Nani mo tsuraku nakunatta. | 何もつらくなくなった。 | Nothing hurt anymore.
nani mo | 何も | anything (with a negative: nothing)
tsuraku | つらく | painful (adverbial form of tsurai)
nakunatta | なくなった | ceased to be (past of nakunaru)

> Zainin ka nanika na no desu ka? | 罪人か何かなのですか？ | Is she a criminal or something?
zainin | 罪人 | criminal
~ka | か | question marker
nanika | 何か | something
~na no desu ka | なのですか | polite explanatory question

> Iwarenai to nani mo dekinai no shokuji mo sou yo. | 言われないと何もできないの食事もそうよ。 | She can't do anything unless she's told, even eat.
iwarenai | 言われない | is not told (negative passive of iu)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
nani mo | 何も | anything (with a negative: nothing)
dekinai | できない | cannot do (negative of dekiru)
~no | の | of; links nouns
shokuji | 食事 | meal, eating
~mo | も | also, even
sou | そう | so, like that
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Hitori no toki wa kono kouka wo nagete kimetara ii wa yo. | 一人のときはこの硬貨を投げて決めたらいいわよ。 | When you're alone, you can toss this coin to decide.
hitori | 一人 | one person, alone
~no | の | of; links nouns
toki | とき | time, occasion
~wa | は | topic marker
kono | この | this (before a noun)
kouka | 硬貨 | coin
~wo | を | object marker
nagete | 投げて | throw (te-form of nageru)
kimetara | 決めたら | if you decide (conditional of kimeru)
ii | いい | good
~wa yo | わよ | gentle emphasis

> Ore mo shinisou datta shi aite mo sugoku tsuyokutte… | 俺も死にそうだったし相手もすごく強くって… | I almost died too, and my opponent was incredibly strong.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~mo | も | also, even
shinisou | 死にそう | looks about to die (shinu + sou)
~datta | だった | was (past of da)
~shi | し | and; gives a reason
aite | 相手 | opponent
~mo | も | also, even
sugoku | すごく | very (adverbial form of sugoi)
tsuyokutte | 強くって | was strong, and (emphatic te-form of tsuyoi)

> Ore no karada wa kawatta. Hayaku katana wo furitai! | 俺の体は変わった。早く刀を振りたい！ | My body has changed. I want to swing my sword soon!
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~no | の | of; links nouns
karada | 体 | body
~wa | は | topic marker
kawatta | 変わった | changed (past of kawaru)
hayaku | 早く | early, quickly (adverbial form of hayai)
katana | 刀 | sword
~wo | を | object marker
furitai | 振りたい | want to swing (desire form of furu)

> Karada wa mou daijoubu desu kara anshin shite ninmu ni maishin shite kudasai. | 体はもう大丈夫ですから安心して任務に邁進してください。 | You're physically fine now, so devote yourself to your missions.
karada | 体 | body
~wa | は | topic marker
mou | もう | already; any more
daijoubu | 大丈夫 | all right
~desu | です | is (polite ending)
~kara | から | because; from
anshin shite | 安心して | relax (te-form of anshin suru)
ninmu | 任務 | mission
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
maishin shite | 邁進して | push forward devotedly (te-form of maishin suru)
kudasai | ください | please do (request form of kudasaru)

> Shitte iru koto ga aru kamo shiremasen ga. | 知っていることがあるかもしれませんが。 | There might be something he knows.
shitte iru | 知っている | know (shiru + te iru)
koto | こと | situation, thing
~ga | が | subject marker
aru | ある | to exist, be present
~kamo shiremasen | かもしれません | might (polite kamo shirenai)
~ga | が | subject marker

> Arigatou Nezuko hagemashite kureta n da na. | ありがとう禰豆子励ましてくれたんだな。 | Thanks, Nezuko. You were encouraging me.
arigatou | ありがとう | thank you
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
hagemashite kureta | 励ましてくれた | encouraged me (hagemasu + past te kureru)
~n da na | んだな | confirming an understanding
