# Sentences: Episode 2 | Short lines from Trainer Sakonji Urokodaki
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Imouto ga mogura mitai ni natte shimatta. | 妹がモグラみたいになってしまった。 | I can't believe my sister has turned into a mole.
imouto | 妹 | younger sister
~ga | が | subject marker
mogura | モグラ | mole (animal)
mitai | みたい | like, resembling
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
natte shimatta | なってしまった | ended up becoming (naru + te shimau)

> Kono yama wa michi ga kewashii kara dareka kega wo shita n da! | この山は道が険しいから誰かケガをしたんだ！ | This mountain path is steep, so someone must be hurt!
kono | この | this (before a noun)
yama | 山 | mountain
~wa | は | topic marker
michi | 道 | path, road
~ga | が | subject marker
kewashii | 険しい | steep, rugged
~kara | から | because; from
dareka | 誰か | someone
kega | ケガ | injury
~wo | を | object marker
shita | した | did (past of suru)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Nande oni to ningen ga tsurunderu n da? | なんで鬼と人間がつるんでるんだ？ | Why is a demon hanging around with a human?!
nande | なんで | why (informal)
oni | 鬼 | demon
~to | と | with; quotation marker
ningen | 人間 | human, human being
~ga | が | subject marker
tsurunderu | つるんでる | hanging around together (contracted tsurunde iru, from tsurumu)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Kono hito ashioto ga shinakatta. | この人足音がしなかった。 | I didn't hear this person's footsteps.
kono | この | this (before a noun)
hito | 人 | person, people
ashioto | 足音 | footsteps
~ga | が | subject marker
shinakatta | しなかった | did not make (past negative of suru)

> Hito ni kiku na. | 人に聞くな。 | Don't ask someone else.
hito | 人 | person, people
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
kiku na | 聞くな | don't ask (kiku + prohibitive na)

> Oni ni sura doujoushin wo motte iru. | 鬼にすら同情心を持っている。 | He even feels compassion for demons.
oni | 鬼 | demon
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~sura | すら | even
doujoushin | 同情心 | compassion
~wo | を | object marker
motte iru | 持っている | has (motsu + te iru)

> Omae wa tonikaku handan ga osoi. | お前はとにかく判断が遅い。 | You're slow to make decisions, above all.
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~wa | は | topic marker
tonikaku | とにかく | anyway, above all
handan | 判断 | judgment, decision
~ga | が | subject marker
osoi | 遅い | slow, late

> Tsumi naki hito no inochi wo omae no imouto ga ubau. | 罪なき人の命をお前の妹が奪う。 | Your sister will take an innocent person's life.
tsumi naki | 罪なき | innocent (literary negative of tsumi aru)
hito | 人 | person, people
~no | の | of; links nouns
inochi | 命 | life
~wo | を | object marker
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~no | の | of; links nouns
imouto | 妹 | younger sister
~ga | が | subject marker
ubau | 奪う | to take away

> Mata kimono wo naoshiteru no? | また着物を直してるの？ | Are you mending your kimono again?
mata | また | again
kimono | 着物 | kimono
~wo | を | object marker
naoshiteru | 直してる | mending (contracted naoshite iru, from naosu)
~no | の | question or explanatory ending

> Kitto itsuka kirei na kimono wo katte yaru! | きっといつかきれいな着物を買ってやる！ | I'll buy you a beautiful kimono someday!
kitto | きっと | surely
itsuka | いつか | someday
kirei na | きれいな | beautiful (kirei + na)
kimono | 着物 | kimono
~wo | を | object marker
katte yaru | 買ってやる | will buy for you (kau + te yaru)

> Imouto wa washi ga sekinin wo motte mite oku. | 妹はわしが責任を持って見ておく。 | I will take responsibility for looking after your sister.
imouto | 妹 | younger sister
~wa | は | topic marker
washi | わし | I (elderly speech)
~ga | が | subject marker
sekinin | 責任 | responsibility
~wo | を | object marker
motte | 持って | take, hold (te-form of motsu)
mite oku | 見ておく | look after for now (miru + te oku)

> Yoru ga akeru mae ni modoreba ii n da na. | 夜が明ける前に戻ればいいんだな。 | I just need to get back before daybreak.
yoru | 夜 | night
~ga | が | subject marker
akeru | 明ける | to dawn
mae | 前 | before
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
modoreba | 戻れば | if I return (conditional of modoru)
ii | いい | good
~n da na | んだな | confirming an understanding

> Kokyuu wo totonoete wana no nioi wo kagiwakero. | 呼吸を整えて罠のにおいを嗅ぎ分けろ。 | Steady your breathing and distinguish the scent of the traps.
kokyuu | 呼吸 | breathing
~wo | を | object marker
totonoete | 整えて | steady (te-form of totonoeru)
wana | 罠 | trap
~no | の | of; links nouns
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~wo | を | object marker
kagiwakero | 嗅ぎ分けろ | distinguish by smell! (command of kagiwakeru)

> Douka sodatete itadakitai. | どうか育てていただきたい。 | Please train him.
douka | どうか | please (earnest request)
sodatete | 育てて | raise, train (te-form of sodateru)
itadakitai | いただきたい | would like you to (humble request, from itadaku)

> Banmeshi wa nikujaga to oden docchi ga ii? | 晩飯は肉じゃがとおでんどっちがいい？ | Which would you like for dinner, meat and potatoes or oden?
banmeshi | 晩飯 | dinner (informal)
~wa | は | topic marker
nikujaga | 肉じゃが | meat and potato stew
~to | と | with; quotation marker
oden | おでん | oden (simmered dish)
docchi | どっち | which (informal)
~ga | が | subject marker
ii | いい | good
