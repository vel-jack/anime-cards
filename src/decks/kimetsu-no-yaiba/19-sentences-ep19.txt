# Sentences: Episode 19 | Short lines from Hinokami
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Hitotachi no iryoku ga chigau tenchi hodo no sa ga aru. | ひと太刀の威力が違う天地ほどの差がある。 | The force of a single stroke is worlds apart.
hitotachi | ひと太刀 | one sword stroke
~no | の | of; links nouns
iryoku | 威力 | power, force
~ga | が | subject marker
chigau | 違う | to differ
tenchi hodo | 天地ほど | as much as heaven and earth
~no | の | of; links nouns
sa | 差 | difference
~ga | が | subject marker
aru | ある | to exist, be present

> Nan da kore? Shibarareteru. | 何だこれ？縛られてる。 | What is this? I'm tied up.
nan da | 何だ | what is it (nani + da)
kore | これ | this
shibarareteru | 縛られてる | am tied up (contracted shibararete iru, passive of shibaru)

> Daijoubu da minna ikiteru! | 大丈夫だみんな生きてる！ | It's all right. Everyone's alive!
daijoubu | 大丈夫 | all right
~da | だ | is (plain ending)
minna | みんな | everyone
ikiteru | 生きてる | are alive (contracted ikite iru, from ikiru)

> Kizu ga fukai. Hidari tekubi ga chigiresou da. | 傷が深い。左手首がちぎれそうだ。 | The wound is deep. My left wrist is almost torn off.
kizu | 傷 | wound
~ga | が | subject marker
fukai | 深い | deep
hidari tekubi | 左手首 | left wrist
~ga | が | subject marker
chigiresou | ちぎれそう | looks about to tear off (chigireru + sou)
~da | だ | is (plain ending)

> Neesan wo sutenaide! | 姉さんを捨てないで！ | Don't abandon your sister!
neesan | 姉さん | older sister (familiar)
~wo | を | object marker
sutenaide | 捨てないで | don't abandon (negative request of suteru)

> Sou shitara sakki no koto mo yurushite yaru. | そうしたらさっきのことも許してやる。 | Then I'll forgive what happened earlier, too.
sou shitara | そうしたら | then, if so
sakki | さっき | a little while ago
~no | の | of; links nouns
koto | こと | situation, thing
~mo | も | also, even
yurushite yaru | 許してやる | will forgive you (yurusu + te yaru)

> Nani wo itteru no ka wakaranai. | 何を言ってるのか分からない。 | I don't understand what you're saying.
nani | 何 | what
~wo | を | object marker
itteru | 言ってる | are saying (contracted itte iru, from iu)
~no | の | nominalizer: turns a clause into a noun
~ka | か | question marker
wakaranai | 分からない | do not understand (negative of wakaru)

> Jibun no omoi mo ishi mo aru n da. | 自分の思いも意思もあるんだ。 | She has her own feelings and will.
jibun | 自分 | oneself
~no | の | of; links nouns
omoi | 思い | feelings, thoughts
~mo | も | also, even
ishi | 意思 | will, intention
~mo | も | also, even
aru | ある | to exist, be present
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Sakarau to dou naru ka chanto oshieru. | 逆らうとどうなるかちゃんと教える。 | I'll teach you exactly what happens when you defy me.
sakarau | 逆らう | to defy
~to | と | with; quotation marker
dou | どう | how
naru | なる | to become
~ka | か | question marker
chanto | ちゃんと | properly
oshieru | 教える | to teach

> Nezuko wo omae nanka ni watasanai. | 禰豆子をお前なんかに渡さない。 | I won't hand Nezuko over to the likes of you.
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
~wo | を | object marker
omae | お前 | you (rough)
~nanka ni | なんかに | to someone like (dismissive)
watasanai | 渡さない | will not hand over (negative of watasu)

> Dekiru nara yatte goran. | できるならやってごらん。 | Try it, if you can.
dekiru | できる | to be able to
~nara | なら | if
yatte goran | やってごらん | try doing it (yaru + te goran)

> Sore ga dekinai nara shinu shika nai yo. | それができないなら死ぬしかないよ。 | If you can't do that, you have no choice but to die.
sore | それ | that
~ga | が | subject marker
dekinai | できない | cannot do (negative of dekiru)
~nara | なら | if
shinu | 死ぬ | to die
~shika nai | しかない | have no choice but
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Urusai yo sukoshi damatte iro. | うるさいよ少し黙っていろ。 | You're noisy. Be quiet for a while.
urusai | うるさい | noisy, annoying
~yo | よ | emphasis
sukoshi | 少し | a little
damatte iro | 黙っていろ | stay quiet! (damaru + command of te iru)

> Ochitsuke. Kanjouteki ni naru na. | 落ち着け。感情的になるな。 | Calm down. Don't get emotional.
ochitsuke | 落ち着け | calm down! (command of ochitsuku)
kanjouteki | 感情的 | emotional
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
naru na | なるな | don't become (naru + prohibitive na)

> Kono mama kyori wo tsumete ikeba kateru! | このまま距離を詰めていけば勝てる！ | I can win if I keep closing the distance!
kono mama | このまま | like this, as things are
kyori | 距離 | distance
~wo | を | object marker
tsumete ikeba | 詰めていけば | if I keep closing (tsumeru + conditional of te iku)
kateru | 勝てる | can win (potential of katsu)
