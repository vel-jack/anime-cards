# Sentences: Episode 13 | Short lines from Something More Important Than Life
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Ore wa ima made yoku yatte kita. | 俺は今までよくやってきた。 | I've done well up to now.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
ima made | 今まで | up to now
yoku | よく | well
yatte kita | やってきた | have done all along (yaru + te kuru)

> Kiai to tomo ni atama mo tsukau n da! | 気合いと共に頭も使うんだ！ | Use your head as well as your fighting spirit!
kiai | 気合い | fighting spirit
~to | と | with; quotation marker
tomo ni | 共に | together with
atama | 頭 | head
~mo | も | also, even
tsukau | 使う | to use
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Hayaku marechi wo kuwaneba naran to iu noni… | 早く稀血を食わねばならんというのに… | I need to eat the rare blood quickly, but...
hayaku | 早く | early, quickly (adverbial form of hayai)
marechi | 稀血 | rare blood
~wo | を | object marker
kuwaneba naran | 食わねばならん | must eat (kuu + old-fashioned negative obligation)
~to iu noni | というのに | even though

> Tsumaranai n da yo kimi no kakimono. | つまらないんだよ君の書き物。 | Your writing is boring.
tsumaranai | つまらない | boring
~n da yo | んだよ | emphatic explanation
kimi | 君 | you
~no | の | of; links nouns
kakimono | 書き物 | writing

> Utsukushisa mo hakanasa mo sugomi mo nai. | 美しさも儚さもすごみもない。 | There's no beauty, no fleeting sadness, no intensity.
utsukushisa | 美しさ | beauty
~mo | も | also, even
hakanasa | 儚さ | transience, fragility
~mo | も | also, even
sugomi | すごみ | intensity, menace
~mo | も | also, even
nai | ない | there is no, does not exist

> Saikin wa hiruma mattaku soto ni dete konai shi. | 最近は昼間全く外に出てこないし。 | Lately you never come outside during the day.
saikin | 最近 | lately
~wa | は | topic marker
hiruma | 昼間 | daytime
mattaku | 全く | completely, at all (with a negative)
soto | 外 | outside
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
dete konai | 出てこない | do not come out (deru + negative of te kuru)
~shi | し | and; gives a reason

> Moshikashite mada hayaku tsuzumi wo uteru no ka? | もしかしてまだ速く鼓を打てるのか？ | Could he be able to beat the drum even faster?
moshikashite | もしかして | perhaps, could it be
mada | まだ | still, yet
hayaku | 速く | quickly (adverbial form of hayai)
tsuzumi | 鼓 | hand drum
~wo | を | object marker
uteru | 打てる | can strike (potential of utsu)
~no ka | のか | explanatory question

> Kougeki wo subete kawashite iru. | 攻撃を全てかわしている。 | He's dodging every attack.
kougeki | 攻撃 | attack
~wo | を | object marker
subete | 全て | all
kawashite iru | かわしている | is dodging (kawasu + te iru)

> Kimi no kekkijutsu wa sugokatta! | 君の血鬼術はすごかった！ | Your demon art was amazing!
kimi | 君 | you
~no | の | of; links nouns
kekkijutsu | 血鬼術 | demonic supernatural art
~wa | は | topic marker
sugokatta | すごかった | was amazing (past of sugoi)

> Sasattara hontou ni jidou de chi wo totte kureru n da na. | 刺さったら本当に自動で血をとってくれるんだな。 | It really does draw blood automatically once it sticks in.
sasattara | 刺さったら | once it sticks in (conditional of sasaru)
hontou ni | 本当に | really
jidou de | 自動で | automatically
chi | 血 | blood
~wo | を | object marker
totte kureru | とってくれる | takes for me (toru + te kureru)
~n da na | んだな | confirming an understanding

> Nande mono wo nagetsukeru n da! | なんで物を投げつけるんだ！ | Why are you throwing things at him?!
nande | なんで | why (informal)
mono | 物 | things, objects
~wo | を | object marker
nagetsukeru | 投げつける | to hurl at
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Zen'itsu to Shouichi no nioi ga suru. | 善逸と正一のにおいがする。 | I can smell Zenitsu and Shoichi.
~Zen'itsu | 善逸 | Zenitsu (name)
~to | と | with; quotation marker
~Shouichi | 正一 | Shoichi (name)
~no | の | of; links nouns
nioi | におい | smell, scent
~ga | が | subject marker
suru | する | to do

> Sonna koto wa saisho kara wakatteru! | そんなことは最初から分かってる！ | I knew that from the very start!
sonna | そんな | that kind of
koto | こと | situation, thing
~wa | は | topic marker
saisho | 最初 | beginning
~kara | から | because; from
wakatteru | 分かってる | know (contracted wakatte iru, from wakaru)

> Nakitaku naru you na yasashii oto ga suru. | 泣きたくなるような優しい音がする。 | His gentle sound makes me want to cry.
nakitaku naru | 泣きたくなる | to feel like crying (naku + taku naru)
~you na | ような | like, such that
yasashii | 優しい | gentle, kind
oto | 音 | sound
~ga | が | subject marker
suru | する | to do

> Demo ore wa hito ni yoku damasareta. | でも俺は人によくだまされた。 | But I was often deceived by people.
~demo | でも | even; but
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker
hito | 人 | person, people
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
yoku | よく | well
damasareta | だまされた | was deceived (past passive of damasu)
