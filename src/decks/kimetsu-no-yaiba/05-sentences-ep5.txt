# Sentences: Episode 5 | Short lines from My Own Steel
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Sore wo tsukete iru sei de minna kuwareta. | それをつけているせいでみんな食われた。 | Wearing that got all of them eaten.
sore | それ | that
~wo | を | object marker
tsukete iru | つけている | are wearing (tsukeru + te iru)
sei | せい | fault, cause of something bad
~de | で | at, with; means marker
minna | みんな | everyone
kuwareta | 食われた | were eaten (past passive of kuu)

> Niichan wo kamikoroshichatta n da. | 兄ちゃんをかみ殺しちゃったんだ。 | I bit my big brother to death.
niichan | 兄ちゃん | big brother (familiar)
~wo | を | object marker
kamikoroshichatta | かみ殺しちゃった | ended up biting to death (kamikorosu + contracted te shimatta)
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Katta yo mou anshin shite ii yo. | 勝ったよもう安心していいよ。 | We won. You can relax now.
katta | 勝った | won (past of katsu)
~yo | よ | emphasis
mou | もう | already; any more
anshin shite | 安心して | relax (te-form of anshin suru)
ii | いい | good
~yo | よ | emphasis

> Oni ni natta hito wo moto ni modosu ni wa dou shitara ii n da? | 鬼になった人を元に戻すにはどうしたらいいんだ？ | How do I turn someone who became a demon back to normal?
oni | 鬼 | demon
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
natta | なった | became (past of naru)
hito | 人 | person, people
~wo | を | object marker
moto | 元 | original state
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
modosu | 戻す | to return something to its former state
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~wa | は | topic marker
dou shitara | どうしたら | what should I do (conditional of dou suru)
ii | いい | good
~n da | んだ | explanatory ending

> Sono ato wa kaikyuu wo kizamasete itadakimasu. | そのあとは階級を刻ませていただきます。 | After that, we will mark your rank.
sono | その | that (before a noun)
ato | あと | afterward
~wa | は | topic marker
kaikyuu | 階級 | rank
~wo | を | object marker
kizamasete itadakimasu | 刻ませていただきます | will mark with your permission (kizamu, causative + humble itadaku)

> Ohanashi wa sumimashita ka? | お話は済みましたか？ | Has everyone finished talking?
ohanashi | お話 | conversation (polite hanashi)
~wa | は | topic marker
sumimashita | 済みました | finished (polite past of sumu)
~ka | か | question marker

> Tabun sugu shinimasu yo ore wa. | 多分すぐ死にますよ俺は。 | I'll probably die soon.
tabun | 多分 | probably
sugu | すぐ | soon, immediately
shinimasu | 死にます | will die (polite shinu)
~yo | よ | emphasis
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~wa | は | topic marker

> Tamahagane nante hajimete miru shi… | 玉鋼なんて初めて見るし… | This is my first time seeing sword steel.
tamahagane | 玉鋼 | traditional sword steel
~nante | なんて | something like
hajimete | 初めて | for the first time
miru | 見る | to see
~shi | し | and; gives a reason

> Dono oni mo matomo ni kaiwa dekiru joutai ja nakatta. | どの鬼もまともに会話できる状態じゃなかった。 | None of the demons were in any state to talk properly.
dono | どの | which, any
oni | 鬼 | demon
~mo | も | also, even
matomo ni | まともに | properly, coherently
kaiwa dekiru | 会話できる | can converse (potential of kaiwa suru)
joutai | 状態 | state, condition
~ja nakatta | じゃなかった | was not (past negative ending)

> Omae nande kyuu ni neru n da yo? | お前なんで急に寝るんだよ？ | Why are you suddenly falling asleep?
omae | お前 | you (rough)
nande | なんで | why (informal)
kyuu ni | 急に | suddenly
neru | 寝る | to sleep
~n da yo | んだよ | emphatic explanation

> Oni ni wa ikutsuka shurui ga aru. | 鬼にはいくつか種類がある。 | There are several kinds of demons.
oni | 鬼 | demon
~ni | に | to, at; target marker
~wa | は | topic marker
ikutsuka | いくつか | several
shurui | 種類 | kind, type
~ga | が | subject marker
aru | ある | to exist, be present

> Ore ga Nezuko wo mamoru kara. | 俺が禰豆子を守るから。 | I'll protect Nezuko.
ore | 俺 | I, me (rough, masculine)
~ga | が | subject marker
~Nezuko | 禰豆子 | Nezuko (name)
~wo | を | object marker
mamoru | 守る | to protect
~kara | から | because; from

> Sou iu imi ja nee. | そういう意味じゃねえ。 | That's not what I mean.
sou iu | そういう | that kind of
imi | 意味 | meaning
~ja nee | じゃねえ | is not (rough ja nai)

> Koryaa katana mo akaku naru kamo shiren zo. | こりゃあ刀も赤くなるかもしれんぞ。 | This sword might turn red too.
koryaa | こりゃあ | this (contracted kore wa)
katana | 刀 | sword
~mo | も | also, even
akaku | 赤く | red (adverbial form of akai)
naru | なる | to become
~kamo shiren | かもしれん | might (contracted kamo shirenai)
~zo | ぞ | forceful emphasis

> Mochinushi ni yotte iro ga kawaru no yo. | 持ち主によって色が変わるのよ。 | Its color changes depending on its owner.
mochinushi | 持ち主 | owner
~ni yotte | によって | depending on
iro | 色 | color
~ga | が | subject marker
kawaru | 変わる | to change
~no yo | のよ | explanatory emphasis
