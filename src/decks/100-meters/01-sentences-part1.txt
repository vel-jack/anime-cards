# Dialogue: Part 1 | Childhood — two boys and the 100 metres
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Hashiru no suki na no? | 走るの好きなの？ | Do you like running?
hashiru | 走る | to run
~no | の | (turns it into "…-ing")
suki | 好き | like, fond of
~na no | なの | (soft question)

> Ja, nande hashitteru no? | じゃあ、なんで走ってるの？ | Then why do you run?
ja | じゃあ | then, in that case
nande | なんで | why
hashitteru | 走ってる | running (short for "hashitte iru")
~no | の | (soft question)

> Genjitsu yori tsurai koto wo suru to ki ga magirerun da. | 現実よりつらいことをすると気が紛れるんだ。 | When I do something harder than real life, it takes my mind off things.
genjitsu | 現実 | reality, real life
yori | より | than
tsurai | つらい | hard, painful
koto | こと | thing
~wo | を | (marks the object)
suru | する | to do
~to | と | when, if
ki ga magireru | 気が紛れる | to be distracted from one's worries
~n da | んだ | (explaining)

> Hito to umaku hanasenai shi. | 人とうまく話せないし。 | I can't talk to people well, either.
hito | 人 | people
~to | と | with
umaku | うまく | well, skilfully
hanasenai | 話せない | can't talk
~shi | し | and besides

> Kono sekai ni wa sugoku kantan na ruuru ga arunda. | この世界にはすごく簡単なルールがあるんだ。 | There's a really simple rule in this world.
kono | この | this
sekai | 世界 | world
~ni wa | には | in (as the topic)
sugoku | すごく | very, really
kantan na | 簡単な | simple
ruuru | ルール | rule
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
aru | ある | there is
~n da | んだ | (explaining)

> Hyaku meetoru wo dare yori mo hayaku hashireba zenbu kaiketsu suru. | 100メートルを誰よりも速く走れば全部解決する。 | Run 100 metres faster than anyone, and everything gets solved.
hyaku meetoru | 100メートル | 100 metres
~wo | を | (marks the distance)
dare yori mo | 誰よりも | than anyone
hayaku | 速く | fast
hashireba | 走れば | if you run
zenbu | 全部 | everything
kaiketsu suru | 解決する | to be solved

> Sore wo kimeru no wa kimi da yo. | それを決めるのは君だよ。 | That's for you to decide.
sore | それ | that
~wo | を | (marks the object)
kimeru | 決める | to decide
~no wa | のは | "the one who…"
kimi | 君 | you
~da yo | だよ | it is (informing)

> Yasumu no mo renshuu no uchi da yo. | 休むのも練習のうちだよ。 | Resting is part of training too.
yasumu | 休む | to rest
~no mo | のも | (…-ing) too
renshuu | 練習 | practice, training
~no | の | of
uchi | うち | part of
~da yo | だよ | it is (informing)

> Ichido de ii kara ichii ni natte mitai. | 一度でいいから一位になってみたい。 | Just once, I want to try coming first.
ichido | 一度 | once
~de ii kara | でいいから | (…is enough, so)
ichii | 一位 | first place
~ni | に | (marks what you become)
natte mitai | なってみたい | want to try becoming

> Nan no tame ni hashitte irun desu ka? | 何のために走っているんですか？ | What do you run for?
nan no tame ni | 何のために | for what, what for
hashitte iru | 走っている | are running
~n desu ka | んですか | (polite question)

> Ichii wo toru tame da yo. | 1位を取るためだよ。 | To take first place.
ichii | 一位 | first place
~wo | を | (marks the object)
toru | 取る | to take
tame | ため | for (the sake of)
~da yo | だよ | it is (informing)

> Hibi no reesu ga jinsei wo sayuu suru. | 日々のレースが人生を左右する。 | The races you run each day decide your life.
hibi | 日々 | daily, day after day
~no | の | of
reesu | レース | race
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
jinsei | 人生 | (one's) life
~wo | を | (marks the object)
sayuu suru | 左右する | to sway, decide

> Jibun ga omotte iru gojuubai no sainou ga aru zo. | 自分が思っている50倍の才能があるぞ。 | You have fifty times the talent you think you do.
jibun | 自分 | oneself, you yourself
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
omotte iru | 思っている | think
gojuubai | 50倍 | fifty times (as much)
~no | の | of
sainou | 才能 | talent
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
aru | ある | there is, have
~zo | ぞ | (emphatic)

> Boku wa te wo nuku koto ga dekinain da. | 僕は手を抜くことができないんだ。 | I just can't hold back.
boku | 僕 | I (soft, boyish)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
te wo nuku | 手を抜く | to slack off, hold back
koto ga dekinai | ことができない | can't (do)
~n da | んだ | (explaining)

> Boku to hyaku meetoru de shoubu shite hoshii. | 僕と100メートルで勝負してほしい。 | I want you to race me over 100 metres.
boku | 僕 | I, me (soft, boyish)
~to | と | with
hyaku meetoru | 100メートル | 100 metres
~de | で | over, in
shoubu shite | 勝負して | compete (te-form of "shoubu suru")
hoshii | ほしい | want (you) to
