# Dialogue: Part 2 | High school — the track club and the relay
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Uwasa ni wa kiiteta kedo hontou ni nyuugaku shiteta n da ne. | 噂には聞いてたけど本当に入学してたんだね。 | I'd heard rumours, but you really did enrol here.
uwasa | 噂 | rumour
~ni wa | には | (as for…)
kiiteta | 聞いてた | had heard (short for "kiite ita")
kedo | けど | but
hontou ni | 本当に | really
nyuugaku shiteta | 入学してた | had enrolled
~n da ne | んだね | so it's true

> Ie kara chikakatta node. | 家から近かったので。 | Because it was close to home.
ie | 家 | home
~kara | から | from
chikakatta | 近かった | was close (past of "chikai")
node | ので | because

> Doushite rikujoubu ni hairanai no? | どうして陸上部に入らないの？ | Why won't you join the track team?
doushite | どうして | why
rikujoubu | 陸上部 | track and field club
~ni | に | into
hairanai | 入らない | don't join
~no | の | (soft question)

> Nan no tame ni? Hayaku naru tame. | 何のために？速くなるため。 | What for? To get faster.
nan no tame ni | 何のために | what for
hayaku naru | 速くなる | to get faster
tame | ため | for (the sake of)

> Ippon hashittara mou kan'yuu shinaide kudasai yo. | 一本走ったらもう勧誘しないでくださいよ。 | If I run once, please stop trying to recruit me.
ippon | 一本 | one (run)
hashittara | 走ったら | if/after (I) run
mou | もう | (not) anymore
kan'yuu | 勧誘 | recruiting
shinaide kudasai | しないでください | please don't
~yo | よ | (asserting)

> Saitei gonin inai to bukatsudou to shite mitomete moraenai no. | 最低5人いないと部活動として認めてもらえないの。 | Without at least five members, it won't be recognised as a club.
saitei | 最低 | at least, minimum
gonin | 五人 | five people
inai to | いないと | if there aren't
bukatsudou | 部活動 | school club activity
to shite | として | as
mitomete moraenai | 認めてもらえない | won't be recognised
~no | の | (explaining)

> Sore tada no uwasa desu. | それただの噂です。 | That's just a rumour.
sore | それ | that
tada no | ただの | just a, mere
uwasa | 噂 | rumour
~desu | です | is (polite)

> Ore, rikujou yameyou to omottemashita. | 俺、陸上辞めようと思ってました。 | I'd been thinking of quitting track.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
rikujou | 陸上 | track and field
yameyou | 辞めよう | (I'll) quit ("yameru", volitional)
to omottemashita | と思ってました | had been thinking (polite)

> Hashiru koto mo yameru koto mo kowaku natte. | 走ることも辞めることも怖くなって。 | I got scared of both running and quitting.
hashiru koto | 走ること | running
~mo | も | both… and
yameru koto | 辞めること | quitting
~mo | も | both… and
kowaku natte | 怖くなって | became scared

> Orei wo iu no wa ore no hou desu. | お礼を言うのは俺の方です。 | I'm the one who should be thanking you.
orei | お礼 | thanks
~wo | を | (marks the object)
iu | 言う | to say
~no wa | のは | "the one who…"
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
no hou | の方 | (my) side
~desu | です | is (polite)

> Mou ichido hashitte minai? | もう一度走ってみない？ | Won't you try running once more?
mou ichido | もう一度 | once more
hashitte minai | 走ってみない | won't (you) try running?

> Kono yo ni wa hyaku meetoru de jinsei wo kuruwasareru ningen mo irunda yo. | この世には100メートルで人生を狂わされる人間もいるんだよ。 | In this world, some people have their lives wrecked by 100 metres.
kono yo | この世 | this world
~ni wa | には | in (as the topic)
hyaku meetoru | 100メートル | 100 metres
~de | で | by
jinsei | 人生 | (one's) life
~wo | を | (marks the object)
kuruwasareru | 狂わされる | to be thrown off, ruined (passive of "kuruwasu")
ningen | 人間 | people
~mo | も | also
iru | いる | there are
~n da yo | んだよ | (explaining)

> Aite ni totte fusoku wa nai. | 相手にとって不足はない。 | A worthy opponent.
aite | 相手 | opponent
ni totte | にとって | as, for
fusoku | 不足 | shortage, lacking
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
nai | ない | there is none

> Mucha shinai yo. | 無茶しないよ。 | I won't overdo it.
mucha | 無茶 | reckless, excessive
shinai | しない | won't do
~yo | よ | (reassuring)

> Fuan wa taiji subeki de wa nai. | 不安は退治すべきではない。 | Anxiety is not something to be beaten.
fuan | 不安 | anxiety
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
taiji | 退治 | getting rid of, defeating
subeki de wa nai | すべきではない | should not

> Fuan to wa, kimi jishin ga kimi wo tamesu toki no kanjou da. | 不安とは、君自身が君を試す時の感情だ。 | Anxiety is the feeling you get when you put yourself to the test.
fuan | 不安 | anxiety
~to wa | とは | (defining: "is")
kimi jishin | 君自身 | you yourself
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
kimi | 君 | you
~wo | を | (marks the object)
tamesu | 試す | to test
toki | 時 | when
~no | の | of
kanjou | 感情 | emotion
~da | だ | is
