# Dialogue: Part 3 | Pro runners — reality, injury and the final
# format:
#   > romaji sentence | japanese | english
#   word | japanese | meaning          (one line per word, in sentence order)
#   ~particle | japanese | meaning     (~ = shown in the breakdown only, no card of its own)

> Itsuka rikujou ni korosareru zo. | いつか陸上に殺されるぞ。 | Track will kill you someday.
itsuka | いつか | someday
rikujou | 陸上 | track and field
~ni | に | by
korosareru | 殺される | to be killed (passive of "korosu")
~zo | ぞ | (warning)

> Kotoshi ga saigo da to omotte ganbaru yo. | 今年が最後だと思って頑張るよ。 | I'll give it everything, as if this year is my last.
kotoshi | 今年 | this year
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
saigo | 最後 | the last
da to omotte | だと思って | thinking that it is
ganbaru | 頑張る | to do one's best
~yo | よ | (asserting)

> Mainen jiko besuto wo koushin suru tsumori de shiizun ni idonde imasu. | 毎年自己ベストを更新するつもりでシーズンに挑んでいます。 | Every year I go into the season aiming to beat my personal best.
mainen | 毎年 | every year
jiko besuto | 自己ベスト | personal best
~wo | を | (marks the object)
koushin suru | 更新する | to break, update (a record)
tsumori de | つもりで | intending to
shiizun | シーズン | season
~ni | に | (marks what you take on)
idonde imasu | 挑んでいます | take on, challenge (polite)

> Genjitsu wa katte ni kiete kurenai. | 現実は勝手に消えてくれない。 | Reality won't just disappear on its own.
genjitsu | 現実 | reality
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
katte ni | 勝手に | on its own, by itself
kiete kurenai | 消えてくれない | won't disappear (for you)

> Sonna no wa hitotsu da. Kiroku de chouetsu suru shika nai. | そんなのは一つだ。記録で超越するしかない。 | There's only one way. You have to rise above it with your times.
sonna no | そんなの | that (sort of thing)
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
hitotsu | 一つ | one
~da | だ | is
kiroku | 記録 | record, time
~de | で | by
chouetsu suru | 超越する | to transcend, rise above
shika nai | しかない | have no choice but

> Zuibun yowaki ja nee ka. | ずいぶん弱気じゃねえか。 | You sound awfully timid.
zuibun | ずいぶん | awfully, quite
yowaki | 弱気 | timid, faint-hearted
ja nee ka | じゃねえか | aren't you (rough)

> Ore ni wa tensei no sainou ga aru. | 俺には天性の才能がある。 | I have natural talent.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~ni wa | には | (as for me)
tensei | 天性 | inborn, natural
~no | の | (links to the noun)
sainou | 才能 | talent
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
aru | ある | there is, have

> Genjitsu touhi wa ore jishin e no kitai da. | 現実逃避は俺自身への期待だ。 | Escaping reality is my faith in myself.
genjitsu touhi | 現実逃避 | escaping reality
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
ore jishin | 俺自身 | myself
e no | への | toward
kitai | 期待 | expectation, hope
~da | だ | is

> Ore ga ore wo akiramete inai. | 俺が俺を諦めていない。 | I haven't given up on myself.
ore | 俺 | I (rough, masculine)
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
~ore | 俺 | me
~wo | を | (marks the object)
akiramete inai | 諦めていない | haven't given up

> Kako no koto wa wasurete, ima jibun ga dekiru koto wo seiippai yatte ikou to omotte imasu. | 過去のことは忘れて、今自分ができることを精一杯やっていこうと思っています。 | I'll put the past behind me and do everything I can right now.
kako | 過去 | the past
~no koto wa | のことは | (things about…)
wasurete | 忘れて | forget, and…
ima | 今 | now
jibun ga | 自分が | I myself
dekiru koto | できること | what (I) can do
~wo | を | (marks the object)
seiippai | 精一杯 | as hard as one can
yatte ikou | やっていこう | keep on doing
to omotte imasu | と思っています | I intend to (polite)

> Akushu shite moratte mo ii desu ka? | 握手してもらってもいいですか？ | Could I shake your hand?
akushu | 握手 | handshake
shite moratte mo ii | してもらってもいい | may I have you do
~desu ka | ですか | (polite question)

> Me wo tojitara doko ni mo nigerarenai. | 目を閉じたらどこにも逃げられない。 | Close your eyes, and you can't run anywhere.
me | 目 | eyes
~wo | を | (marks the object)
tojitara | 閉じたら | if you close
doko ni mo | どこにも | (not) anywhere
nigerarenai | 逃げられない | can't escape

> Nikubanare wa amaku mite wa ikemasen. | 肉離れは甘く見てはいけません。 | You mustn't take a pulled muscle lightly.
nikubanare | 肉離れ | pulled muscle
~wa | は | (marks the topic)
amaku mite | 甘く見て | taking lightly
wa ikemasen | はいけません | mustn't (polite)

> Hayasa dake ga subete ja nai. | 速さだけが全てじゃない。 | Speed isn't everything.
hayasa | 速さ | speed
dake | だけ | only
~ga | が | (marks the subject)
subete | 全て | everything
ja nai | じゃない | is not

> Makettatte inochi wo torareru wake ja nai shi. | 負けたって命を取られるわけじゃないし。 | Losing won't cost you your life.
makettatte | 負けたって | even if you lose
inochi | 命 | life
~wo | を | (marks the object)
torareru | 取られる | to be taken (passive of "toru")
wake ja nai | わけじゃない | it's not as if
~shi | し | and besides

> Honki de hashireba subete fukitobu. | 本気で走れば全て吹き飛ぶ。 | Run for real, and everything else is blown away.
honki de | 本気で | seriously, for real
hashireba | 走れば | if you run
subete | 全て | everything
fukitobu | 吹き飛ぶ | to be blown away
